Which side of Busan fits your trip?

Busan is a large city. Pick a neighborhood first, then build your day around nearby stops instead of crossing town repeatedly.

Seomyeon Central · food · connections

A practical base for restaurant-hopping, shopping and connecting to other parts of the city. Start here if your itinerary mixes several neighborhoods.
