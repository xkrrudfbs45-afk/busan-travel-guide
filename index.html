BUSAN NOW · WINTER 2026

Busan’s winter nights are lighting up

Three seasonal reasons to stay out after sunset: Millak Luce Festa starts November 14, Busan Christmas Village opens November 26, and Haeundae Light Festival begins November 28. Prefer an indoor escape? Add immersive media art on Yeongdo to your plan.

Another reason to go out: the free Gwangalli M Drone Light Show runs on Saturdays in winter at 19:00 and 21:00. Weather and organizer notices may change the show.

  • Nov 14–Jan 10Millak Luce Festa · free waterfront light walk
  • Nov 26–Dec 25Christmas Village · ticketed seasonal experience
  • Nov 28–Jan 10Haeundae Light Festival · free beach and street displays

CHOOSE YOUR BASE · PLAN BY AREA

Which side of Busan fits your trip?

Busan is a large city. Pick a neighborhood first, then build your day around nearby stops instead of crossing town repeatedly.

Seomyeon Central · food · connections

A practical base for restaurant-hopping, shopping and connecting to other parts of the city. Start here if your itinerary mixes several neighborhoods.

Haeundae Beach · coastal attractions

Choose Haeundae for beach time and east-coast attractions. Popular rides and sights can need advance planning, especially on weekends and holidays.

Nampo-dong Markets · old downtown · food

A useful area for Jagalchi Market, shopping streets and historic central Busan. Check seafood prices and preparation charges before ordering.
