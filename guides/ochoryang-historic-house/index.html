OCHORYANG: A Historic House and Garden in Busan

Looking beyond Busan’s beaches and busy shopping streets? OCHORYANG is a historic house and garden in Choryang, a neighborhood close to Busan Station. Its appeal is quieter: architecture, garden details and cultural programming rather than a conventional café stop.

Plan ahead: Do not assume you can walk in. Visit formats, reservation requirements, available programmes and operating hours can change. Check the official Visit Busan listing before setting out.

Why include OCHORYANG in a local itinerary?

OCHORYANG offers a different pace from Busan’s headline attractions. It can suit travelers interested in historic domestic architecture, small gardens and cultural spaces. The house also sits within Choryang, an area worth exploring slowly rather than treating only as a transfer point near Busan Station.

History and context

The building reflects a layered period of Busan’s history. When visiting, take time to understand that historic architecture from the Japanese colonial period carries a complicated context; it is more than a photogenic backdrop. Follow the site’s interpretation and respect any restrictions on photography or access.

How to get there

  • Area: Choryang, Dong-gu, Busan.
  • Address listed by Visit Busan: 22, Gogwan-ro 13beonna-gil, Dong-gu, Busan (부산광역시 동구 고관로13번나길 22, 1층).
  • Subway: Visit Busan lists Choryang Station on Busan Metro Line 1, exits 7 or 9, about a five-minute walk. Check the station exit and walking route on the day.

Opening hours and reservations

The official Visit Busan listing currently gives Tuesday as a closed day and lists visit windows on Wednesday–Monday, 10:30–12:00 and 14:00–15:30. It describes a short docent-style visit and a separate longer exhibition experience. These details are subject to change and should not be treated as guaranteed availability or walk-in admission.

Before visiting, confirm the current programme, language, duration, reservation method, fees (if any), photography rules and any temporary closures directly through the official Visit Busan OCHORYANG listing.

Make it part of a Choryang day

Keep the schedule flexible: pair the visit with a walk around Choryang or the Busan Station area, but avoid booking a tight connection immediately afterward. If the visit requires a reservation, build the rest of the day around the confirmed time.

Visitor etiquette

  • Confirm permission before photographing interiors, artworks or people.
  • Keep voices low and follow staff instructions in the house and garden.
  • Do not enter closed areas or assume every cultural programme is included in a general visit.
  • Recheck current access information before traveling; third-party listings may be out of date.
