Homigot Sunrise Festival 2027: Dates, Location & Planning

The 29th Homigot Sunrise Festival is scheduled for December 31, 2026–January 1, 2027, at Homigot Sunrise Square in Pohang. Here is what is confirmed, what still needs checking and how to plan the trip without relying on unverified transport assumptions.

DEC 31, 2026 – JAN 1, 2027POHANG, KOREACHECK LIVE NOTICES

Official date confirmed: Pohang Cultural Foundation lists the 29th Homigot Sunrise Festival for December 31, 2026–January 1, 2027, at Homigot Sunrise Square. Detailed program times, traffic controls and special transport arrangements should be checked in the organizer’s latest notices before departure.

What is the Homigot Sunrise Festival?

Homigot is a coastal destination in Pohang, North Gyeongsang Province, known for its New Year sunrise gathering. The annual festival spans the afternoon and evening of December 31 and the first sunrise of the new year. The organizer’s overview describes it as a long-running event that began in 1999.

2026–27 dates and location

  • Festival: 29th Homigot Sunrise Festival
  • Dates: December 31, 2026–January 1, 2027
  • Venue: Homigot Sunrise Square
  • Address: 136 Haemaji-ro, Homigot-myeon, Nam-gu, Pohang-si, Gyeongsangbuk-do, South Korea

The date and venue are listed by Pohang Cultural Foundation. A published event date does not guarantee that every planned performance, road or visitor service will operate unchanged; check the official program and traffic notices close to the event.

Planning the journey from Busan

Homigot is outside Busan, so treat this as a separate regional trip rather than a quick city attraction. Plan the full journey to and from Pohang, including the final local transfer to Homigot. Do not assume a train or intercity bus will take you directly to the festival square.

For public transport, compare current services to Pohang and the local onward connection using official rail and bus booking services. For bus routes, the Korea Tourism Organization explains the difference between express and intercity services and links to the booking platforms. Check the final departure and return options before committing to an overnight or same-night plan.

For a New Year event, allow a generous buffer for congestion, possible temporary traffic controls, cold weather and crowded boarding areas. If you plan to stay overnight, confirm accommodation and cancellation conditions before purchasing transport.

What to check before you go

  • Official program and performance timetable for December 31 and January 1.
  • Traffic restrictions, parking arrangements and any shuttle or special bus service.
  • Rail and intercity-bus schedules for both directions, including the final local connection to Homigot.
  • Weather, wind chill and sunrise visibility; sunrise viewing is weather-dependent.
  • Whether food stalls, rest areas and public facilities operate throughout the overnight period.

Can you combine it with a Busan trip?

Yes, but avoid squeezing the festival into an already full Busan day. A more realistic plan is to spend time in Busan before or after the Pohang visit, with a separate transfer window.
