Yeongdo Half-Day Route: Village Lanes, Sea Walk and Taejongdae

See a different side of Busan on Yeongdo Island. This route combines the cliffside lanes of Huinnyeoul Culture Village, a section of the Jeoryeong Coastal Trail and the forest-and-cliff scenery of Taejongdae.

YEONGDO ISLANDABOUT 4–5 HOURSWALKING + LOCAL BUS/TAXI

Route tip: Visit Huinnyeoul and the coastal trail together, then use a local bus or taxi to reach Taejongdae. Do not assume the whole route is a short, flat walk: Yeongdo has steep slopes, stairs and exposed coastal paths.

The half-day route

  1. Huinnyeoul Culture Village (60–90 minutes). Walk the narrow lanes, lookouts and small galleries. The village is also a residential neighbourhood, so keep voices low, do not enter private spaces and avoid photographing residents at close range.
  2. Jeoryeong Coastal Trail (45–75 minutes). Use one of the stairways down from the village and enjoy a manageable section of the seaside path. The full trail is longer; turn back when the steps, weather or your pace make it sensible.
  3. Transfer to Taejongdae (allow 20–35 minutes, depending on traffic). Take a local bus or taxi rather than treating the coastal trail as a direct, easy connection to the park entrance. Check the current bus route and direction before boarding.
  4. Taejongdae Park (90–120 minutes). Follow the park loop on foot or check whether the Danubi Train is operating. The train normally stops at key viewpoints including the observatory, Yeongdo Lighthouse and Taejongsa Temple, but service can stop in rain or poor weather and it has a regular weekly closure.

Getting there from central Busan

From Nampo Station on Metro Line 1, transfer to a bus serving Huinnyeoul Culture Village. Visit Busan lists buses 7, 71 and 508 for the village area. For Taejongdae, Visit Busan lists several services from the Nampo area, including routes 8, 17, 30, 88 and 186. Routes and stops can change, so confirm the live direction and stop in a local bus app before setting out.

What to wear and plan for

  • Wear shoes with good grip. The route includes slopes, stairs and uneven coastal surfaces.
  • Carry water and allow extra time for photo stops and stairways.
  • In strong wind, heavy rain or rough coastal conditions, skip the exposed trail and spend more time in the village or park.
  • Do not block narrow residential lanes for photos; Huinnyeoul is a living neighbourhood, not a closed film set.
  • If you need a step-free route, plan the village and trail separately; this is not an accessible, flat itinerary.

Danubi Train: check before you go

The Danubi Train is a convenient way to move around Taejongdae, but published operating times and fares can change by season. Visit Busan notes a regular Monday closure and possible suspension during rain or severe weather. Check the official Taejongdae page on the day, especially in winter or on a wet day. If it is not running, the park can still be explored on foot, but allow more time.
