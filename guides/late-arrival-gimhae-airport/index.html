Late Arrival in Busan: How to Get from Gimhae Airport to Your Hotel

Compare the light rail, buses, taxis and pre-booked transfers when your flight arrives late in the day.

BUSAN, SOUTH KOREA6 MIN READCHECK DETAILS BEFORE TRAVEL

Quick summary

  • Check the last light rail, metro and bus services for your actual landing date and time.
  • The Seomyeon/Bujeon limousine's published last airport departure is 21:30; check the current light-rail timetable and the onward Sasang connection separately.
  • Compare the full public-transport route with a taxi or pre-booked transfer, especially with luggage.
  • Confirm your hotel's late check-in policy before your flight.

Quick advice: If you land late, check the final departures for every public transport leg before you board. If your arrival time leaves little margin, compare the cost of a taxi or a pre-booked transfer against the risk of missing the last connection.

Start with your arrival time and destination

Gimhae International Airport serves Busan and the neighboring area. The best route depends on where you are staying, when you clear immigration and baggage claim, and how much luggage you have. Do not plan around the scheduled landing time alone: allow for delays and the time needed to reach the station or taxi rank.

For a route focused specifically on the east coast, see our Gimhae Airport to Haeundae route guide.

Compare the main options

Light rail and metro

The Busan-Gimhae Light Rail connects the airport area with the wider rail network. Depending on your destination, you may need to transfer to Busan Metro. This can be a good-value option earlier in the day, but it is less forgiving if you arrive near the end of service or have bulky luggage.

Airport limousine or other bus services

Bus routes and operating patterns can change. Confirm that a service is running on your date, where it stops, and whether the stop is convenient for your accommodation. Never assume an older blog post has current departure times.

Taxi

A taxi can be simpler when traveling with several bags, children or a group. Before leaving, confirm the destination address in Korean or show it on a map. Use the official taxi queue and check the fare method shown by the service. Extra charges may apply under local rules.

Pre-booked transfer

A pre-booked vehicle can reduce uncertainty for very late arrivals, especially when several people share the cost. Check the meeting point, waiting-time policy, flight-delay process, luggage allowance and cancellation terms before paying.

Airport Limousine 1 has separate Jangsan and Gijang branch timetables; the later Gijang departure does not serve every Haeundae stop. Both limousine routes are cashless: book a QR ticket through Bustago or pay on site by transit or credit card if seats remain. There is no free transfer, and each passenger may bring up to two bags with a combined weight below 30 kg. The airport lists Platform 3 on the first floor of both terminals for Airport Limousine 1 and 2.

Light rail: the last train is only the first leg

The operator's publicly accessible airport-station timetable pages have different update dates and disagree on the last departure by one minute: the station-specific page (last updated 2016) shows 00:09, while the network summary (last updated 2020) shows 00:10 toward Sasang. Because neither page provides a sufficiently current, consistent confirmation, this guide does not publish a fixed light-rail last-train time. Check the current station timetable on your travel date and confirm the onward Busan Metro connection at Sasang separately.

Taxi: use the official rank and treat estimates as estimates

At the international terminal, the official taxi stand is across the road from Gate 3; at the domestic terminal, it is across the road from Gate 4. Use the marked rank. The airport's published standard-taxi estimates are Seomyeon: ₩18,000 (about 45 minutes); Nampo/Jagalchi: ₩20,000 (about 60 minutes); Gwangalli: ₩25,000 (about 70 minutes); and Haeundae: ₩25,000 (about 70 minutes). These are planning estimates, not fixed quotes. Traffic, route, tolls and night surcharges can change the meter.

The airport's published standard-taxi meter starts at ₩4,800 for 2 km. Its listed night surcharge is 20% from 23:00 to midnight, 30% from midnight to 02:00, and 20% from 02:00 to 04:00. Deluxe and jumbo taxis use different rates. If you plan to use a ride-hailing app, confirm before travel that your account can sign in and your payment method works; do not assume every overseas visitor can use the same app. Keep the official taxi rank as a backup, and confirm the payment method before the ride.

A late-arrival checklist

  • Save your accommodation address in Korean and offline on your phone.
  • Check the last train or bus from the airport and any onward transfer.
  • Keep a working data connection or know how to access airport Wi-Fi.
  • Have a backup plan if immigration, baggage or your flight runs late.
  • Tell your accommodation if you expect to arrive after its normal check-in time.
