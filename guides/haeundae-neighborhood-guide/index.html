Haeundae Neighborhood Guide: Beach, Food, Stays and Getting There

Haeundae is Busan’s best-known beach district. Use this page to plan the area in one place, then open the detailed guide for each decision.

Quick summary

  • Best for beach time, coastal walks, cafés and a relaxed seaside stay.
  • Haeundae Station is on Metro Line 2; from Busan Station, take Line 1 and change to Line 2 at Seomyeon.
  • The trade-off is distance from western and central Busan, so group days by area.

Is Haeundae the right base for you?

Choose Haeundae if the beach, coastal walks and evenings by the sea are a big part of your trip. It suits travelers who want an easy, resort-like base in the east of the city.

If most of your plans are in Nampo-dong, Jagalchi or the old downtown, compare Seomyeon as a more central base. Our where-to-stay guide compares both.

Getting there and around

  • From Gimhae Airport: compare the light rail and Metro Line 2, a taxi and the airport limousine bus in our airport-to-Haeundae guide. It lists the details on the airport’s official page, checked November 5, 2026.
  • From Busan Station (KTX): take Metro Line 1 to Seomyeon and change to Line 2 toward Jangsan. Arriving late? Read the late-arrival guide first.
  • Getting around: a transit card covers the metro and buses. See the transit card guide.

Where to stay

Pick accommodation by its walking distance to the station or the beach, not just the word “Haeundae” in the hotel name. The district is long, and a hotel near Jangsan feels different from one by the beach.

Compare Haeundae with Seomyeon and Gwangalli in Where to Stay in Busan. For quieter coastal stays further east, see Songjeong and Gijang.

  • Seafood by the sea, with tips on checking the price before you order.
  • Haeridan-gil for cafés and independent restaurants.

See and do nearby

  • Haeundae Blueline Park: popular rides can sell out, so check availability before you go.
  • Spa Land at Shinsegae Centum City: an indoor option for cold or rainy days.
  • Gwangalli, a few stops west on Line 2, and its Saturday drone show.
  • Night views and Haedong Yonggungsa Temple on the east coast.

Seasonal events

Haeundae-gu’s 2026 event calendar lists the 13th Haeundae Light Festival for November 28, 2026 to January 10, 2027, on Haeundae Beach and Gunam-ro. See our December section for sources and planning tips. Dates can change, so check the latest notice before you go.

A simple Haeundae half day

  1. Morning: walk the beach and the coastal path around Dongbaekseom.
  2. Late morning: ride Blueline Park if you booked a slot.
  3. Lunch: Haeundae Market.
  4. Evening: Haeridan-gil cafés, or Gwangalli on a show night.

Add travel time between stops and keep one indoor backup for wind or rain.

Common questions

Is Haeundae or Seomyeon better for a first trip?

Haeundae suits a beach-focused trip. Seomyeon is more central if your days are spread across the city. Many visitors stay in one and visit the other for a day.

How do I get from Busan Station to Haeundae?

Take Metro Line 1 to Seomyeon and change to Line 2 toward Jangsan, then get off at the station closest to your hotel.
