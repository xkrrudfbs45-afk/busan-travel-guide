Busan Taxi or Subway? Choose the Right Ride for Your Route

Taxis can help with luggage or awkward transfers, while the metro is often easier to predict in traffic. Choose by route and comfort, not blanket assumptions.

BUSAN · GETTING AROUND5 MIN READPRACTICAL LOCAL TIPS

Quick answer: For many cross-city trips, the subway is predictable and avoids road traffic. A taxi may be useful for luggage, late-night transfers or places far from a station. If winding roads make you motion-sick, compare the exact route rather than assuming every taxi ride will feel the same.

1. When the subway is a good choice

  • Travelling between major areas connected by metro.
  • Moving during rush hour when road traffic can be slow.
  • Keeping costs predictable for a solo traveller or small group.
  • Reducing the amount of time spent on winding hillside roads.

Check station exits and walking distance at both ends. A route with several transfers or many stairs may be less convenient with heavy luggage.

2. When a taxi can make sense

  • Travelling with bulky luggage or several people.
  • Reaching a destination far from a metro station.
  • Making a short transfer where the saved walking time is worth the fare.
  • Travelling at a time when public transport is limited.

Traffic and road conditions vary. Use the destination name and exact map pin, confirm the route in your map app, and check the fare estimate where available. Do not assume a taxi can drive directly to a pedestrian-only attraction entrance.

3. If you are prone to motion sickness

Busan has hills, coastal roads and winding approaches to some viewpoints. If those roads trigger symptoms for you, consider a subway route for the longest part of the journey and use a short taxi transfer only for the final stretch. On a road journey, looking ahead toward the direction of travel and avoiding reading while moving may help some people.

4. Luggage and station access

Check elevator access at the exact station exits you plan to use. If you arrive before hotel check-in, compare station lockers, staffed luggage storage and luggage delivery services. Confirm opening hours, cut-off times, delivery coverage and baggage rules directly before relying on a service.

5. Compare the whole trip

Do not compare only the vehicle time. Include walking to the stop, waiting, transfers, stairs, traffic, luggage and the return trip. For two or more travellers, compare the total taxi fare with the combined transit fares rather than comparing one taxi fare with one transit ticket.

Before you go

  • Check the full door-to-door route.
  • Confirm last-train or bus times if travelling late.
  • Save your destination in Korean as well as English when possible.
  • Consider hills, luggage and motion sensitivity.
