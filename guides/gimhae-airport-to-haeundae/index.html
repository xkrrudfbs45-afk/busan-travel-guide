Quick answer: For a budget-friendly journey, take the Busan-Gimhae Light Rail Transit to Sasang and transfer to Busan Metro Line 2 toward Haeundae/Jangsan. A taxi is simpler with heavy luggage, several travelers or a late arrival. Check live departure information before relying on a bus or rail connection.

Option 1: Light rail + Metro Line 2

Follow airport signs to the Busan-Gimhae Light Rail Transit station. Ride to Sasang, then transfer to Busan Metro Line 2 in the direction of Haeundae and Jangsan. Get off at the station that best matches your accommodation; Haeundae Station is not the closest stop for every hotel in the wider district.

This is usually the first option to compare if you are traveling on a budget and your arrival leaves enough time for the connection. Allow extra time for finding the station, buying or topping up a transport card, changing platforms and walking from the final station to your hotel.

For a broader explanation of cards and transfers, read our Busan public transportation guide.

Option 2: Taxi

A taxi can be more convenient if you have several bags, are traveling with children or a group, or would rather avoid a transfer after a long flight. The actual journey time and fare depend on traffic, the precise destination and any applicable surcharges. Use the official taxi queue, confirm the hotel address, and check the fare method before the trip begins.

If you are comparing a taxi with a pre-booked vehicle, check the pickup point, waiting-time policy, flight-delay procedure, luggage capacity, cancellation terms and total price—not just the headline fare.

Option 3: Airport or city bus

A bus may be convenient if a currently operating service stops near your accommodation. However, routes and operating patterns can change, and a route that appeared in an older guide may no longer be the best choice. Confirm the current route, stop location, departure time and luggage conditions on the day you travel.

Do not choose a bus solely because it appears direct. Compare the walk from the stop to your hotel, the expected traffic and the time of your arrival.

Airport limousine update (checked Nov 10, 2026)

  • Airport Limousine 1 — Haeundae/Gijang: The airport timetable is split by destination. The Jangsan–Haeundae service is listed with first/last airport departures at 07:10 / 16:50; the Gijang service at 10:10 / 21:40. Adult fare is KRW 9,500. These are separate services, not one continuous operating window.
  • Stops on the Jangsan–Haeundae route include Haeundae Beach and Dongbaekseom Entrance. The Gijang route serves the East Busan Tourism Complex. Check the exact stop and destination shown on the timetable before boarding.
  • Rail: the airport light rail connects with Busan Metro Line 2 at Sasang and Line 3 at Daejeo. For Haeundae, compare the Sasang–Line 2 route with the bus based on your hotel address and arrival time.

Schedules, fares and payment options can change. Before departure, check Busan City's airport limousine route update and the official Gimhae Airport transport page.

Before leaving the airport

  • Save your hotel’s full address and nearest station or bus stop.
  • Check the last rail departure for your travel date; landing time is not the same as the time you reach the platform.
  • Keep a backup option if your flight is delayed or baggage collection takes longer than expected.
  • Check whether your hotel has late check-in instructions.
  • Use a route planner for the final journey and recheck it before leaving the terminal.

Routes, timetables, fares and airport bus operations can change. Verify the latest details with Visit Busan’s official transportation guide and the relevant operator before departure.

Common questions

What is the budget-friendly way to reach Haeundae?

The light rail and metro combination is often a budget-friendly option, but it involves transfers. Check current fares, service hours and the route to your exact accommodation before travelling.

Is a taxi easier with luggage?

A taxi can reduce transfers and walking with heavy luggage, but the fare is usually higher and road traffic affects travel time. Compare the convenience against your budget and arrival time.

What if my flight arrives late?

Check the last light rail, metro and bus services for your arrival date rather than relying on a general timetable. If public transport will not work for your arrival, compare a taxi with a pre-booked transfer.

Plan your airport transfer first—then check if a pass saves money

The Visit Busan Pass is worth comparing only if you plan to visit several included paid attractions. Airport transfers and everyday metro or bus trips are separate costs, and the mobile pass is not a transit card.
