Christmas Village Busan 2026: Dates, Tickets & Visitor Guide

A ticketed Christmas village comes to Busan Cinema Center from November 26 to December 25, 2026. Check the operating calendar and ticket time slot before travelling.

BUSAN LOCAL GUIDEUPDATED 11 NOV 2026

Quick facts: Nov 26–Dec 25, 2026 · 21 operating days · Busan Cinema Center outdoor plaza · paid, timed-entry tickets.

Dates and operating days

Christmas Village Busan runs from November 26 to December 25, 2026, but it is not open every day. The regular schedule is Thursday through Sunday, with daily operation added from December 21 through 25.

  • November: Nov 26–29.
  • December 3–20: Thursday through Sunday each week.
  • December 21–25: open every day.

Opening hours and ticket time slots

  • Weekdays: 16:00–22:00.
  • Weekends: 11:00–22:00. Day tickets allow first entry from 11:00–15:00; night tickets from 16:00.
  • Dec 24–25: 11:00–22:00.

On Dec 21–23, weekday hours apply. Your ticket type determines the first permitted entry time. Check the exact ticket conditions before purchase; leaving the venue may affect re-entry.

Standard ticket prices

  • Weekday: ₩5,900.
  • Weekend daytime: ₩5,900.
  • Weekend evening: ₩7,900.
  • Christmas season, Dec 24–25: ₩14,900.

These are the standard prices shown in the ticket-opening notice. Promotional inventory and availability can change. Tickets are sold for specific dates and entry periods; check the live ticket page for remaining slots and current terms.

Check Christmas Village Busan tickets on YES24. The organizer’s official website also provides a separate “For International Visitors” ticket-information link. International booking, payment and entry conditions should be checked there; do not assume a Korean ticketing account or payment method will work.

What can you see there?

The outdoor village is planned around a central plaza, themed spaces, food and drink stalls, gift shops, performances and participatory experiences. The published program includes a Santa parade, choir and busking performances, seasonal food from Busan brands, and photo or activity areas. Specific performers and time tables may change.

Location and how to get there

The event takes place at the outdoor plaza of Busan Cinema Center, 120 Suyeonggangbyeon-daero, Haeundae-gu, Busan. Use Centum City Station on Metro Line 2 as the main rail reference and follow signs toward Busan Cinema Center. Allow extra time for the walk from the station and for entry checks.

Tips for a smoother visit

  • Choose the date first, then select the matching day/night ticket.
  • Do not arrive assuming every date between Nov 26 and Dec 25 is an operating day.
  • Check the organizer’s notice for entry rules, re-entry, weather operations and program times.
  • Expect higher demand around weekends and Dec 24–25; compare nearby dining plans before or after your slot.
  • For another winter evening, see the Busan winter travel guide and its Haeundae Light Festival notes.
