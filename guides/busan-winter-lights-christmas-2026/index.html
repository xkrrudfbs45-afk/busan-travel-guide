Busan Winter Lights & Christmas 2026–27

Busan's winter events are spread across different neighborhoods. Use this guide to compare the confirmed Haeundae light festival and Christmas Village dates, understand what is not yet announced, and plan an evening without crossing the city unnecessarily.

2026–27 SEASONOFFICIAL DATES CHECKEDRECONFIRM BEFORE TRAVEL

Important date note: Event dates below are taken from Busan Metropolitan City’s event calendar as checked on November 10, 2026. Event operations, access, ticketing and performance times can change. Gwangbok-ro’s 2026–27 festival dates were not confirmed in the official listing reviewed for this guide.

At a glance: confirmed winter dates

  • Christmas Village Busan: November 26–December 25, 2026, at Busan Cinema Center.
  • 13th Haeundae Light Festival: November 28, 2026–January 10, 2027, around Haeundae Beach, Gunam-ro and Haeundae Square.
  • Gwangbok-ro Winter Light Tree Festival: the 2026–27 dates were not confirmed in the official event listing checked for this update. The previous edition ran December 5, 2025–February 22, 2026; do not use those dates as this season’s schedule.

Haeundae Light Festival: a beach-and-downtown evening

The city calendar lists the 13th Haeundae Light Festival from November 28, 2026, to January 10, 2027. Its listed area covers Haeundae Beach, Gunam-ro and Haeundae Square. This makes it easiest to pair with a Haeundae-area dinner, beach walk or nearby stay rather than treating it as a stop on a Nampo-dong evening.

Allow time to walk between the beach and Gunam-ro, and check the district’s latest notice for lighting hours, performances, access arrangements and any weather-related changes before setting out.

Christmas Village Busan: a ticketed-event check matters

Busan Metropolitan City lists Christmas Village Busan for November 26–December 25, 2026, at Busan Cinema Center. Unlike an open street-light display, this is a named event at a defined venue. Check the organizer’s current admission, reservation, operating-hour and program notices before visiting; do not assume entry is free or that every program runs every day.

Its location in Centum City makes it practical to combine with an indoor shopping or dining plan. If you also want the Haeundae Light Festival, check the evening schedule and allow for the transfer rather than assuming the two venues are next door.

Gwangbok-ro: wait for the new season announcement

Gwangbok-ro and Gwangbokjungang-ro are the traditional winter-light area in Jung-gu. The official district page describes the annual Christmas Tree Culture Festival as a late-November-to-early-January event, while the city’s archived listing for the previous Winter Light Tree Festival gives December 5, 2025–February 22, 2026. Neither source establishes the 2026–27 dates. Treat Gwangbok-ro as a place to monitor, not a confirmed event date.

When the new schedule is announced, this area can be paired with BIFF Square, Gukje Market and Kkangtong Market. Those are separate neighborhood stops, so check closing times and plan a walkable route instead of trying to fit in Haeundae on the same short evening.

Which winter event should you choose?

  • For a beach atmosphere: choose Haeundae and build the evening around the beach, Gunam-ro and nearby restaurants.
  • For a venue-based Christmas outing: check the Christmas Village ticket and program details at Busan Cinema Center.
  • For old-town shopping and street food: keep Nampo-dong and Gwangbok-ro on a separate evening, and wait for the 2026–27 light-festival announcement.

Practical winter planning

Coastal wind can make an evening feel colder than the daytime forecast suggests. Wear layers, bring a wind-resistant outer layer and comfortable shoes, and keep an indoor alternative in mind. Crowds can affect walking speed and public transport access; follow local staff instructions and check transit notices after major events.
