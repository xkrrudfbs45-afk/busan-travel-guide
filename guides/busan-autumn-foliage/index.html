Busan Autumn Foliage: Hwangnyeongsan, Geumjeongsan & Beomeosa

Busan is known for its coastline, but November brings another side of the city into view. Choose a mountain lookout, a temple walk or a wooded park—and check local conditions before setting out.

Busan, South KoreaIndependent travel guideUpdated November 2026

Before you go: Autumn color in Busan is generally expected from late October into mid-November, but the timing changes with temperature, rainfall and elevation. Treat seasonal dates as estimates, not guarantees.

When to see autumn leaves in Busan

Visit Busan describes early to mid-November as a typical period for peak autumn color in Busan. The Korea Forest Service and the National Arboretum publish seasonal forecasts, but local color can vary between a mountain ridge, a temple courtyard and a lower city park. If you are visiting around November 10, check recent local observations rather than assuming every site is at its peak.

Hwangnyeongsan: city views with autumn color

Hwangnyeongsan is a central Busan mountain spanning several districts. Its summit area and viewing lounge are known for wide city and coastal views; Visit Busan also describes red maple foliage as one of the mountain's autumn attractions. This is the best choice if you want a combination of mountain scenery and a panoramic view of Busan rather than a long forest walk. The summit is reached by hiking trails or road access; the full hike can take around two to three hours depending on the route. The official tourism listing gives the viewing lounge address as 391-39 Hwangnyeongsan-ro, Nam-gu. A taxi is the more straightforward option for many visitors. Public-transport access involves a local bus and an additional walk, so check the current route before leaving.

Geumjeongsan and Beomeosa Temple: a slower mountain day

Geumjeongsan offers a different autumn experience: forested slopes, historic fortress scenery and Beomeosa Temple at the foot of the mountain. Visit Busan recommends Beomeosa as one of its autumn foliage locations and lists the temple at 250 Beomeosa-ro, Geumjeong-gu, with regular visiting hours of 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. The temple is an active religious site; keep voices low and follow photography notices. For a more active outing, use the official Geumjeongsan information to plan a hike or check the Geumgang Park Cable Car, which reaches a ridge area. The cable car is a separate access option, not a direct connection to Beomeosa; confirm operating hours and service status on the day.

Geumgang Park: an easier woodland walk

Geumgang Park in Dongnae-gu is another option for travelers who want trees and walking paths without committing to a mountain hike. Visit Busan lists it among the city's autumn destinations and notes that the park's illuminated fortress area can be attractive in the evening. The park and Beomeosa are separate stops; do not assume they form one short walking route. Check the park's current opening and cable-car information before visiting.

Which autumn route should you choose?

Choose Hwangnyeongsan for a panoramic city view and a shorter visit focused around the lookout. Choose Geumjeongsan and Beomeosa for a half-day or longer outing that combines temple architecture and mountain scenery. Choose Geumgang Park for a more relaxed woodland walk. If you have only one day, avoid trying to hike all three areas; they are spread across Busan and require separate transfers.

Practical tips

Wear shoes with grip because fallen leaves can make steps and slopes slippery. Bring a light layer: temperatures and wind at higher viewpoints can feel different from the coast. Check the latest foliage conditions, weather, cable-car operation and temple notices before departure. Avoid entering closed trails, picking leaves or disturbing temple visitors.
