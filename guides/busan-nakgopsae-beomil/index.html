Nakgopsae in Busan: Beomil-dong, Jobang-ro & What to Order

Octopus, beef intestine and shrimp simmer together in a spicy, savoury sauce. Nakgopsae is a signature Busan meal—but knowing the dish name, the neighbourhoods and how to order makes the experience easier for first-time visitors.

Quick answer: Beomil-dong’s Jobang-ro area is closely associated with Busan-style spicy octopus restaurants. Gaemijip’s well-known main branch, however, is near Gukje Market in Nampo-dong—not in Beomil-dong. Choose the area that fits the rest of your day.

What does nakgopsae mean?

The name combines three Korean ingredient words: nakji (octopus), gopchang (intestine, usually beef intestine on menus) and saeu (shrimp). The ingredients simmer in a red chilli-garlic sauce with vegetables. The result is savoury, a little sweet and often spicy, with richness from the intestine and sweetness from the seafood. Recipes and heat levels vary by restaurant.

Related menu names can help you choose: naksae is octopus and shrimp; nakgop is octopus and intestine. Ask whether a restaurant can adjust ingredients if you do not eat offal or have a seafood allergy.

Why visit Beomil-dong and Jobang-ro?

Jobang-ro in Beomil-dong is part of the older downtown area associated with Busan’s spicy octopus dining culture. It works especially well if you are exploring the Beomil Station area, Busanjin Market or the nearby jewellery district. Rather than travelling across the city for a single meal, pair the restaurant with sights and errands in the same part of town.

Where to start

Oryukdo Nakji Beomil Main Branch (오륙도 낙지 범일본점)

This restaurant is listed by Visit Busan as a Beomil-dong option for nakgopsae and other octopus dishes. The tourism listing gives the address as 46-1 Jeonggongdan-ro, Dong-gu, Busan, and lists hours as 10:30–21:00 with public-holiday closures. Hours can change, so confirm before setting out.

Jobang-ro spicy octopus restaurants

Several restaurants around Jobang-ro serve Jobang-style nakji bokkeum and nakgopsae. Compare the current menu, group size, spice level and route from your nearest station instead of assuming every restaurant uses the same recipe or prices.

Gaemijip Main Branch (개미집 본점) — Nampo / Gukje Market

Gaemijip is a famous name for nakgopsae, but its main branch is at 21-1 Junggu-ro 30beon-gil, Jung-gu, near Gukje Market and BIFF Square. This is a better fit if your day already includes Nampo-dong, Jagalchi Market or the old downtown area.

How to eat it

  1. Let the pot simmer until the restaurant staff or the cooking instructions indicate it is ready. Avoid overcooking the octopus, which can become chewy.
  2. Spoon a little of the sauce and ingredients over rice and mix as you go.
  3. Ask about spice level before cooking; the sauce can taste hotter as it reduces.
  4. If the restaurant offers fried rice at the end, save some sauce and ask whether it is available.

Before you go

  • Check current opening hours, last order and weekly or public-holiday closures.
  • Ask about the minimum order; some hot-pot dishes are easier to share.
  • Confirm whether rice, noodles or fried rice cost extra.
  • Check spice level and ingredients, especially if you avoid offal, shellfish or seafood.
  • Use the address and map link for the exact branch—restaurant names can be similar.
