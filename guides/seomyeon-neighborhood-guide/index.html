Seomyeon Neighborhood Guide: Food, Stays and Getting Around

Seomyeon is where Busan’s two main metro lines meet. Use this page to plan the area in one place, then open the detailed guide for each decision.

NEIGHBORHOOD HUB5 MIN READCHECK LIVE ROUTES BEFORE TRAVEL

Quick summary

  • Best as a flexible city base with restaurants, shops and evening life nearby.
  • Seomyeon Station is where Metro Lines 1 and 2 meet, so Nampo-dong and Haeundae are both direct rides.
  • The trade-off is atmosphere: it feels busy and urban, not like a seaside escape.

Is Seomyeon the right base for you?

Choose Seomyeon if your days are spread across Busan, or if you want plenty of restaurants and shops close to your hotel. Its metro connections make cross-city days easier.

If the beach is the main reason for your trip, compare Haeundae. Our where-to-stay guide compares both.

Getting there and around

  • From Gimhae Airport: take the light rail to Sasang and change to Metro Line 2, or compare a taxi and the airport limousine bus. Our airport-to-Seomyeon guide lists the details on the airport’s official page, checked November 5, 2026.
  • From Busan Station (KTX): Metro Line 1 runs directly to Seomyeon.
  • Getting around: Line 1 runs to Nampo-dong and Dongnae, and Line 2 runs to Haeundae. See the transit card guide.

Where to stay

Seomyeon covers a large commercial area. Check the walk from your hotel to the station exit, because a hotel described as “Seomyeon” can still mean a longer or uphill walk.

Compare it with Haeundae and Gwangalli in Where to Stay in Busan.

Easy day plans from Seomyeon

  • South on Line 1: Jagalchi Market and Nampo-dong, or the Busan Station to Nampo walk.
  • North on Line 1: a Dongnae hot-spring half day or the Beomeosa area.
  • East on Line 2: Haeundae and Gwangalli.

Check the journey and the last train for your return. Our three-day itinerary groups these by area.

Common questions

Is Seomyeon a good base for a first trip to Busan?

Yes, if your plans are spread across the city. It sits where Metro Lines 1 and 2 meet, so Nampo-dong and Haeundae are both direct rides.

How do I get from Gimhae Airport to Seomyeon?

A common route is the Busan–Gimhae Light Rail to Sasang, then Metro Line 2 to Seomyeon. Compare a taxi or the airport limousine if you have heavy bags or arrive late.
