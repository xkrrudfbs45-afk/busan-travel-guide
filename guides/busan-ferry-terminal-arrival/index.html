ARRIVAL GUIDE · BUSAN PORT

Japan to Busan by Ferry: Port Arrival and City Transfer Guide

Your first steps after arriving by ferry: identify the right terminal, reach Busan Station and continue into the city without confusing the international and coastal ports.

BUSAN, SOUTH KOREAFERRY ARRIVALCHECK YOUR OPERATOR'S NOTICE

Quick answer: International ferries from Japan use Busan Port International Passenger Terminal at 206 Chungjang-daero, Dong-gu. From Busan Station, the Busan Port Authority describes a pedestrian route via Station Exit 10, moving walkways and the Busan Port skywalk; allow about 10–15 minutes on foot. Check your ferry operator's latest check-in and boarding instructions.

1. Make sure you are going to the right terminal

Busan has more than one passenger terminal. Ferries serving Japan use the Busan Port International Passenger Terminal. The Coastal Passenger Terminal is a different facility used for domestic routes such as the Busan–Jeju service. Do not copy an address or taxi destination from a page about the other terminal.

International terminal address: 206 Chungjang-daero, Dong-gu, Busan, Republic of Korea (부산항 국제여객터미널).

2. From the ferry terminal to Busan Station

The Busan Port Authority's route is: Busan Station building Exit 10 → moving walkway → Busan Port pedestrian skywalk → International Passenger Terminal. The authority gives an approximate walking time of 10–15 minutes. If you are carrying large suitcases, allow extra time for lifts, crowds and finding the correct entrance.

Some venue pages describe a shorter 5–10 minute connection, depending on the starting point and route. For trip planning with luggage, use the more conservative 10–15 minute estimate and follow the signs on site.

3. What is inside the terminal?

The terminal is a multi-level building. The ferry operator's passenger information describes the second floor as the arrival level, with services such as immigration access, currency exchange, roaming/SIM services, tourist information, convenience stores and baggage services. The third floor is used for departure procedures and includes ferry check-in counters and other passenger facilities. Individual counters and services can change, so use the signs and your operator's current notice rather than relying on a fixed counter number.

After immigration and baggage collection, check where you need to go next before leaving the building. If you need a SIM, cash or help with directions, check current availability and opening hours; do not assume every counter is staffed for every sailing.

4. Continue into Busan

  • KTX or intercity train: Walk to Busan Station and follow station signs for the train hall. Check your departure station and ticket before setting out.
  • Subway Line 1: Busan Station connects with central areas including Nampo and Seomyeon. For Haeundae, you will generally need to transfer to Line 2 at Seomyeon.
  • Taxi: Use the official taxi stand and show the destination name in Korean. Confirm the destination is your hotel, not simply “Busan Station” if you are staying elsewhere.
  • Walking around the old downtown: Choryang and Busan Station are close to Nampo by metro, but the full walk is longer than the terminal-to-station link. Plan it as a separate outing if you have luggage.

Prepare with our Busan transit card and public transport guide, Busan Station to Nampo walking route and Seoul–Busan KTX booking guide.

5. Before your return ferry

  • Check the operator's latest check-in opening and closing times, boarding gate and required documents.
  • Do not use the ship's departure time as your terminal arrival target; allow time for check-in, security and immigration as instructed by the operator.
  • Confirm whether your sailing departs from the international passenger terminal and which floor/counter your operator uses.
  • If you are connecting from KTX, leave a generous buffer for train delays, walking and ferry procedures. Avoid booking a tight same-day connection.

Ferry schedules, routes, check-in deadlines, terminal services and floor arrangements can change by operator and sailing. This guide explains the terminal and city connection; it is not a substitute for the instructions on your ticket or the ferry company's latest notice.
