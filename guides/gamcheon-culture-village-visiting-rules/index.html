Gamcheon Culture Village: New Visiting Rules for 2027

Gamcheon is a colorful hillside attraction and a residential neighborhood. Saha-gu has designated it a special-management area, with different rules for residential lanes, tour vehicles and groups.

Important distinction: the 09:00–18:00 visitor-time restriction applies to designated residential-dense areas, not automatically to every street and attraction in Gamcheon Culture Village. Check the official zone map before planning your route.

The key rules at a glance

  • Designation and start dates: the special-management area was designated on August 1, 2026. The official guide lists January 1, 2027 as the effective date for the residential-area visiting-hour regime.
  • Residential-dense areas: visitor access is limited to 09:00–18:00.
  • Tour vehicles: vehicles carrying visitors, including taxis, are restricted on an approximately 1 km section of Gamnae 2-ro, from the Information Center entrance toward the Memory’s Shadow Photo Zone, from 09:00–18:00. The guidance period runs through December 31, 2027.
  • Groups of 10 or more: groups must purchase the official village guide map. This requirement has been effective since August 1, 2026. The official guide does not state the current map price.
  • Charter buses: an advance-reservation system applies. Confirm arrangements before arriving by coach.
  • Penalties: fines for violations of the tourist-vehicle restriction are scheduled to begin on January 1, 2028, after the guidance period.

Where do the visiting-hour rules apply?

The special-management area is divided into four management zones: residential-dense areas, mixed residential-and-commercial areas, monitoring areas and a tourist-vehicle restriction area. The strict 09:00–18:00 visitor window applies to the residential-dense zone. Other zones use different management measures, such as visitor-etiquette guidance, monitoring or vehicle controls.

Do not treat the entire village as either fully open or fully closed after 18:00. Use the official map to identify the zone you plan to enter, and follow the signs posted on the day.

Visiting with a group or by taxi

If your party has 10 or more visitors, arrange to purchase the official guide map. The published special-management notice does not provide a confirmed price, so this guide does not quote one. Tour groups arriving by charter bus should check the advance-reservation process with the village or their tour operator.

For the Gamnae 2-ro restriction, the notice covers vehicles carrying tourists, including taxis, during 09:00–18:00. Do not assume a taxi can take you through the restricted road to a photo stop. Plan to use the permitted drop-off points shown on the official map and walk only on routes open to visitors.

How to visit respectfully

  • Keep voices low in residential lanes and do not enter private yards, stairways or buildings.
  • Do not block doorways, narrow stairs or residents’ routes for photographs.
  • Follow the zone map and on-site signs, especially around the residential-dense area.
  • Do not fly a drone unless the current rules for that exact location explicitly allow it.
  • For sunset photography, check the sunset time and make sure your chosen viewpoint is outside any restricted residential zone or within its permitted hours.

Download the official visitor map

The village’s official website provides special-management guides and zone maps in multiple languages. Review the map before travelling; it is more reliable than guessing zone boundaries from a general tourist map.
