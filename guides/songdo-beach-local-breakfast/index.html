Songdo Beach, Busan: Things to Do & Local Breakfast

If the busiest beach districts feel a little too fast, head west. Songdo pairs an easy seaside stroll with wide ocean views, coastal walks and a proper Korean fish-soup breakfast just steps from the beach.

BUSAN, SOUTH KOREA5 MIN READLOCAL AREA GUIDE

Why visit Songdo? Located in Seo-gu in western Busan, Songdo is a historic beach area with a more laid-back feel than the city’s best-known resort strips. It is not guaranteed to be empty, especially on sunny weekends, but it can be a lovely choice if your idea of a beach day is a walk, a sea view and an unhurried meal rather than a packed schedule.

A slower way to enjoy the sea

Songdo Beach opened in 1913 and is recognized as Korea’s first public beach. Today, you can walk along the sand, watch the fishing boats and ships offshore, and take in the coastline without needing to plan a full day of activities. The atmosphere changes with the weather and season, so early morning is especially appealing if you prefer a quieter start.

For a little more adventure, the Songdo Cloud Walk stretches out above the water, while the Songdo Marine Cable Car crosses the sea between the beach side and Amnam Park. If you have more time, continue toward Amnam Park’s coastal scenery and walking trails.

Local Pick · Breakfast

Breakfast by the beach: Soksihwonhae Daegutang

A seaside morning is even better when you can sit down to something warm. One local place to look up is 속시원해대구탕 송도본점 (Soksihwonhae Daegutang Songdo Main Branch), a Korean fish-soup restaurant on Songdohaebyeon-ro, close to the beach.

Its signature dish is daegu-tang, a hot soup made with cod. It is a familiar Korean comfort-food breakfast: warming broth, fish and rice, with side dishes. For international visitors who want to try a local-style morning meal instead of starting with coffee and pastries, it is a useful option to consider. The soup is served as a meal, so you do not need to plan a separate breakfast stop.

Before you go

  • Search the Korean name: 속시원해대구탕 송도본점. This helps you find the right place in map apps.
  • Location: 79 Songdohaebyeon-ro, Seo-gu, Busan, on the beach road.
  • Early opening: Public listings show a 7:00 a.m. opening, making it worth checking for breakfast. Hours can change, so confirm on the day.
  • What to order: Daegu-tang (cod soup). The menu and prices may change; check the current menu at the restaurant.
  • Language tip: Save the Korean restaurant name and menu name on your phone. A translation app can make ordering easier if English menus are not available.

An easy Songdo morning itinerary

  1. Start with the beach. Arrive early, take a short walk and enjoy the changing light over the water.
  2. Have daegu-tang for breakfast. Head to Soksihwonhae Daegutang and try a classic Korean fish-soup meal.
  3. Walk above the sea. Visit Songdo Cloud Walk if it is open and weather conditions allow.
  4. Choose one extra stop. Ride the marine cable car or continue toward Amnam Park instead of trying to fit everything into one morning.

For a little more structure, check current details for the official Songdo Beach visitor information before setting out. The sea-facing walkways and cable car can be affected by weather or operational changes.

Want to ride the cable car?

Getting there and choosing where to stay

Songdo Beach is in Seo-gu in western Busan, near the Nampo-dong and Jagalchi area but not directly served by a metro station at the beach itself. From Jagalchi Station, local buses connect to Songdo Beach; check the route from your accommodation in a map app before leaving. If you are carrying luggage or arriving late, compare the door-to-door journey rather than relying only on straight-line distance. Songdo Beach: frequently asked questions

Is Songdo Beach less crowded than Haeundae?

Songdo often feels more relaxed than Busan’s busiest resort areas, but it is not always quiet. Sunny weekends, holidays and peak summer days can bring more visitors; go earlier in the day if you prefer a calmer walk.

What can you do at Songdo Beach?

Walk along the beach, visit Songdo Cloud Walk when open, ride the Songdo Marine Cable Car or continue toward Amnam Park. Check weather and operating conditions before setting out.

Where can I eat breakfast near Songdo Beach?

Soksihwonhae Daegutang (속시원해대구탕 송도본점) is a local option to look up for daegu-tang, a Korean cod-soup meal. Check its current opening hours and menu before visiting.

Is Songdo Beach easy to reach by subway?

There is no metro station directly at the beach. Visitors commonly combine the subway with a local bus or taxi; check a map app for the best route from your hotel on the day.

Local tip: Leave room in the schedule. Songdo works best when the beach walk, breakfast and one coastal attraction are the plan—not just three stops squeezed between bigger sights.
