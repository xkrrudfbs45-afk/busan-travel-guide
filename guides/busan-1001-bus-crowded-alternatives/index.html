Bus 1001 Is Full at Busan Station? Routes to Centum, Haeundae & Songjeong

If you cannot board the 1001, choose a route by destination instead of waiting without a plan.

GO2BUSAN LOCAL GUIDEUpdated 20 November 2026

Quick answer: For Centum City / BEXCO, take regular Bus 40 or Metro Line 1 → Seomyeon → Line 2. For Haeundae Station, use Line 1 → Seomyeon → Line 2. For Haeundae Beach, check Bus 1003: Busan City lists 1001 for Haeundae Station and 1003 for the beach. Space is not guaranteed. For Songjeong, transfer from Line 2 to the Donghae Line at BEXCO.

When should you switch routes?

Bus 1001 is a direct option from Busan Station toward the eastern side of the city. If a full vehicle leaves without you, the best fallback depends on your destination—not on finding another bus with the same number.

On BEXCO event days: A public Busan concert-travel guide posted on 10 February 2026 recommends the metro as a predictable option for first-time visitors and describes the express bus as very busy in that event context. This is a local event-day observation, not a forecast for every departure. Read the original Korean community guide.

How to make each trip

Busan Station to Centum City

  1. For a direct bus, find the Busan Station stop and check Bus 40 in the live arrival system. Confirm the destination display and get off at Centum City Station / BEXCO.
  2. For the metro, board Line 1 at Busan Station, change to Line 2 at Seomyeon, and get off at Centum City. Follow station signs for BEXCO or your exact destination.

Busan Station to Haeundae

  1. Take Line 1 from Busan Station to Seomyeon.
  2. Transfer to Line 2 towards Jangsan and get off at Haeundae Station.
  3. Walk to your hotel or beach entrance; the best exit and walking time depend on your exact destination.

Busan City lists Bus 1001 for Haeundae Station and Bus 1003 for Haeundae Beach. If you are heading to the beach, check Bus 1003's direction and arrival before walking to the stop; it is not guaranteed to have spare seats.

Busan Station to Songjeong Beach

  1. Take Line 1 to Seomyeon and transfer to Line 2.
  2. At BEXCO, transfer to the Donghae Line and ride to Songjeong Station.
  3. Songjeong Station is not on the beach itself. Check the final walking leg or a local bus connection for your exact destination, especially with luggage.

Check the bus before joining the queue

Use the official Busan Bus Information System (English) to search routes and stops and check estimated arrivals and live vehicle locations. The system does not confirm seat availability. A route number appearing in search results does not mean you can board a full vehicle.

What if you have a suitcase?

For a large suitcase, the metro may be easier to plan than waiting for a crowded bus, but transfers and station walking can be tiring. Check lift locations and exits at both ends. If you have several heavy bags, compare a taxi for the door-to-door journey.

Common questions

Does Bus 40 go to Haeundae?

No. Bus 40 is the direct regular-bus option for Centum City / BEXCO. For Haeundae, use the metro transfer at Seomyeon or check Bus 1003 in live information.

Does Bus 1003 go to Haeundae Beach?

Busan Geopark lists Bus 1003 for Haeundae Beach and Bus 1001 for Haeundae Station. Confirm the live direction and stop before boarding; a seat is not guaranteed.

Is the metro easier with large luggage?

It can be easier to plan than waiting for a full bus, but transfers and station walks still take effort. Check lift access and consider a taxi if you have several large bags.
