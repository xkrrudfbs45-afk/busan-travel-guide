Bus 1001 Is Full? How to Reach Haeundae, Centum City and Songjeong from Busan Station

If the 1001 bus pulls away full, you do not have to wait for the next one. Choose a route by destination instead.

GO2BUSAN LOCAL GUIDEUpdated 20 November 2026

Quick answer: For Centum City / BEXCO, take regular Bus 40 or Metro Line 1 → Seomyeon → Line 2. For Haeundae, the same metro transfer is the most predictable alternative; Bus 1003 is another direct bus to check, but it can also be crowded. For Songjeong, use the metro and Donghae Line via BEXCO rather than waiting for a full 1001.

Why not just wait for another 1001?

Bus 1001 links Busan Station with the eastern coast, including Centum City, Haeundae, Songjeong and the Gijang area. Its direct route is convenient, which is exactly why many visitors choose it. But a scheduled bus is not a guaranteed seat: if the vehicle is full, the next departure may also be busy. Switch modes when the destination makes that easier.

How to make each trip

Busan Station to Centum City

  1. For a direct bus, find the Busan Station stop and check Bus 40 in the live arrival system. Confirm the destination display and get off at Centum City Station / BEXCO.
  2. For the metro, board Line 1 at Busan Station, change to Line 2 at Seomyeon, and get off at Centum City. Follow station signs for BEXCO or your exact destination.

Busan Station to Haeundae

  1. Take Line 1 from Busan Station to Seomyeon.
  2. Transfer to Line 2 towards Jangsan and get off at Haeundae Station.
  3. Walk to your hotel or beach entrance; Haeundae Beach is around a 15-minute walk from Exit 5, depending on your destination.

Bus 1003 can be checked as a direct alternative, but do not assume it will have spare seats. Use the real-time system before walking to its stop.

Busan Station to Songjeong Beach

  1. Take Line 1 to Seomyeon and transfer to Line 2.
  2. At BEXCO, transfer to the Donghae Line and ride to Songjeong Station.
  3. Walk approximately 17 minutes to Songjeong Beach, or check local bus connections if you have luggage or want to reduce walking.

Check the bus before joining the queue

Use the official Busan Bus Information System to search by route or stop and view estimated arrivals and live bus locations. Bus arrival estimates change with traffic. A route number appearing in the search results does not mean the bus has room for every waiting passenger.

What if you have a suitcase?

For a large suitcase, the metro may be more predictable than waiting for a crowded city bus, but transfers and station walking can be tiring. Check lift and exit locations at both ends, and consider a taxi for a door-to-door trip if you are travelling with several heavy bags. Do not assume an express bus will accept oversized luggage; follow the operator's luggage rules.
