Gijang Kkeutjip: Abalone Porridge Worth Planning a Stop For

If your Busan plans take you out toward Gijang, this is one restaurant to consider for a comforting abalone porridge meal served with a generous spread of Korean side dishes.

LOCAL FOODGIJANG · JUKSEONGCHECK DETAILS BEFORE VISITING

Why we recommend it: Go2Busan's local editor recommends Gijang Kkeutjip for its abalone porridge and the full-table meal that comes with it. It is a more deliberate food stop than a quick snack, so plan it around a Gijang day rather than squeezing it between distant city sights.

What to expect

Gijang Kkeutjip Jukseong Main Branch specializes in jeonbokjuk, Korean abalone porridge. The signature porridge is served as part of a set-style meal with a variety of side dishes and seafood accompaniments, making the experience feel more like a shared Korean table than a single bowl of porridge.

The restaurant is best suited to visitors who enjoy seafood and traditional Korean side dishes. If you are travelling alone or have a small appetite, pay attention to the minimum order: online menu listings indicate that the signature porridge sets are generally ordered for at least two servings. Confirm the current policy before visiting.

What should you order?

  • Ojik Jeonbokjuk: the signature abalone porridge set and a sensible starting point for a first visit.
  • Teuk Jeonbokjuk: the richer option listed with a larger amount of abalone.
  • Jeonbok-miyeokguk: an alternative if you prefer seaweed soup to porridge; check whether you are ordering the set or a standalone portion.

Menus and prices can change. Check the restaurant's current listing before you go, especially if you are choosing between set and standalone dishes or need to confirm the number of servings.

Plan around the location

The restaurant is at 895 Gijanghaean-ro, Gijang-eup, Gijang-gun, Busan. This is in the Jukseong area, away from the central Seomyeon and Nampo sightseeing circuit. It makes more sense as part of a Gijang or northeast-coast day than as a detour during a tightly packed central Busan itinerary.

Driving or taking a taxi can make the trip simpler, particularly for groups. If you are using public transport, check the full route from your accommodation and allow time for the final leg. Do not estimate the journey from straight-line distance alone.

Three things to know before you go

  • Expect possible queues. It is a popular destination restaurant, especially at busy meal times and on weekends. Check the current waiting or remote-queue options before setting out.
  • Avoid the break period. Listings have shown a mid-afternoon break, with different times on weekdays and weekends. Verify the day's hours and last order on the current restaurant listing.
  • Plan for a full meal. The set format and minimum serving rules may not suit someone looking for a quick, inexpensive solo lunch.

Practical information

  • Name: Gijang Kkeutjip Jukseong Main Branch (기장끝집 죽성본점)
  • Address: 1F, 895 Gijanghaean-ro, Gijang-eup, Gijang-gun, Busan
  • Phone: 0507-1491-0272 (confirm before travel)
  • Parking: parking is listed as available; check on-site directions when you arrive.
