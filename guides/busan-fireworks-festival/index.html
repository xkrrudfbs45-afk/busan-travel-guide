2026 Busan Fireworks Festival: Where to Watch, Transport & Local Tips

The 21st Busan Fireworks Festival takes place on Saturday, November 7, with viewing areas around Gwangalli Beach, Igidae and Dongbaekseom. Compare Gwangalli, Igidae, Dongbaekseom and Millak waterfront options, plan the journey home and check café or restaurant prices before booking.

Local perspective · Confirm live details before setting out. Crowds, road closures, ticket availability and event operations can change. Check the official festival notices on the day.

The essentials

  • Date: Saturday, November 7, 2026
  • Festival area: Gwangalli Beach, Igidae and Dongbaekseom
  • Main fireworks: the official schedule lists an overseas show at 7:00 p.m., the Busan multimedia show from 7:25 to 7:55 p.m., and a curtain-call display until 8:00 p.m.
  • Viewing: general public areas and separately ticketed seats; check current access notices.

Where should you watch?

Gwangalli Beach — closest to the atmosphere

Choose Gwangalli if you want the classic view toward Gwangan Bridge and the busiest festival atmosphere. It is also the most obvious place to expect dense crowds. Arrive with a plan, follow staff directions and do not push toward the waterline for a better photo.

Igidae — a view from across the water

Igidae offers a different, more distant perspective across the bay. Paths can be sloped and access may be restricted by crowd controls, so use only open, designated routes. Do not climb barriers or stand on rocks or exposed coastal edges.

Dongbaekseom — Haeundae-side option

Dongbaekseom gives you a Haeundae-side view of the wider seascape, but the bridge and fireworks may look farther away and sightlines vary by position. Check the route and crowd controls before committing to this area.

Local tip: the “best” spot depends on whether you value atmosphere, a wider view or a less central position. Do not assume every viewpoint will remain open throughout the event.

Millak Waterfront Park and the Millak Raw Fish Center area — a different angle

Millak Waterfront Park and the nearby waterfront around Millak Raw Fish Center are alternatives to standing on the busiest part of Gwangalli Beach. The view is more angled and can be partly blocked by buildings, crowds or temporary barriers, so treat this as a possible viewpoint rather than a guaranteed clear view. Check the latest access and crowd-control notices, and do not assume the market building, rooftop or a restaurant will allow festival viewing without permission.

Café and restaurant budget tip: a seat facing the bridge can be in high demand on festival night. Before booking, ask whether there is a special event menu or package, a minimum spend per person or table, a time limit, a reservation deposit, or a separate viewing charge. Confirm the total price in writing. If the terms feel unclear, watch from a public area instead.

Getting there and getting home

Public transport is the sensible default. For Gwangalli, the official guide points visitors to Gwangan Station on subway Line 2 (Exit 3). Igidae and Dongbaekseom have different bus and station approaches; check the official directions for your chosen area.

  • Expect phased road closures around the festival area and Gwangan Bridge.
  • Do not plan on parking near the beach; the official festival guide says visitor parking is not provided nearby.
  • Agree on a meeting point with your group before arriving. Mobile networks and messaging can be unreliable in dense crowds.
  • After the show, wait for crowd flow to ease rather than forcing your way onto a packed platform. Follow station staff and temporary signs.

Check the official traffic-control notice and official directions before leaving.

Tickets, food and avoiding surprise charges

Watching from public areas is different from buying a reserved seat. The published 2026 prices are R seats at KRW 100,000 (table and chairs) and S seats at KRW 70,000 (chair seating). Availability and sales deadlines can change; buy only through links from the official ticket page.

  • Avoid resale scams: do not rely on screenshots, unofficial transfers or social-media sellers. Check ticket name, entry rules and refund terms at the official seller.
  • Check restaurant prices before ordering: look at the menu and price per person, serving size, minimum order, set-menu conditions and any seating or service charge. Ask before ordering if anything is unclear.
  • Confirm accommodation totals: check the final price for the exact date and number of guests, including taxes, cleaning fees, parking and cancellation terms. A low headline price may not be the final amount.
  • Keep evidence: save the booking confirmation, menu or displayed price and receipt if the amount charged differs from what was shown.
  • Skip pressure sales: avoid anyone insisting on an immediate cash payment or promising a “secret” private viewing spot without clear terms.

These are sensible precautions for a high-demand event, not a claim that every business raises prices unfairly.

Official 2026 festival merchandise

The festival's first official merchandise collection was announced for online sale from November 3. The designs were created in collaboration with illustrator Merry Friday, who designed this year's main festival poster.

  • Blanket (130 × 160 cm): KRW 55,000 — two designs, useful as a warm layer or picnic mat.
  • Fabric coaster: KRW 9,000.
  • Snow globe: KRW 42,000.
  • Umbrella: KRW 29,000.
  • Card-cover sticker: KRW 3,000.

Official goods are sold through the NOL platform. The announced options are delivery or pickup at the designated event MD booth. Event-day pickup is only available on November 7 from 11 a.m. to 4 p.m.; collection is not available after the booth closes. Check the current product listing, stock, delivery terms and pickup instructions before paying.

Crowd and weather checklist

  • Wear comfortable shoes and warm layers; the waterfront can feel colder and windier at night.
  • Bring water and a small power bank, but keep bags compact.
  • Choose a meeting point outside the densest viewing area in case your group gets separated.
  • Keep children close, agree on an emergency contact and do not lift children onto railings.
  • Follow staff instructions, keep emergency access routes clear and leave immediately if a crowd begins to surge.
  • Use public toilets before the main show when possible; queues can grow quickly.
