Arriving at Busan Station After 9 PM: How to Reach Your Hotel

A late KTX arrival does not have to become a stressful transfer. Choose your route based on the actual time you leave the station, your hotel area and how much luggage you carry.

First rule: do not use a remembered “last train” or “last bus” time. The final departure depends on the station, direction, service date and transfer. Check the live timetable for the exact night you arrive.

Choose by your actual arrival time

  • Still within metro service: the subway is usually the easiest way to avoid road congestion. For Haeundae, take Line 1 from Busan Station to Seomyeon and transfer to Line 2 toward Jangsan. Confirm the last train for both legs before entering the station.
  • A suitable bus is still running: check the Busan Bus Information Management System for the stop, direction and live arrival time. Route 1003 is one of the seated-bus routes included in Busan’s cashless-bus pilot; have a transport card ready.
  • Metro and bus service have ended, or you have bulky luggage: use the official taxi rank outside the station or a taxi app. Show the driver your hotel name and Korean address.

Option 1: Subway via Seomyeon

For Haeundae, the standard rail route is Line 1 from Busan Station to Seomyeon, then Line 2 toward Jangsan. Other hotel areas may require a different transfer or no transfer at all. Do not assume the last train from Busan Station is also the last useful train for your full journey: the connection at Seomyeon may have an earlier final departure.

Use the official Busan Metro station timetable and select the boarding station, direction and date. If your arrival is close to the final departure, allow time to walk from the KTX platform to the metro entrance, find the correct platform and make the transfer.

Option 2: Bus 1003 and other city buses

Bus 1003 is one of the seated-bus routes in the cashless-bus pilot. Before choosing it, check the official route map to confirm that it serves your exact boarding stop and destination, then check the live stop display and the official Busan Bus Information System; do not rely on an old social-media post or an unverified “1:30 a.m. last bus” claim.

Important: cashless-bus pilot. Route 1003 is included in Busan’s three-month cashless-bus pilot that began October 1, 2026. Cash-only passengers may board first and pay later by bank transfer under the city’s policy, but that is not a dependable option for visitors without a Korean bank account. Prepare a T-money or Cashbee card before boarding. Read the cashless-bus and transport-card guide.

Option 3: Taxi from the station

If public transport is no longer practical, use the marked taxi queue outside Busan Station or a recognised taxi app. Before the ride, check that the destination shown to the driver is the correct hotel. A Korean-language address is useful because several hotels can have similar English names.

  • Keep your hotel name, Korean address and phone number available offline.
  • Check the destination on the taxi app or meter display before setting off.
  • For a group or large suitcases, confirm that the vehicle has enough passenger and luggage space.
  • Do not accept an unsolicited ride from someone approaching you inside the station.

Before your KTX arrives: five quick checks

  1. Save the hotel’s Korean address and phone number.
  2. Check the actual KTX arrival time, not only the scheduled departure.
  3. Look up the final metro departure from Busan Station and the transfer station.
  4. Check the live bus arrival and direction if you plan to use route 1003.
  5. Decide in advance when you will switch to a taxi rather than waiting for a service that may have ended.

For luggage storage or delivery, compare the service’s actual collection deadline and hotel delivery conditions before booking. See our Busan transport-card guide, Busan Station local walk and Gimhae Airport late-arrival guide.
