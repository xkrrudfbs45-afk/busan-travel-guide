YEONGDO · COASTAL WALKS

Taejongdae after the Danubi train suspension: Free shuttle and local tips

The Danubi train is currently suspended for safety inspection. A free circular shuttle operates Wednesday to Sunday; check the operator’s timetable and weather notice before visiting.

YEONGDO · BUSAN5 MIN READPRACTICAL LOCAL TIPS

Quick answer: The Danubi train is currently suspended for safety inspection. Busan Tourism Organization provides a free circular shuttle Wednesday to Sunday, 09:30–17:30. Pick up a free ticket for a specific departure at the ticket office, 09:10–17:30; tickets are first come, first served. There is no shuttle on Mondays or Tuesdays, and bad weather can stop service.

1. Understand the terrain first

Taejongdae is a large coastal park with slopes and walking sections. The Danubi train can reduce some of the walking, but it is still important to check the route, stops and current operating information. Visitors with limited mobility, strollers or tight schedules should not assume every viewpoint is step-free or directly beside a stop.

2. The Danubi train is suspended; use the free shuttle

Busan Tourism Organization currently lists the Danubi train as suspended for safety inspection. Its free circular shuttle operates Wednesday to Sunday, 09:30–17:30, until the inspection ends. Collect a free ticket for a specific departure at the Danubi ticket office, 09:10–17:30; tickets are first come, first served. There is no shuttle on Mondays or Tuesdays (holiday schedules may change), and service stops in heavy rain, typhoons or other severe weather. The operator page does not list a train restart date, so check the notice again on the day you visit.

3. If the shuttle is full or not running

  • Walk only if the slopes and distance suit your group. The full park loop is about 3.8 km; wear suitable shoes and allow extra time.
  • Check local bus access before setting out. Buses reach the park entrance area, but they do not replace the shuttle around the park loop.
  • Do not assume a taxi can reach every viewpoint. Check the operator’s vehicle-access notice and the final walking distance.

On Mondays and Tuesdays, or when the shuttle stops for bad weather, plan for walking or postpone the visit if the hills are not suitable for your group.

4. Pair Taejongdae with one nearby stop

Rather than packing several far-apart attractions into one afternoon, consider a nearby coastal or village stop only after checking transport time and return options. Gamji area seafood restaurants can be an option for a meal, but opening hours and availability vary; confirm before building the whole day around a particular restaurant.

5. Before you go

  • Check the BTO shuttle notice and weather conditions on the day.
  • Remember that the free shuttle normally runs Wednesday to Sunday, not Monday or Tuesday.
  • Collect a free ticket for a specific departure at the ticket office (09:10–17:30).
  • Wear shoes suitable for slopes and uneven paths.
  • Allow time for the shuttle, walking and the return journey.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Danubi train running?

No. Busan Tourism Organization currently lists it as suspended for safety inspection. A free circular shuttle operates Wednesday to Sunday, 09:30–17:30.

Is the Taejongdae shuttle free?

Yes. Collect a free ticket for a specific departure at the Danubi ticket office, 09:10–17:30. Tickets are first come, first served.

Does the shuttle run on Monday or Tuesday?

No. It normally does not run on Mondays or Tuesdays. Bad weather can also stop service.
