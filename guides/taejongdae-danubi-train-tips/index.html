YEONGDO · COASTAL WALKS

Taejongdae Danubi Train: Hours, Access and Local Tips

The Danubi train normally runs Tuesday–Sunday and closes on Mondays. Seasonal hours vary, and severe weather can suspend service; check the official timetable before visiting.

YEONGDO · BUSAN5 MIN READPRACTICAL LOCAL TIPS

Quick answer: The Danubi train normally operates Tuesday–Sunday and closes on Mondays. Off-season hours (November–February) are 09:20–17:30; the timetable changes by season, and severe weather can suspend service. Check the official timetable and same-day notice before travelling.

1. Understand the terrain first

Taejongdae is a large coastal park with slopes and walking sections. The Danubi train normally operates on a seasonal timetable, closes on Mondays and may stop in severe weather. Check the official timetable before planning your visit. Visitors with limited mobility, strollers or tight schedules should not assume every viewpoint is step-free or directly beside a stop.

2. Check the Danubi train’s seasonal hours

Visit Busan lists the Danubi train at 09:20–19:30 in summer (June–August) and 09:20–17:30 in the off-season (November–February). Busan Metropolitan City’s park FAQ lists 09:20–18:30 for the fall season (September–October). The train is normally closed on Mondays, and heavy rain or other severe weather can stop service. Ticket sales may close 2–3 hours before the final departure, so check the official timetable and same-day notice before travelling.

3. If the train is not operating

  • Walk only if the slopes and distance suit your group. The full park loop is about 3.8 km; wear suitable shoes and allow extra time.
  • Check local bus access before setting out. City buses reach the park entrance area, but they do not serve every viewpoint inside the park.
  • Do not assume a taxi can reach every viewpoint. Check the operator’s vehicle-access notice and the final walking distance.

If the train is closed on Monday or stops for severe weather, check whether the park’s walking route suits your group before continuing or rescheduling.

4. Pair Taejongdae with one nearby stop

Rather than packing several far-apart attractions into one afternoon, consider a nearby coastal or village stop only after checking transport time and return options. Gamji area seafood restaurants can be an option for a meal, but opening hours and availability vary; confirm before building the whole day around a particular restaurant.

5. Before you go

  • Check the official Danubi train timetable and weather notice on the day.
  • The train is normally closed on Mondays; seasonal hours vary.
  • Check current fares and ticket-office closing times before visiting.
  • Wear shoes suitable for slopes and uneven paths.
  • Allow time for the train, walking and the return journey.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Danubi train running?

Yes. The Danubi train normally operates Tuesday–Sunday and closes on Mondays. Off-season hours (November–February) are 09:20–17:30; severe weather can suspend service. Check the official timetable before visiting.

Do I need a ticket for the Danubi train?

The Danubi train is a paid attraction. Check the current fare and ticket-office hours with the official operator before visiting.

Does the Danubi train run on Mondays?

No. The train is normally closed on Mondays. Heavy rain and other severe weather can also stop service.
