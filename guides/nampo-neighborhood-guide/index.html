Nampo-dong Neighborhood Guide: Markets, Street Food and the Old Downtown

Nampo-dong is Busan’s old downtown, with markets, shopping streets and the harbor close together. Use this page to plan the area, then open the detailed guide for each stop.

Quick summary

  • Best for markets, street snacks, shopping streets and Busan’s old downtown.
  • Nampo and Jagalchi stations are on Metro Line 1, a short ride from Busan Station.
  • The trade-off is distance from the east-coast beaches, so pair it with Yeongdo, Songdo or Gamcheon rather than Haeundae.

Is Nampo-dong the right area for you?

Choose Nampo-dong if markets, street food and older city streets matter more to you than the beach. It is also a practical first stop after arriving at Busan Station.

Many visitors stay in Seomyeon and come here on Line 1. Our where-to-stay guide covers Nampo and the Busan Station area as bases.

Getting there and around

  • From Busan Station (KTX): Metro Line 1 runs south to Nampo and Jagalchi stations. If you have time and light bags, try the Busan Station to Nampo walking route.
  • From Gimhae Airport: a common metro route is the light rail to Sasang, Line 2 to Seomyeon, then Line 1 south. With heavy bags or a late flight, compare a taxi in the late-arrival guide.
  • Getting around: the area is walkable, but side streets are hilly in places. A transit card covers the metro and buses.

What to eat

  • Jagalchi Market: confirm the type, weight, price and cooking fee before you order seafood.
  • Bupyeong Kkangtong Market: fried pork galbi, market snacks and the night market.
  • BIFF Square snacks such as ssiat hotteok and fish cakes.
  • See these stops together on the Busan map, filtered to Nampo.

See and do nearby

  • Kkangtong Market bath and sauna shopping for local everyday goods.
  • Taejongdae on Yeongdo, with the Danubi train, and MOMOS Coffee’s Yeongdo roastery.
  • Songdo Beach and Gamcheon Culture Village to the west.

A simple Nampo half day

  1. Morning: walk from Busan Station through Choryang, or take Line 1 to Nampo.
  2. Lunch: Jagalchi Market, after checking prices.
  3. Afternoon: BIFF Square snacks and the shopping streets.
  4. Evening: Kkangtong night market. Check the day’s opening times locally.

Leave time for hills and crowds, and keep one indoor stop in reserve for rain.

Common questions

How do I get from Busan Station to Nampo-dong?

Take Metro Line 1 south to Nampo or Jagalchi Station. With time and light bags, you can also walk via Choryang; our walking route guide has the details.

Is Nampo-dong a good place to stay?

It suits travelers focused on markets and the old downtown. If your plans are spread across the city, compare Seomyeon; for the beach, compare Haeundae or Gwangalli.
