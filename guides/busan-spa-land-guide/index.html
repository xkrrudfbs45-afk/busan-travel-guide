Busan Spa Land Guide: How to Visit Shinsegae Centum City

Want to try a Korean jjimjilbang without guessing how it works? This practical guide covers the location, tickets, time limits, luggage storage and what first-time visitors should expect at Spa Land.

FIRST-TIMER FRIENDLYTRANSPORT & TICKETSOFFICIAL RULES CHECKED 3 NOV 2026

Quick answer: Spa Land is inside Shinsegae Department Store Centum City, on the department store's first floor. It is directly connected to Centum City Station on Busan Metro Line 2. The official Korean page currently lists adult admission at KRW 26,000 and opening hours of 09:00–22:00, with last admission at 21:00. Check the official Spa Land page before you go.

1. Where is Spa Land?

Spa Land is located on the first floor of the department store at Shinsegae Centum City in Haeundae-gu, eastern Busan. Take Busan Metro Line 2 to Centum City Station. The station connects to the shopping complex; if you approach from street level, Busan tourism guidance points visitors toward Exit 12, followed by a short walk. Once inside, follow the department-store signs for “Spa Land” rather than searching for a separate building. See the Visit Busan attraction listing for additional directions.

Address: 35 Centumnam-daero, Haeundae-gu, Busan (Shinsegae Department Store Centum City). If you are arriving by taxi, showing the driver “신세계백화점 센텀시티점 스파랜드” can help clarify the destination.

Official directions and facility details: Shinsegae Spa Land — Centum City.

2. Opening hours and admission price

The official Shinsegae page currently lists the following. Prices and operating hours can change, so check the official page again on the day of your visit, especially on holidays.

  • Opening hours: 09:00–22:00
  • Last admission: 21:00
  • Adult admission: KRW 26,000
  • Student admission: KRW 21,000
  • Included time: Up to 4 hours
  • Overtime: KRW 5,000 per additional hour
  • Possible extended stay: Up to 6 hours if you spend at least KRW 10,000 inside Spa Land, excluding the admission fee, according to the current official rules.
  • Parking: Shinsegae lists 4 hours free, or 6 hours when qualifying in-facility spending reaches KRW 10,000, excluding admission.

The official page says elementary-school-age children and older may enter; elementary school students must be accompanied by a guardian of the same gender. Confirm age and student-rate eligibility with the facility if you are visiting with children.

3. How to enter: a simple step-by-step

  1. Find the Spa Land entrance. Follow the signs inside Shinsegae Department Store Centum City to the first-floor facility.
  2. Buy or validate your admission. Follow the ticket counter's current instructions. Keep your ticket or locker key safe throughout the visit.
  3. Store large luggage before entering. Suitcases cannot be taken into the internal locker room; use the luggage lockers near the ticket booth if needed.
  4. Use the changing and bathing areas. The bathing areas are separated by gender. Follow the posted shower and bathing instructions before entering the pools.
  5. Change for the shared sauna area. Use the facility's spa clothes in the shared jjimjilbang rooms and lounge areas. Check staff instructions about towels and clothing when you enter.
  6. Keep track of your time. The standard admission period is four hours. Allow time to change and exit before your permitted stay ends.

If this is your first jjimjilbang, the main cultural detail to know is that the bathing area and shared sauna area work differently: bathing is typically done without swimwear in the gender-separated bath area, while spa clothes are worn in shared rooms. If you are uncomfortable or unsure, ask staff before entering. Keep phones and cameras away from bathing and changing areas, and follow all posted privacy rules.

4. Suitcases and luggage storage

Travellers arriving before hotel check-in may be tempted to visit with their luggage, but Spa Land restricts large suitcases in the internal locker room. The official page currently lists a separate luggage-storage fee:

  • First 5 hours: KRW 3,000
  • Each additional hour: KRW 1,000

These are separate from the admission fee. If you have several large bags or unusually sized luggage, confirm storage availability before making Spa Land your first stop after arriving in Busan.

5. What should you bring?

  • Bring: A phone and any essentials you need during the visit; keep valuables secured and follow the facility's locker instructions.
  • Check on arrival: Whether towels and spa clothes are included with your current ticket and where to collect them.
  • Leave time for: Changing, showering, moving between rooms and cooling down between hot rooms.
  • For longer stays: Plan food and drink breaks, and check which items can be brought into the facility.

You do not need to plan a complicated schedule. For a first visit, choose a few sauna rooms, take breaks between hotter rooms and leave enough time to enjoy the baths without rushing.

6. How long should you stay?

For a first visit, around two to four hours is a practical planning window, depending on whether you want to use the baths, try several themed sauna rooms, eat or simply rest. The standard admission period is four hours; the facility's current terms specify an overtime charge and a possible extension with qualifying in-facility spending.

Spa Land is not an overnight jjimjilbang: its listed closing time is 22:00. Do not plan to sleep there overnight or use it as a substitute for a hotel.

7. What can you combine it with?

Because Spa Land is inside a large shopping complex connected to the metro, it works well as a half-day indoor stop on a rainy, cold or very hot day. You can browse the department store or plan a meal before or after your visit. If you are heading to the coast afterwards, check the route and travel time rather than assuming every Busan attraction is close by.

Common questions

Where is Spa Land in Busan?

Spa Land is on the first floor of Shinsegae Department Store at Centum City. Centum City Station on Busan Metro Line 2 is directly connected to the complex.

How much does Spa Land cost?

The official Shinsegae page currently lists admission at KRW 26,000 for adults and KRW 21,000 for students. The base admission includes up to four hours; check the official page for the latest rules before visiting.

Can I bring a suitcase into Spa Land?

Large suitcases are not allowed in the internal locker room. The official page lists luggage storage at KRW 3,000 for the first five hours and KRW 1,000 for each additional hour.

What should first-time visitors know about a Korean jjimjilbang?

Bathing areas are separated by gender and are generally used without swimwear; shared sauna and lounge areas are used in the facility's spa clothes. Follow posted instructions and ask staff if unsure.
