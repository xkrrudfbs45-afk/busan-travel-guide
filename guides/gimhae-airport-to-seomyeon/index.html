AIRPORT & TRANSPORT

Gimhae Airport to Seomyeon: Best Routes for Your Arrival

Compare public transport and taxi options for one of Busan’s most practical central bases, with a plan for late flights and luggage.

BUSAN, SOUTH KOREA4 MIN READCHECK LIVE ROUTES BEFORE TRAVEL

Quick summary

  • Public transport: compare the light rail to Sasang and transfer to Metro Line 2.
  • Heavy luggage or a group: compare a taxi’s convenience with the full public-transport journey.
  • Late arrival: check the final departure after allowing time for immigration and baggage collection.

Quick answer: A common public-transport route is the Busan–Gimhae Light Rail Transit to Sasang, then Metro Line 2 toward Seomyeon. A taxi can simplify the trip if you have heavy bags, several travellers or a late arrival. Check current services before setting out.

Option 1: Light rail + Metro Line 2

Follow airport signs to the Busan–Gimhae Light Rail Transit station and ride to Sasang. Transfer to Busan Metro Line 2 in the direction of Seomyeon and continue to the station that best fits your accommodation.

Seomyeon is a busy commercial district rather than one small hotel zone. Check the walking route from the station to your exact address; a hotel described as “Seomyeon” may still involve a longer walk, an uphill section or another short ride.

Allow time for finding the station, buying or topping up a transport card, changing lines and navigating exits with luggage. Our Busan transit card guide explains the basics before your first ride.

Option 2: Taxi

A taxi can be easier after a long flight, especially with multiple suitcases, children or a group. The fare and journey time depend on traffic, your exact destination and any applicable surcharges. Use the official taxi queue, have your hotel address ready in Korean if possible, and confirm the fare method before the journey begins.

If comparing a taxi with a pre-booked transfer, check the pickup point, waiting-time policy, flight-delay procedure, luggage capacity, cancellation terms and total price.

Option 3: Bus or pre-booked transfer

Consider a bus only after confirming that a currently operating service reaches a convenient stop near your accommodation. Do not rely on an old route listing: services, stops and operating hours can change. A pre-booked transfer may suit a late arrival or a group, but compare the complete price and pickup instructions.

What the airport’s official page lists (checked Nov 5, 2026)

  • Limousine bus to Seomyeon/Bujeon: Platform 3, 1st floor of the international and domestic terminals. Fare KRW 6,000, credit card only. First departure 07:20, last 21:30, roughly every 70 minutes.
  • Stops: International Terminal, Domestic Terminal, Jurye Station, Dongeui University Station, Seomyeon Station (Lotte Hotel/Department Store), Bujeon Market and Bujeon Station.
  • Rail: the page notes that the light rail connects to Metro Line 2 at Sasang Station and to Line 3 at Daejeo Station.

Timetables and fares can change without notice. Check the Gimhae International Airport public transportation page (Korea Airports Corporation) before you travel.

If your flight arrives late

Your landing time is not the time you reach the platform. Immigration, baggage collection and the walk to the station can take longer than expected. Check the final rail departures for your travel date and keep a backup plan if your flight is delayed.

  • Save your accommodation’s full address and check-in instructions.
  • Check the last light-rail and metro services for your actual arrival date.
  • Confirm whether your hotel accepts late check-in.
  • Compare the taxi queue with a pre-booked transfer if public transport will not work.

Routes, timetables and fares can change. Verify the latest information with Visit Busan’s official transportation guide and the relevant operator before departure.

Common questions

What is the public transport route from Gimhae Airport to Seomyeon?

A common route is the Busan–Gimhae Light Rail Transit to Sasang, followed by Busan Metro Line 2 toward Seomyeon. Check current service information and the route to your exact accommodation before travelling.

Is a taxi worth it from Gimhae Airport to Seomyeon?

A taxi may be more convenient with heavy luggage, several travellers or a late arrival, but traffic affects journey time and the fare is usually higher than public transport.

Can I take the metro after a late flight?

Allow for immigration, baggage collection and walking to the station, then check the last services for your travel date and prepare a backup option.
