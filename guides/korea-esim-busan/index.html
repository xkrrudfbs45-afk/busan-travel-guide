Korea eSIM for Busan: Compare Mobile Data Options

You may need mobile data as soon as you land at Gimhae Airport—for maps, translation, ride-hailing and contacting your accommodation. Before choosing a plan, check whether your phone supports eSIM and is carrier-unlocked, then compare the alternatives.

Start with device compatibility

An eSIM is a digital SIM profile, but not every phone or carrier supports it. Check the exact model's specifications and carrier-lock status before paying. If your phone is not compatible, compare a physical SIM, pocket Wi-Fi or roaming instead.

There is no single best option for every traveler. A solo traveler with a compatible phone may prefer an eSIM; a group sharing several devices may find pocket Wi-Fi practical; someone who needs their existing number may prefer roaming. Compare the total cost and conditions for your dates.

When you will need data after landing at Gimhae

  • Leaving arrivals: checking light-rail and airport-bus times, or comparing a taxi with a pre-booked car. See Gimhae Airport to Haeundae and Gimhae Airport to Seomyeon.
  • Late arrivals: the last light-rail and metro services run close to midnight. If you switch to a taxi or a pre-booked car, you need data to check the route and contact the driver. See the late-arrival guide.
  • Around check-in: messaging your hotel with your arrival time, or showing a driver the hotel's Korean address.

Install the eSIM before you fly, and also keep screenshots of your hotel's Korean address and transport plan, so you can start moving even if the data setup is not finished when you land.
