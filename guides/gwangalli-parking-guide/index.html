Parking at Gwangalli Beach: Public Lots and Seasonal Rates

A practical guide to the official beach-side public lot, seasonal fees and nearby Millak-area alternatives—without assuming a space will be available.

GO2BUSAN LOCAL GUIDETRANSPORTUpdated 12 November 2026

Quick answer: Gwangalli Beach Public Parking Lot is listed with 54 spaces and 24-hour operation, but it does not offer daily or monthly parking passes. Its hourly rate changes by season: ₩500 per 10 minutes in peak-season periods and weekends/holidays, or ₩300 per 10 minutes on off-season weekdays.

Gwangalli Beach Public Parking Lot

  • Address: 75 Namcheonbadaro 33beon-gil, Suyeong-gu, Busan (Gwangalli-dong 198-3).
  • Listed capacity: 54 spaces. This is the lot's total size, not the number of spaces currently available.
  • Operating hours: 24 hours, year-round, according to Suyeong-gu's public parking list.
  • Daily/monthly pass: Not offered for this lot in the district's listing.
  • Contact: Suyeong-gu traffic administration, 051-610-4522 for the public-lot information page; the district's notice lists 051-610-4552 for parking enquiries.

Check driving restrictions before you go

Suyeong-gu published a notice in April 2026 adding Gwangalli Beach Public Parking Lot to a vehicle-number rotation scheme during the resource-security response. The notice ties the end of the measure to the crisis status, so this guide does not assume whether the restriction is still active. Check the district's latest notice before driving, especially on weekdays.

Practical parking tips

  • Use the full Korean lot name, 광안리해수욕장 공영주차장, when searching in a Korean map app.
  • Check the current seasonal rate and any vehicle-number restriction before entering.
  • Do not assume the lot has a daily cap or that a listed space is currently empty.
  • For a long visit, compare the hourly total with a different public lot or public transport.
  • Keep the parking receipt and confirm the payment kiosk's instructions before leaving.
