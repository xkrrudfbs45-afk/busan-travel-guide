BTS Busan Travel Guide: Jimin, Jung Kook & ARMY Spots

Follow BTS-related stories around Busan while discovering colorful hillside streets, ocean sunsets and the local food scene—with practical advice from a Busan-based guide.

BUSAN, SOUTH KOREA6 MIN READCHECK DETAILS BEFORE TRAVEL

Plan smart: BTS-related locations are spread across the city. Choose a small cluster of stops, check current access and opening information, and leave time to enjoy Busan beyond the fan photo spots.

Why Busan matters to ARMY

Busan is the hometown of BTS members Jimin and Jung Kook, and official tourism guides highlight several places associated with BTS memories, visits or fan-created tributes. Connections vary by location: a mural or public viewpoint is different from a private business or an active school. Treat each stop according to its actual access rules.

For a useful official starting point, read the Korea Tourism Organization's BTS one-day Busan tour and its updated Busan guide for ARMY. Recheck details before travel because business status and event information can change.

Fan-friendly places to consider

1. Gamcheon Culture Village — murals and colorful lanes

The hillside village is known for its brightly painted houses, viewpoints, small shops and maze-like alleys. BTS fans also seek out murals celebrating Jimin and Jung Kook. Visit with time to explore the wider village rather than treating it only as a photo stop.

2. Dadaepo Beach — a sunset walk

Dadaepo is associated with a Jimin vlog and a sunset walk. Its broad beach and walking paths make it a good choice for a slower afternoon. Sunset times vary by season, so check the local forecast and leave enough time to get back to your accommodation.

Open Dadaepo Beach in Google Maps

3. Oryukdo Skywalk — coastal scenery

Oryukdo islets and the nearby coastal paths offer dramatic sea views and are included in official BTS-related tourism recommendations. Check current operating notices for the skywalk and follow on-site safety directions, especially in strong winds or rain.

Open Oryukdo Skywalk in Google Maps

4. Seodong Miro Market — try a local market snack

Official tourism coverage connects the market with Jimin's childhood memories and mentions egg dumplings at Matna Bunsik. Market stalls and menus can change, so treat this as a food lead rather than a guarantee that a particular item will be available. Explore other stalls and be considerate in busy aisles.

Open Seodong Miro Market in Google Maps

5. ZM-ILLENNIAL — verify the café's current status first

The bakery café in Daeyeon-dong, formerly known as MAGNATE, has been associated with Jimin's father. A November 2026 news report says the café announced a plan to close in about a year, without publishing a specific reason. Because this is time-sensitive, do not assume it is open: check the café's own latest announcement before making a special trip.

How to plan the route

  • Western Busan: Combine Gamcheon Culture Village with other nearby old-downtown or market stops, checking travel time between them.
  • Southern coast: Give Dadaepo a separate afternoon and evening if sunset is a priority.
  • Nam-gu coast: Pair Oryukdo with nearby coastal walks; check public transport connections before leaving.
  • Market and café stops: Verify opening days and business status on the day of your visit.

Do not try to visit every location in a single day. Busan is geographically large, and a route that looks short on a list can involve long transfers. Use the Busan public transport guide and three-day itinerary to plan around your accommodation.

Fan etiquette: keep the places welcoming

  • Do not enter schools, private homes or restricted areas without permission. Former schools are working schools, not public attractions.
  • Keep entrances, sidewalks and market aisles clear when taking photos.
  • Ask before photographing staff, residents or other visitors.
  • Do not leave gifts, stickers or messages on walls or property unless the venue explicitly permits it.
  • Follow posted rules at coastal viewpoints and never cross barriers for a photo.

Common questions

Which BTS members are from Busan?

Jimin and Jung Kook are the BTS members associated with Busan as their hometown. Confirm the specific connection for each place before visiting.

Can I visit every BTS spot in one day?

It is possible to visit a few places in a day, but the sites are spread across Busan. Group stops by area and avoid trying to cover the entire city in one route.

Can I visit BTS members' former schools?

Schools are active learning environments, not tourist attractions. Do not enter without permission; respect access restrictions and students' privacy.

Make it a Busan trip, not just a checklist. Leave room for local food, the coast and the neighborhoods between fan spots.
