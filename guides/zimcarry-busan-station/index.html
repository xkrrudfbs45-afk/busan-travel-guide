Travel Hands-Free: Zimcarry Storage and Delivery at Busan Station

A practical checklist for deciding whether to store your bags near Busan Station or arrange a separate luggage-delivery service.

Quick answer: Start by checking the current Zimcarry branch listing and Busan City’s luggage-storage information. Confirm the exact counter, opening hours, bag size rules and whether the service you need is storage or delivery before making plans.

1. Storage and delivery are different services

Luggage storage means you leave your bag at a location and return there to collect it. Luggage delivery means your bag is transported between specified locations, subject to the provider’s service area, booking deadlines and collection arrangements. Availability of one service does not guarantee the other is offered at the same branch.

2. Find the right Busan Station branch

Busan City’s visitor information lists a Zimcarry location around Busan Station, while the operator maintains its own live branch directory. Use both to identify the current location, then check the exact exit, floor or counter details before you set out.

Where exactly can you leave your bags?

Busan City currently lists the Zimcarry counter inside KTX Busan Station, near Exit 5 on the station building’s first floor. The city lists the service as luggage storage and transport and gives 1544-8215 as the contact number. This is a staffed service listing, not confirmation that a self-service locker is beside the counter.

If you prefer a subway locker, Busan City separately lists luggage lockers across Busan Metro Lines 1–4. These are in the subway system, not the same facility as the KTX-station Zimcarry counter. Check the station signs and on-site availability before putting your bags away.

Before you walk: decide whether you need the KTX-station counter or a subway locker, save the exact Korean location name, and confirm today's opening and collection times. Do not assume a KTX ticket or subway fare includes luggage storage.

3. Travelling with a suitcase, stroller or wheelchair

Busan Transportation Corporation’s elevator directory lists two elevators inside and two outside at Busan Station on Metro Line 1. This confirms elevator facilities at the subway station, but it does not identify the best step-free path from every KTX platform or concourse entrance. Follow the station’s elevator and accessible-route signs, and ask station staff if a lift is out of service or the route is unclear.

4. What to confirm before booking

  • Service type: Is it storage, same-day delivery or delivery to a hotel/airport?
  • Drop-off and collection: Confirm the date, cut-off time, opening hours and what happens if your train is delayed.
  • Bag details: Check dimensions, weight limits and whether strollers or oversized items are accepted.
  • Destination eligibility: For delivery, verify the exact hotel or terminal, delivery window and whether someone must receive the bag.
  • Price and payment: Rates may vary by size, duration, branch and service. Review the current quote and cancellation terms.
  • Confirmation: Save the booking details and follow the operator’s instructions for tagging and collection.

6. Plan the route around your bags

Choose the service location based on where you will be, not only on the headline price. If you store bags at Busan Station, include the return trip in your day. If you arrange delivery, keep essentials and valuables with you and allow for the provider’s stated delivery window.

Compare a luggage-delivery option

If your plan involves moving between Busan Station, Gimhae Airport, the international ferry terminal or a hotel, compare the service area, luggage limits, delivery window and booking deadline before choosing a provider.
