10 Practical Busan Travel Tips Locals Want You to Know

Small local details can save time, reduce confusion and help you enjoy more of Busan. Use these tips as a starting point, then check current details before you go.

10 tips for a smoother Busan trip

1. Save the Korean name of each place

English search can be inconsistent across map apps. Save the Korean name and address of your hotel, restaurant and attraction before leaving. Open the route in Naver Map or KakaoMap and keep a screenshot as a backup. These apps can show different results, so compare when a route looks odd.

2. Plan door to door, not station to station

Busan is spread along a long coastline, with hills and destinations that may sit well beyond the nearest metro station. Check the walking segment, slope, bus connection and final approach—not only the train time. A short distance on the map does not always mean an easy walk.

Open KakaoMap ↗

3. Compare taxi estimates before you ride

Check the estimated fare, journey time and route in a map or taxi app before getting in. If you want the meter used, you can say: “미터기로 가주세요” (Please use the meter). Fares can change with traffic, distance and applicable surcharges; avoid relying on old screenshots or fixed estimates.

Check available taxi options ↗

4. Keep a payment backup

Some visitors report problems registering an overseas card in a taxi app. App support and payment options can change, so check the current app instructions before your trip and keep a second way to pay. Do not assume one workaround works for every account or card.

Check Kakao Mobility ↗

5. Check restaurant hours on the day

Opening hours, break times, last order and weekly closing days can differ from old blog posts. Check a current map listing and the restaurant’s own recent notice when available. If the trip is built around one famous restaurant, prepare a nearby alternative.

Check current listings ↗

6. Treat a queue as part of the itinerary

For a popular restaurant, check whether it uses on-site waiting, a remote queue or reservations—do not assume every shop uses the same system. If the queue is confirmed, use the wait to explore a nearby market or take a short walk, but stay close enough to return when called.

7. Agree on the full seafood bill before ordering

At a seafood market, confirm the seafood type, weight or quantity, price, preparation method, table-setting fee and any other charges before agreeing to the order. Not every shop uses the same pricing structure. Our detailed Jagalchi guide explains the questions to ask.

Read the Jagalchi Market guide ↗

8. Group your day by neighborhood

Avoid zigzagging between the west, central city and east coast several times in one day. Group nearby places together and leave room for food, transfers and rest. Busan’s coastline makes the order of stops matter.

See the three-day itinerary ↗

9. Separate transit cards from attraction passes

A rechargeable transport card and a sightseeing pass serve different purposes. Check what each card pays for, where it is accepted, how its balance works and whether specific attractions are included. Do not buy a pass before comparing it with the places you actually plan to visit.

Read the transport guide ↗

10. Save a rainy-day and late-night backup

Coastal weather, changing operating hours and the last train can affect a plan. Before heading to a distant attraction, check the latest operating notice and your return route. Keep one indoor option and one practical way back to your accommodation.
