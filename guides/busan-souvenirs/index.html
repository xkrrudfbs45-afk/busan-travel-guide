What to Buy in Busan: Official Souvenir Shops & Local Gift Ideas

Skip generic airport gifts and look for products selected to represent Busan. The city runs an official souvenir program and lists dedicated shops where travelers can browse locally developed gifts.

Busan, South KoreaIndependent travel guideUpdated November 2026

Before you go: The city updates its selected makers and retail channels by edition. Product availability, pop-up dates and shop hours can change; confirm the latest city listing before visiting.

Start with the city's Top 10 Busan Souvenirs

Busan Metropolitan City runs a program that selects tourism souvenirs through an open call and supports the makers in developing products that represent the city's culture and identity. The 2026 fourth edition selected ten companies, with products being introduced through official souvenir shops and other retail channels in phases. The selected list includes Busan-linked food, design and lifestyle makers; check the city's current catalogue for the actual products and participating brands.

Where to shop: three listed souvenir stores

The city lists Dongbaek Sanghoe at Busan Eurasia Platform near Busan Station (Room 106, 210 Jungang-daero, Dong-gu); Yeogageoga at 1F, 219 Gwanganhaebyeon-ro in Gwangalli; and Haeundae Gift Shop at 1F, 264 Haeundaehaebyeon-ro, inside the Haeundae Tourist Information Center. These locations make it possible to shop near a transport hub or combine souvenir browsing with a beach visit. Confirm current opening hours before setting out.

What makes a good Busan gift?

Look for an item with a clear connection to the city: a local food product, a design object inspired by Busan's coastline or port, or a practical item made by a selected local brand. If you are buying food, check the expiry date, storage requirements and airline restrictions. For fragile goods, ask the shop about protective packaging.

A simple shopping plan

If you are leaving by KTX, browse Dongbaek Sanghoe near Busan Station near the end of your trip. If you are spending the day at Gwangalli, visit Yeogageoga as part of the beach walk. If your final day is in Haeundae, the gift shop at the tourist information center is a convenient option. The city has also announced pop-up and vending-machine channels in some periods; these are not guaranteed permanent locations, so check the current official notice.

Before you pay

Check whether the item is food, liquid or fragile; confirm carry-on and checked-baggage rules with your airline. Keep the receipt for higher-value purchases, and ask whether the product is sealed for travel. Do not assume every souvenir shop carries all ten selected products.
