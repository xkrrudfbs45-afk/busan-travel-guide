Yeongdo, Busan: A One-Day Coastal Walk from Huinnyeoul to Taejongdae

Yeongdo is more than a photo stop. Pair a hillside seaside village with a coastal path and the cliffs of Taejongdae for a day that shows a quieter, maritime side of Busan.

Busan, South KoreaIndependent travel guideUpdated November 2026

Before you go: Yeongdo's sights are spread across the island. This route is designed as a sequence of separate stops connected by local transport—not one continuous short walk.

Start with the island's maritime story

If you have time before heading west, Kangkangee Arts Village near the old ship-repair area offers a different introduction to Yeongdo's working waterfront. Treat it as an optional first stop rather than part of the coastal walking trail; check current access and exhibition information before going.

Huinnyeoul Culture Village and the coastal path

Huinnyeoul Culture Village sits along a steep coastal slope and is known for narrow lanes, sea-facing viewpoints and small cultural spaces. The Korea Tourism Organization's coastal walking route links the Jeoryeonghaean Coastal Walking Trail, Huinnyeoul Culture Village and Haneul Observatory. The route is approximately 5.37 km in total before adding Taejongdae, so allow time for stairs, slopes and photo stops. This is a residential neighbourhood: keep noise low, do not block doorways, and follow signs around private homes.

Haneul Observatory: a sea-facing pause

Haneul Observatory is included in the official coastal route and offers an open view toward the sea. Use it as a rest stop between the village lanes and the next section of the coast. Check access notices and weather, especially on windy or rainy days.

Finish at Taejongdae

Taejongdae is a coastal park and national geopark known for cliffs and ocean views. The Danubi Train can help visitors reach major viewpoints within the park; operating status, waiting times and ticket information should be checked on the official site before arrival. Busan Geopark lists local bus routes 66, 88 and 101 from Busan Station to Taejongdae, but route details can change—confirm on the day.

A practical one-day schedule

Morning: Kangkangee Arts Village (optional) or go directly to Huinnyeoul. Late morning: walk through Huinnyeoul and follow the coastal trail toward Haneul Observatory. Lunch: choose a currently open restaurant or café in the area; do not assume every small shop opens daily. Afternoon: travel to Taejongdae and use the Danubi Train or walk according to your energy and the park's operating conditions. Leave time to return before the evening transport becomes less frequent.

If you have extra time

The National Maritime Museum is another Yeongdo stop, especially for families or visitors interested in Korea's maritime history. It is not on the coastal walking route, so add it only if opening hours and travel time fit your day.
