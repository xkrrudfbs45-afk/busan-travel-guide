Catchtable Global Busan Guide: Restaurant Reservations Without a Korean Number

A practical guide to reservations and waiting lists for visitors to Busan.

BUSAN TRAVEL4 MIN READCHECK BEFORE BOOKING

Quick answer: Use Catchtable Global for participating restaurants. Check whether the restaurant offers a reservation, remote waitlist or on-site waiting; these are different services.

1. Use the Global version

Catchtable Global is designed for international visitors. The Korea Tourism Organization says users can register with Google, Apple or email without a Korean phone number. It supports 20 languages, including English, Japanese and Traditional Chinese.

2. Check the restaurant’s available option

On a restaurant page, look for Reserve, Join Remote Waitlist or instructions for on-site waiting. Reserve lets you choose an available time. Remote waitlist lets you join before arriving. If only on-site waiting is offered, you need to register at the restaurant.

3. Know when a deposit applies

Some reservation-only restaurants require a deposit, payable with an overseas-issued card through Catchtable Global. VISITKOREA says remote and on-site waitlisting do not require a deposit. Deposit amounts, cancellation deadlines and no-show rules vary by restaurant; read the terms before confirming.

4. Before you go

Confirm the branch, date, time and party size. Check whether you have a confirmed reservation or only a waitlist entry. Keep app and email notifications on, monitor your place in line and arrive when your turn approaches.

5. Not every restaurant participates

Catchtable only works for participating restaurants and enabled features. Some traditional Busan restaurants are walk-in only or use another system. If there is no booking or waitlist button, do not assume online reservations are available.
