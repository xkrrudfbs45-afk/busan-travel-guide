G-STAR 2026 in Busan: Tickets, Hours & How to Get to BEXCO

Going to Korea’s major game exhibition? The biggest planning mistake is arriving without a ticket: public BTC admission is online-only, with no ticket sales at the venue.

BUSAN LOCAL GUIDEUPDATED 20 NOV 2026

At a glance: November 19–22, 2026 · BEXCO Exhibition Halls 1 & 2 · Adult BTC ticket ₩18,000 · Youth ticket ₩8,000 · Online booking only.

Dates and what the ticket covers

The public exhibition (BTC) runs from Thursday, November 19 to Sunday, November 22, 2026, at BEXCO in Haeundae. The business exhibition (BTB) runs November 19–21 and is a separate pass category for trade visitors. A standard BTC ticket is for the public exhibition only; it does not include BTB or a G-CON conference pass.

What to look out for at G-STAR 2026

Beyond the main publisher booths, Visit Busan highlights three programme strands for this year: global game showcases, G-STAR Indie Showcase 2.0: GALAXY, and the Overwatch Champions Series (OWCS) Asia Finals. These are useful starting points if you want more than hands-on demos. The public event listing does not provide a complete session timetable, so check the official G-STAR notices and on-site screens for exact stages and times.

BTC is the public exhibition. BTB is a separate business programme running November 19–21, and G-CON uses its own conference pass. Check your ticket category before joining a queue.

Opening-day snapshot: how large is G-STAR this year?

At the November 19 opening, the organizer’s provisional figures were 1,237 participating companies from 41 countries and 3,445 booths. The public BTC area occupies all of Exhibition Hall 1 and the first floor of Exhibition Hall 2. The second hall’s first floor includes the Global Game Showcase, with titles from overseas publishers including Sega, Capcom, Koei Tecmo Games, NetEase Games and CD Projekt Red. The indie showcase brings together 108 developers from 28 countries. Figures are provisional and may be updated by the organizer.

For a first visit, decide which hall matters most to you before entering: Hall 1 is the main public exhibition, while Hall 2 combines the global showcase and organizer-led programmes. Check the official BTC floor plan for booth locations. The opening-day figures were reported from the organizer’s announcement by The Elec.

  • Online advance booking only: there are no BTC ticket sales at BEXCO.
  • Buy by 24:00 the day before: the deadline is Korea Standard Time and applies to the date you plan to visit.
  • One date per ticket: a ticket is valid only on its booked day.
  • Purchase limit: up to two tickets per person per day.
  • Wristband exchange: collect an admission wristband at the on-site exchange desk before entering.

Book through the official G-STAR BTC ticket page. The organizer’s checkout notice says Ticketlink receives identifying details including name, email, user ID and phone number for the ticket transaction. If you are booking from overseas, check the current Ticketlink account and payment requirements before relying on a last-minute purchase. Do not assume a ticket can be bought at the door.

Free admission: the organizer lists people with disabilities (all grades; in-person application with a welfare card), persons of national merit (all grades; in person), and preschool children born in 2019 or later. Free-admission visitors must collect an admission ticket at the Information Center, which opens one hour before the exhibition starts. These visitors should not assume they can walk straight into the hall.

Youth ticket holders may be asked to show proof of age or student status. The organizer lists a passport among acceptable examples, so bring the document used for your booking.

Sales window: the official BTC page currently lists sales through November 21, 2026 at 23:59 KST. Each visit date still has its own previous-day 24:00 cutoff. Cancellation fees are calculated against the visit date, not the booking date; check the official notice before cancelling.

Opening hours and last entry

  • November 19: doors open at 11:00.
  • November 20–22: the exhibition is scheduled for 10:00–18:00.
  • Ticket verification closes: 17:00.
  • Last admission: 17:30.

Ticket verification closes earlier than the listed last-admission time, so plan to complete ticket checks by 17:00 rather than aiming for 17:30. Allow extra time for the wristband exchange and security checks, and check the organizer’s latest notice in case the daily schedule changes.

Should you arrive at 10:00 or 12:00? Regular BTC tickets are date-specific, not separate 10:00 or 12:00 timed-entry sessions. Arrive around 10:00 if you want the longest visit and time to explore both halls. A noon arrival can work if your schedule requires it, but it leaves less time before the 17:00 ticket-verification cutoff. There is no official promise that either time avoids queues.

Which BEXCO station exit should you use?

BEXCO has two exhibition centres, so choose the station exit for the hall you are visiting:

  • Exhibition Center 1: Centum City Station on Metro Line 2, Exit 1, about 50 m on foot; BEXCO Station (Busan Museum of Art), Exit 7, about 100 m.
  • Exhibition Center 2: Centum City Station, Exit 1, about 380 m; BEXCO Station (Busan Museum of Art), Exit 5, about 453 m.

From Busan Station, take Metro Line 1 to Seomyeon, transfer to Line 2 and get off at Centum City. Confirm the exact route and service times on the day of travel.

Coming from Gimhae Airport?

By rail (allow about 55 minutes): Take the Busan–Gimhae Light Rail from Airport Station to Sasang, transfer to Metro Line 2 towards Centum City, then follow the signs for BEXCO. This is the straightforward public-transport option, but allow extra time for the transfer and walking inside the station.

By airport limousine: The airport operator currently lists Airport Limousine 1 for the Haeundae–Gijang corridor, with BEXCO on the route. The stop is at Platform 3 on the first floor of the domestic and international terminals. The published fare is KRW 9,500 (credit card only); the listed first and last departures from the airport are 07:10 and 21:40. Timetables and operating stops can change, so confirm the departure for your terminal and travel date before boarding. On a busy G-STAR weekend, leave a generous buffer rather than planning to arrive just before ticket verification closes.

A short checklist before leaving

  • Confirm that your ticket date matches the day you plan to visit.
  • Save the booking confirmation and bring the passport or ID used for age verification if relevant.
  • Know whether you need Exhibition Center 1 or 2 before choosing the station exit.
  • Leave time for the wristband desk; ticket verification closes at 17:00.
  • For food and a break nearby, use the Haeundae neighborhood guide. For transport cards and transfers, see the Busan public transport guide.
