G-STAR 2026 in Busan: Tickets, Hours & How to Get to BEXCO

Going to Korea’s major game exhibition? The biggest planning mistake is arriving without a ticket: public BTC admission is online-only, with no ticket sales at the venue.

BUSAN LOCAL GUIDECHECKED 17 NOV 2026

At a glance: November 19–22, 2026 · BEXCO Exhibition Halls 1 & 2 · Adult BTC ticket ₩18,000 · Youth ticket ₩8,000 · Online booking only.

Dates and what the ticket covers

The public exhibition (BTC) runs from Thursday, November 19 to Sunday, November 22, 2026, at BEXCO in Haeundae. The business exhibition (BTB) runs November 19–21 and is a separate pass category for trade visitors. A standard BTC ticket is for the public exhibition only; it does not include BTB or a G-CON conference pass.

Ticket prices and the rule visitors must not miss

BTC ticketPriceWho it is for
General / adult₩18,000General visitors
Youth₩8,000Born on or after January 1, 2008
  • Online advance booking only: there are no BTC ticket sales at BEXCO.
  • Buy by 24:00 the day before: the deadline is Korea Standard Time and applies to the date you plan to visit.
  • One date per ticket: a ticket is valid only on its booked day.
  • Purchase limit: up to two tickets per person per day.
  • Wristband exchange: collect an admission wristband at the on-site exchange desk before entering.

Book through the official G-STAR BTC ticket page. The organizer’s checkout notice says Ticketlink receives identifying details including name, email, user ID and phone number for the ticket transaction. If you are booking from overseas, check the current Ticketlink account and payment requirements before relying on a last-minute purchase. Do not assume a ticket can be bought at the door.

Youth ticket holders may be asked to show proof of age or student status. The organizer lists a passport among acceptable examples, so bring the document used for your booking.

Opening hours and last entry

  • November 19: doors open at 11:00.
  • November 20–22: the exhibition is scheduled for 10:00–18:00.
  • Ticket verification closes: 17:00.
  • Last admission: 17:30.

Ticket verification closes earlier than the listed last-admission time, so plan to complete ticket checks by 17:00 rather than aiming for 17:30. Allow extra time for the wristband exchange and security checks, and check the organizer’s latest notice in case the daily schedule changes.

Which BEXCO station exit should you use?

BEXCO has two exhibition centres, so choose the station exit for the hall you are visiting:

  • Exhibition Center 1: Centum City Station on Metro Line 2, Exit 1, about 50 m on foot; BEXCO Station (Busan Museum of Art), Exit 7, about 100 m.
  • Exhibition Center 2: Centum City Station, Exit 1, about 380 m; BEXCO Station (Busan Museum of Art), Exit 5, about 453 m.

From Busan Station, take Metro Line 1 to Seomyeon, transfer to Line 2 and get off at Centum City. Confirm the exact route and service times on the day of travel.

A short checklist before leaving

  • Confirm that your ticket date matches the day you plan to visit.
  • Save the booking confirmation and bring the passport or ID used for age verification if relevant.
  • Know whether you need Exhibition Center 1 or 2 before choosing the station exit.
  • Leave time for the wristband desk; ticket verification closes at 17:00.
  • For food and a break nearby, use the Haeundae neighborhood guide. For transport cards and transfers, see the Busan public transport guide.
