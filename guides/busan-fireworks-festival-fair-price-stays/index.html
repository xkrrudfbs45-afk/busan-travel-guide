2026 Busan Fireworks Festival: 34 Stays Selected by the City (Archive)

Busan City published a list of 34 accommodation businesses that met its fair-pricing criteria for the 2026 festival period. The list period ended on November 8, 2026. This is a historical record, not a current booking shortlist; room availability and rates may have changed.

Archive status: Historical list from the 2026 festival period; not a current booking recommendation.

Festival date: Saturday, November 7, 2026 (ended)

Area: Gwangalli Beach, with event viewing areas also around Igidae and Dongbaek Island

City list available: November 4–8, 2026

How the city selected the historical list

Busan City reviewed 157 accommodation businesses within 500 metres of the festival venue. It compared advertised prices on five booking platforms from October 26 to November 3. The average listed price for festival night was 94.5% higher than the comparison Saturdays. The city selected 34 businesses, mainly those whose prices had risen by 20% or less, while also considering whether previously announced rates were honoured and whether hygiene-related complaints or legal violations had been recorded.

Important: “Fair-price” is the city’s screening label, not a promise that rooms are cheap, available, unchanged in price, high quality or facing the fireworks. The list does not confirm a sea view or event view.

Five cautions when reading this historical list

  • This was a historical list published from November 4 to 8, 2026; it may not reflect current city criteria.
  • Inclusion does not mean a property currently has rooms available or retains the same rates.
  • “Fair-price” described the city's screening criteria at that time; it is not a guarantee of low prices, quality or official endorsement.
  • For a current stay, check the property's live room type, total price, cancellation terms and check-in rules.
  • For the next fireworks festival, verify the new date, accommodation list, traffic controls and event arrangements when announced.

Where to stay if Gwangalli is full

Official information

Busan City says the 34-business list is published on Visit Busan from November 4 through November 8, under Busan to Visit → Accommodation → Busan Fireworks Festival Fair-Price Accommodations. The page is time-limited; check the official listing for the latest details.
