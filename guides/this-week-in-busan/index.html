THIS WEEK IN BUSAN · 23–29 NOV 2026

This Week in Busan: 6 Things to See, 23–29 November 2026

A short, traveller-friendly pick of exhibitions and events running in Busan this week. Each entry lists the area, opening hours, price and the official page to recheck before you go. This page is updated weekly.

BUSAN, SOUTH KOREAUPDATED WEEKLYChecked 22 November 2026

Before you go: Several venues below close on Mondays. Opening hours, prices and programmes can change; check the official page linked under each entry on the day you visit.

1. Gwangalli M Drone Light Show — Saturday 28 November

The last show of November is a regular drone programme (no laser). Arrive early for a spot on the sand and plan your trip home before the 21:00 show ends.

  • Where: Gwangalli Beach, Suyeong-gu
  • Dates: Sat 28 Nov only (this week)
  • Hours: 19:00 and 21:00 (winter times)
  • Price: Free
  • Official page: www.gwangallimdrone.co.kr
  • Related guide: Drone show viewing tips

2. Jean-Michel Othoniel: In the Labyrinth of Love

The French artist's glass-bead sculptures start outdoors with a golden lotus in the garden, then continue inside the former factory hall. A good pick if you want one internationally known artist rather than a gallery crawl.

  • Where: F1963 (Seokcheon Hall and Moonlight Garden), Suyeong-gu
  • Dates: Until 31 Dec 2026
  • Hours: 10:00–18:00 (last entry 17:00), closed Mondays
  • Price: Adult ₩10,000 · youth ₩8,000 · child ₩6,000
  • Official page: www.busan.go.kr

3. Attack on Titan Exhibition FINAL (Busan)

Around 200 reproduction artworks plus a Colossal Titan space made for the Busan run. Tickets are sold on NOL World, which has an English booking page for international visitors.

  • Where: DUEX Busan, B1, 50 Jungang-daero 666beon-gil, Busanjin-gu
  • Dates: Until 4 Apr 2027
  • Hours: 11:00–18:00 (last entry 17:20)
  • Price: Adult ₩26,000 · youth/child ₩22,000 (ages 7+)
  • Official page: world.nol.com
  • Related guide: Rainy-day ideas in Busan

4. Busan Museum of Art reopening exhibitions

The city art museum reopened in September 2026 after renovation. Shows such as 'Future Museology' and 'Dear All The Yesterdays' make it an easy free stop between Haeundae and Centum.

  • Where: Busan Museum of Art, Haeundae-gu (Centum area)
  • Dates: Until 14 Mar 2027
  • Hours: Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00, closed Mondays
  • Price: Free
  • Official page: art.busan.go.kr

5. The Gardens of Joseon — last three weeks

Around 80 objects on how Joseon-era scholars designed and enjoyed gardens, including national treasures. The English audio guide makes it one of the easier museum visits for non-Korean speakers.

  • Where: Busan Museum, Nam-gu
  • Dates: Until 13 Dec 2026
  • Hours: 9:00–18:00, closed Mondays
  • Price: Free (free audio guide in Korean and English)
  • Official page: www.busan.go.kr

6. 'Gagyageogyeogo!' Hangul exhibition

Marks 100 years since the day that became today's Hangul Day, set up as a playful 'school' with art and hands-on corners, including Busan dialect. Most text is in Korean, so it suits visitors already curious about the alphabet.

  • Where: KT&G Sangsang Madang Busan, Gallery 5F (Seomyeon)
  • Dates: Until 14 Feb 2027
  • Hours: 10:00–19:00 (last entry 18:00)
  • Price: ₩15,000
  • Official page: nol.yanolja.com
  • Related guide: Busan dialect guide

Plan the week around one area

  • Haeundae and Centum: Busan Museum of Art is a short trip from the beach area; combine it with a Haeundae evening.
  • Suyeong and Gwangalli: F1963 and the Saturday drone show sit on the same side of the city.
  • Seomyeon and Busanjin-gu: the Hangul exhibition and the Attack on Titan exhibition suit a rainy afternoon in the city centre.
