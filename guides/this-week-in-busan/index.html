THIS WEEK IN BUSAN · 28 NOV–12 DEC 2026

This Week in Busan: Winter Events & Dec 12 Shows (Nov 28–Dec 12)

A traveler-focused guide to winter lights, exhibitions, a book fair, downtown events and Dec 12 live shows, with dates, prices and official links.

BUSAN, SOUTH KOREAUPDATED WEEKLYCHECKED 28 NOVEMBER 2026

Edition window: Nov 28–Dec 12, 2026. Some exhibitions continue beyond this window. Dates, weather and ticket inventory can change; recheck official information before travelling.

1. Haeundae Light Festival — Nov 28–Jan 10

“Deep Dive Haeundae” brings ocean-themed light displays to the beach and Gunam-ro. The opening ceremony is Nov 28 at 18:00.

Where: Haeundae Beach and Gunam-ro · Lights: 18:00–23:00 · Admission: Free. Official event page · Map

2. Millak Luce Festa — Nov 14–Jan 10

Seasonal lights around Millak Waterside Park, including camellia-themed elements.

Where: Millak Waterside Park · Lights: Daily, 18:00–23:30 · Admission: Free. Official event page · Map

3. Seomyeon Light Festival — Oct 29–Feb 14

Light displays and photo zones around Seomyeon 1st Street.

Where: Seomyeon 1st Street · Lights: 18:00–24:00 · Ticket price is not listed. Official event page · Map

4. Christmas Village Busan — Nov 26–Dec 25

The outdoor plaza at Busan Cinema Center becomes a festive village. It runs Thu–Sun through Dec 20, then daily Dec 21–25. Weekdays are 16:00–22:00; weekends and Dec 24–25 are 11:00–22:00. Weekend tickets use entry slots at 11:00 and 16:00; confirm your slot at checkout.

Ticket range: ₩5,900–₩14,900; under 36 months free. The city notice and ticket page list the current price and session.

Official event site / ticket options · City event listing · Busan City ticket and entry-slot notice · Map

5. Busan International Children's Book Fair — Dec 3–6

BEXCO Hall 2, Exhibition Center 1. Hours: Dec 3–5, 10:00–18:00; Dec 6, 10:00–17:00 (last entry 30 minutes before closing). Stage programmes are free but fair registration is required. Free admission requires consent to marketing information; otherwise ₩10,000. Under-14s free. International stage-program/accessibility inquiries: bicbf@kpa21.or.kr.

Official visitor guide · Programme timetable · Map

6. Gwangbok-ro Street Festa — Dec 5

Holiday lights, live music, a parade-style party and a craft market along the pedestrian street in Nampo-dong. Ticketing is not listed in the city notice.

Where: Gwangbok-ro, Jung-gu · Hours: 16:00–22:00. Official city notice · Map

7. The Gardens of Joseon — through Dec 13

A free exhibition on Joseon garden culture at Busan Museum. Open 09:00–18:00, closed Mondays; an English audio guide is available.

Official event page · Map

8. Jean-Michel Othoniel: In the Labyrinth of Love — through Dec 31

Glass-and-light works at F1963, Seokcheon Hall and Moonlight Garden. Aug 28–Dec 31; 10:00–18:00, closed Mondays. Tickets: adult ₩10,000, youth ₩8,000, child ₩6,000.

Official event page · F1963 · Map

9. Busan MoCA reopens — Dec 11

The museum is closed through Dec 10 while it prepares new exhibitions. Three exhibitions are scheduled from Dec 11: Symbiotic Couture, The Labor of Reading the World and Busan MoCA Collection: New Media and Media. Regular galleries are free; check individual exhibitions for fees. Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00, last admission 17:30; closed Mondays.

Official closure/exhibition notice · Hours and admission · Map

10. The Little Singers of Paris — Dec 11

Busan Concert Hall, 19:30, 120 minutes; primary-school age and older (born in 2019 or earlier). Tickets: R ₩70,000 / S ₩50,000 / A ₩30,000 / student ₩10,000.

Official booking and details · Map

Plan by area

  • Nampo: Dec 5 Street Festa + BIFF Square + Bupyeong Kkangtong Market.
  • Centum: BICBF at BEXCO + Christmas Village at Busan Cinema Center; confirm ticket sessions.
  • Haeundae: light festival as an evening walk after an earlier meal.
  • Suyeong: Millak Luce Festa and F1963 are separate stops; check transfers.
  • Seomyeon: lights after dinner; subway onward.

See the Busan map, Nampo guide, Haeundae guide, Seomyeon guide and 3-day itinerary.

Travel impact: Busan Bridge Marathon on 6 December

Busan Bridge Marathon takes place on Sunday, 6 December, starting near BEXCO. The official district notice lists traffic controls from 05:00 to 14:30 and temporary bus detours from 05:00 to 13:00 across 42 routes (city, village, airport-limousine and city-tour services). Registration is not confirmed as open and no spectator ticket information was found. Use the subway and check the latest Korean notice before travelling.

Organizer notices · Busan City event page · Bus-detour notice (Korean, updated Nov 24)

Dec 12 weekend supplement

Saturday, Dec 12 adds two free outdoor options and five ticketed performances. Ticket inventory and foreign-buyer rules are not confirmed; use the ticket seller’s current terms.

  • Balcony Music Show: Gwangbok-ro, 18:00–21:00, free. Visit Busan schedule.
  • Gwangalli M Drone Light Show: 19:00 and 21:00, about 12 minutes, free. Dec 12 is a second Saturday, so the regular drone-only format is expected (no laser show); theme not confirmed. Weather/technical cancellation is possible. Busan City schedule.
  • Cho Yong-pil & Great Birth: Dec 12, 18:00; Dec 13, 17:00 · BEXCO Exhibition Center 1 · ₩110,000–176,000 · ages 8+ · 130 minutes. YES24 notice.
  • Dynamic Duo: Dec 12, 18:00 · KBS Busan Hall · ₩143,000 · ages 7+. NOL World.
  • Baek Ji-young: Dec 12, 18:00 · Busan Citizens Hall Grand Theater · ₩121,000–165,000 · ages 8+ · 120 minutes. NOL World.
  • Kim Na-young: Dec 12, 18:00 · Sohyang Theater Woori Bank Hall · ₩132,000–154,000 · ages 7+ · 120 minutes. YES24 notice.
  • ON STAGE year-end concert: Dec 12, 18:00 · Geumsarak · ₩10,000 · all ages · 90 minutes. NOL ticket listing.

Ticket prices are published snapshots, not a guarantee of remaining seats. Check age rules, foreign-card/ID restrictions, delivery or pickup requirements, and cancellation fees at the seller before purchase.
