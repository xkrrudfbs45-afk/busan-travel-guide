THIS WEEK IN BUSAN · 23–29 NOV 2026

This Week in Busan: 10 Winter Events (Nov 28–Dec 11)

A traveler-focused selection of winter lights, exhibitions, a book fair, a downtown festival and a concert, with dates, prices and official links.

BUSAN, SOUTH KOREAUPDATED WEEKLYCHECKED 27 NOVEMBER 2026

Edition window: Nov 28–Dec 11, 2026. Some exhibitions continue beyond this window. Dates, weather and ticket inventory can change; recheck official information before travelling.

1. Haeundae Light Festival — Nov 28–Jan 10

“Deep Dive Haeundae” brings ocean-themed light displays to the beach and Gunam-ro. The opening ceremony is Nov 28 at 18:00.

Where: Haeundae Beach and Gunam-ro · Lights: 18:00–23:00 · Admission: Free. Official event page · Map

2. Millak Luce Festa — Nov 14–Jan 10

Seasonal lights around Millak Waterside Park, including camellia-themed elements.

Where: Millak Waterside Park · Lights: Daily, 18:00–23:30 · Admission: Free. Official event page · Map

3. Seomyeon Light Festival — Oct 29–Feb 14

Light displays and photo zones around Seomyeon 1st Street.

Where: Seomyeon 1st Street · Lights: 18:00–24:00 · Ticket price is not listed. Official event page · Map

4. Christmas Village Busan — Nov 26–Dec 25

The outdoor plaza at Busan Cinema Center becomes a festive village. It runs Thu–Sun through Dec 20, then daily Dec 21–25. Weekdays are 16:00–22:00; weekends 11:00–22:00, split into daytime and evening entry (no new entry 15:00–16:00).

Regular-sale prices: ₩5,900 weekdays/weekend daytime, ₩7,900 weekend evening, ₩14,900 on Dec 24–25. Under 36 months free. Check the ticket time slot and checkout price.

Official event site · City listing · Map

5. Busan International Children's Book Fair — Dec 3–6

BEXCO Hall 2, Exhibition Center 1. Hours: Dec 3–5, 10:00–18:00; Dec 6, 10:00–17:00 (last entry 30 minutes before closing). Stage programmes are free but fair registration is required. Free admission requires consent to marketing information; otherwise ₩10,000. Under-14s free. International stage-program/accessibility inquiries: bicbf@kpa21.or.kr.

Official visitor guide · Programme timetable · Map

6. Gwangbok-ro Street Festa — Dec 5

Holiday lights, live music, a parade-style party and a craft market along the pedestrian street in Nampo-dong. Ticketing is not listed in the city notice.

Where: Gwangbok-ro, Jung-gu · Hours: 16:00–22:00. Official city notice · Map

7. The Gardens of Joseon — through Dec 13

A free exhibition on Joseon garden culture at Busan Museum. Open 09:00–18:00, closed Mondays; an English audio guide is available.

Official event page · Map

8. Jean-Michel Othoniel: In the Labyrinth of Love — through Dec 31

Glass-and-light works at F1963, Seokcheon Hall and Moonlight Garden. Aug 28–Dec 31; 10:00–18:00, closed Mondays. Tickets: adult ₩10,000, youth ₩8,000, child ₩6,000.

Official event page · F1963 · Map

9. Busan MoCA reopens — Dec 11

The museum is closed through Dec 10 while it prepares new exhibitions. Three exhibitions are scheduled from Dec 11: Symbiotic Couture, The Labor of Reading the World and Busan MoCA Collection: New Media and Media. Regular galleries are free; check individual exhibitions for fees. Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00, last admission 17:30; closed Mondays.

Official closure/exhibition notice · Hours and admission · Map

10. The Little Singers of Paris — Dec 11

Busan Concert Hall, 19:30, 120 minutes; primary-school age and older (born in 2019 or earlier). Tickets: R ₩70,000 / S ₩50,000 / A ₩30,000 / student ₩10,000.

Official booking and details · Map

Plan by area

  • Nampo: Dec 5 Street Festa + BIFF Square + Bupyeong Kkangtong Market.
  • Centum: BICBF at BEXCO + Christmas Village at Busan Cinema Center; confirm ticket sessions.
  • Haeundae: light festival as an evening walk after an earlier meal.
  • Suyeong: Millak Luce Festa and F1963 are separate stops; check transfers.
  • Seomyeon: lights after dinner; subway onward.

Just beyond this edition

Balcony Music Show is scheduled for Dec 12 and 19, 18:00–21:00, along Gwangbok-ro. Verify it in the next weekly update.
