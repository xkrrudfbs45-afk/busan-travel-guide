Where to Store Luggage in Busan

Practical options around Busan Station and Gimhae Airport, plus the details to check before leaving your bags.

BUSAN, SOUTH KOREA5 MIN READCHECK BRANCH DETAILS

Save this guide to Pinterest ↗

Quick answer: If you arrive before check-in or have time after checkout, start by checking luggage storage near your next stop. Busan Station and Gimhae Airport have listed options, but opening hours, prices and availability depend on the specific location.

Choose storage around your route

The most convenient storage point is usually the one that fits your day—not necessarily the one closest to your hotel. Busan Station may work well if you arrive by KTX or plan to explore central Busan before check-in. Gimhae Airport storage may suit you if you want to explore before a flight or after landing.

Think about where you will collect your bags. Travelling across the city just to retrieve luggage can take more time than expected.

Busan Station

Busan City’s official luggage-storage listing includes a Zimcarry location inside Busan Station near Exit 5. Check the latest branch listing before you go, especially if you need early drop-off or late collection.

See Busan City’s luggage-storage information and Zimcarry’s store locations.

Gimhae Airport

Busan City’s official list also includes Zimcarry storage locations at Gimhae Airport’s domestic and international terminals. Confirm the correct terminal and counter location for your flight; the details for each terminal may differ.

Start with the official Busan City list, then confirm the branch details on Zimcarry’s location page.

What to check before leaving your bags

  • Opening hours: Match both drop-off and collection times to your itinerary.
  • Exact location: Confirm the station exit, terminal, floor or counter.
  • Price: Rates may vary by branch, bag size, duration and service type. Check the current rate for your exact needs.
  • Bag size: Ask about oversized luggage, strollers or special equipment.
  • Booking and payment: Check whether advance booking is available and which payment methods are accepted.
  • Collection: Keep your confirmation or claim details and leave enough time before boarding.

Storage or luggage delivery?

Storage means you return to the same location to collect your bags. Luggage delivery is a separate service that transports bags between specified locations and may have different booking rules, deadlines and prices. Do not assume a storage counter automatically offers delivery.

A simple plan for arrival and checkout days

First ask your accommodation whether it can hold bags before check-in or after checkout. If not, compare a station or airport storage location with the places you plan to visit. For a late arrival, plan your route to the hotel before choosing a storage or delivery service.

Need luggage delivery instead of storage?

For travel days when you would rather send bags between a transport hub and your accommodation, compare the provider’s eligible locations, cut-off times, baggage rules and delivery window before booking.
