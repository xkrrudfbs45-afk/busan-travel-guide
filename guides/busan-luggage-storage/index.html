Where to Store Luggage in Busan

Practical options around Busan Station and Gimhae Airport, plus the details to check before leaving your bags.

BUSAN, SOUTH KOREA5 MIN READCHECK BRANCH DETAILS

Save this guide to Pinterest ↗

Quick answer: If you arrive before check-in or have time after checkout, start by checking luggage storage near your next stop. Busan Station and Gimhae Airport have listed options, but opening hours, prices and availability depend on the specific location.

Choose storage around your route

The most convenient storage point is usually the one that fits your day—not necessarily the one closest to your hotel. Busan Station may work well if you arrive by KTX or plan to explore central Busan before check-in. Gimhae Airport storage may suit you if you want to explore before a flight or after landing.

Think about where you will collect your bags. Travelling across the city just to retrieve luggage can take more time than expected.

Busan Station

For KTX arrivals and departures, the clearest confirmed option is Zimcarry inside Busan Station: the branch is listed just inside Gate 5 on the station building’s first floor (206 Jungang-daero, Dong-gu). Busan City’s luggage-storage directory gives the same location.

Branch details published by Zimcarry: daily 09:00–22:00; suitcase storage is listed at KRW 5,000 (up to 55 cm), KRW 8,000 (56–70 cm) and KRW 10,000 (71 cm or larger) per day. Bag storage is listed separately from KRW 3,000. These are the branch’s published rates, not a guarantee that every item or special-size bag qualifies.

Before leaving your bags, confirm the item category, size measurement, chargeable period and collection deadline. The branch page lists the Busan Station address and service details; Busan City’s directory lists the service contact as 1544-8215.

Check Zimcarry’s Busan Station branch page, all Zimcarry locations and Busan City’s luggage-storage directory before travelling.

If Busan Station lockers are full: practical backup options

Do not assume every locker bank is full—or that every large suitcase will fit. Check the locker screen and the available compartment dimensions first. If there is no suitable space, compare a staffed counter or a bookable self-service location before changing your route.

Option 1: Zimcarry inside Busan KTX Station

Busan City lists Zimcarry inside the KTX station building, just inside Exit 5 on the first floor. The operator lists daily opening hours of 09:00–22:00. Its published 24-hour price table ranges from ₩8,000 to ₩15,000 depending on the size category, with separate rates for shorter storage periods. Confirm the Busan Station branch’s current size category, exact price and final collection time before leaving your bag; the published price does not guarantee same-day capacity. Call 1544-8215.

Option 2: ParkZim Storage near Exit 7

Visit Busan lists ParkZim Storage at 3F, 211 Jungang-daero, about a one-minute walk from Busan Station Exit 7, as a 24-hour self-service option. Its listing shows ₩10,000 for a four-hour pass and ₩25,000 for a 24-hour pass. Treat these as the listed offer, not a guaranteed walk-in price: check the booking page, exact locker capacity, access instructions and payment method before going.

Option 3: Try another metro station locker only if it fits your route

Busan City’s luggage-storage directory lists lockers across Busan Metro Lines 1–4. This does not mean a specific station has an available extra-large compartment when you arrive. If you are already heading to Nampo or Seomyeon, check the locker bank and size at that station before relying on it. Avoid making a special cross-city trip without confirming availability.

Option 4: Choryang I-eum self-service luggage lockers

Busan City also lists a luggage-locker point in Choryang-dong 355, in front of Hana Bank’s Choryang branch. The published contact number is 051-469-9905. The city listing does not provide a confirmed live capacity, price or opening-hours guarantee, so call first rather than treating it as an always-available backup.

Before you choose

  • For a staffed counter, confirm both drop-off and final collection time.
  • For a self-service locker, check the actual compartment dimensions—not just the label “large”.
  • Do not assume a 28-inch suitcase will fit; measure the case including wheels and handles.
  • Do not assume an overseas card, a local app or cash will work. Confirm accepted payment methods before booking.
  • Keep passports, medication, valuables and items needed during the day with you.
  • Storage and hotel delivery are different services. Confirm route, cut-off time and delivery window separately.

We could not verify a blanket rule that Busan Station lockers sell out early, a universal 28-inch restriction, or that a particular third-party app accepts every overseas card. Check live availability and payment terms directly. Last checked: 12 November 2026.

Gimhae Airport

Busan City’s official list also includes Zimcarry storage locations at Gimhae Airport’s domestic and international terminals. Confirm the correct terminal and counter location for your flight; the details for each terminal may differ.

Start with the official Busan City list, then confirm the branch details on Zimcarry’s location page.

What to check before leaving your bags

  • Opening hours: Match both drop-off and collection times to your itinerary.
  • Exact location: Confirm the station exit, terminal, floor or counter.
  • Price: Rates may vary by branch, bag size, duration and service type. Check the current rate for your exact needs.
  • Bag size: Ask about oversized luggage, strollers or special equipment.
  • Booking and payment: Check whether advance booking is available and which payment methods are accepted.
  • Collection: Keep your confirmation or claim details and leave enough time before boarding.

Storage or luggage delivery?

Storage means you return to the same location to collect your bags. Luggage delivery is a separate service that transports bags between specified locations and may have different booking rules, deadlines and prices. Do not assume a storage counter automatically offers delivery.

A simple plan for arrival and checkout days

First ask your accommodation whether it can hold bags before check-in or after checkout. If not, compare a station or airport storage location with the places you plan to visit. For a late arrival, plan your route to the hotel before choosing a storage or delivery service.

Need luggage delivery instead of storage?

For travel days when you would rather send bags between a transport hub and your accommodation, compare the provider’s eligible locations, cut-off times, baggage rules and delivery window before booking.
