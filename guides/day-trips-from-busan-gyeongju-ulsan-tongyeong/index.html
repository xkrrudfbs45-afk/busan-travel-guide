Day Trips from Busan: Gyeongju, Ulsan and Tongyeong

Busan makes a useful base for exploring southeast Korea, but these three destinations offer very different days. Choose Gyeongju for historic sites, Ulsan for a mix of coast and riverside nature, or Tongyeong for harbor scenery and island views.

Before booking: Busan has more than one intercity-bus terminal. The correct departure point depends on the destination and operator. Confirm the exact terminal, arrival point and return service in the official booking system before you leave.

Which day trip suits you?

  • Gyeongju: best for Silla history, temples, royal tombs and heritage sites. Plan around a small number of widely spaced stops.
  • Ulsan: best for a nature-focused day with riverside gardens, bamboo paths or a rocky east-coast park.
  • Tongyeong: best for harbor views, a cable-car ride and a compact old-town walk. Allow extra time for the intercity transfer.

Gyeongju: Silla heritage and historic landscapes

Gyeongju is the strongest choice if you want a history-focused day. Bulguksa Temple is a UNESCO-listed heritage site, while Daereungwon Ancient Tomb Complex, Hwangnidan-gil and Donggung Palace & Wolji Pond offer different views of the historic city.

Route idea: choose either a temple-focused visit (Bulguksa and nearby heritage sites) or a central-city route (Daereungwon, Hwangnidan-gil and Donggung & Wolji). Trying to cover every major site in one day can turn the trip into a sequence of transfers.

Getting there: options include intercity bus services from Busan and rail to Singyeongju Station. The station is outside the historic centre, so include the onward city-bus or taxi transfer in your plan. Busan Metropolitan City lists Gyeongju among destinations served from its Dongbu and Seobu intercity terminals; check the specific service and arrival terminal.

Ulsan: riverside garden or east-coast park

Ulsan works well for travelers who prefer open space. Taehwagang National Garden and the Simni Bamboo Grove offer a riverside walking experience, while Daewangam Park has a pine-lined coastal trail leading toward the lighthouse and rock formations.

Route idea: choose one main area. Pair Taehwagang National Garden with the bamboo grove for a slower green-space day, or focus on Daewangam Park for a coastal walk. The two areas are not adjacent, so combining them requires local transport and a realistic time allowance.

Getting there: the Donghae Line connects Bujeon in Busan with Taehwagang Station in Ulsan. From Taehwagang, use local transport to reach the specific attraction. Ulsan’s official tourism site also lists rail, bus, flight and city-transport information; check current routes and any city-tour operation before departure.

Tongyeong: harbor views and the cable car

Tongyeong is a good choice for sea views and a port-town atmosphere. The Tongyeong Cable Car climbs Mireuksan and offers views over the surrounding islands when weather and visibility allow. In the old town, plan time around the harbor and nearby market streets rather than adding too many distant stops.

Route idea: make the cable car the main timed activity, then explore the harbor area on foot. Cable-car operations can be affected by weather; confirm the operating status, ticketing and last admission on the official operator site before traveling.

Getting there: intercity buses run from Busan terminals to Tongyeong. Busan Metropolitan City lists Tongyeong services from both its Seobu and Dongbu terminal networks. Confirm which departure terminal is most convenient for your accommodation and where the bus arrives in Tongyeong.

How to choose your transport

  • Rail: useful for Gyeongju via Singyeongju Station and Ulsan via Taehwagang on the Donghae Line; check the final local transfer.
  • Intercity bus: useful for Gyeongju and Tongyeong, with departure options depending on Busan terminal. Check the exact route and terminal in advance.
  • Taxi or local bus: often needed for the final leg, especially when attractions are outside the city centre. Estimate the return trip before starting a long walk.

A realistic day-trip checklist

  • Choose one destination and two or three main stops at most.
  • Check the first outbound and last return service before buying anything.
  • Confirm the departure terminal: Nopo and Sasang are different parts of Busan.
  • Check weather-sensitive operations, especially the Tongyeong Cable Car.
  • Keep a buffer for local buses, queues and the return transfer.
