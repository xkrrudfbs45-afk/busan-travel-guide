LOCAL FOOD HISTORY · Food origins can be disputed; this guide distinguishes documented context from stories that remain uncertain.

Busan’s food is more than a list of famous dishes. Some recipes grew around factories and markets; others took shape when war brought displaced people to the port city. This guide follows the history behind three everyday favourites, using published sources and keeping disputed origin stories clearly labelled.
