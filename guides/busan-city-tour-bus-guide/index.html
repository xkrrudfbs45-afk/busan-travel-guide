BUSAN TRANSPORT

Busan City Tour Bus: Routes, Timetables and Tickets

A practical guide to the Red, Green and Orange loops, where to board at Busan Station, and how to plan your day without missing the last return bus.

GO2BUSAN LOCAL GUIDETRANSPORTUpdated 12 November 2026

Quick answer: The official circular service has three routes—Red, Green and Orange. One day ticket covers all three. The regular fare is ₩20,000 for adults and ₩10,000 for children; advance online purchase is listed at 5% off. The service is normally closed on Mondays and Tuesdays.

Where to board at Busan Station

The official boarding point is opposite the Starbucks on the first floor of Ramada Encore Hotel. The operator describes it as near KTX Busan Station Exit 1 and Busan Metro Station Exit 6. Follow the City Tour Bus signs and confirm the stop name before joining the queue.

What each route connects

  • Red Line: Busan Museum and UN Memorial Park, Gwangalli Beach, Marine City, Dongbaekseom, Haeundae Beach and Centum City.
  • Green Line: Yeongdo Bridge, Huinnyeoul Culture Village, Taejongdae, National Maritime Museum, Arte Museum Busan, Oryukdo Skywalk, Songdo and Jagalchi/BIFF Square.
  • Orange Line: Songdo, Amnam Park, Gamcheon Culture Village, Dadaepo Beach, Amisan Observatory, Bunezia/Jangnim Port, Gukje Market and Yongdusan Park.

Ticket price and how to buy

The circular day ticket is listed at ₩20,000 for adults and ₩10,000 for children aged 48 months through elementary-school age. The official site lists a 5% discount for advance online purchase: ₩19,000 and ₩9,500 respectively. One day ticket covers the Red, Green and Orange circular routes; it does not include separate themed night tours.

Online purchases are issued as a QR ticket. The operator also lists on-site purchase from staff, subject to available seats. Review the current purchase instructions and ticket terms before paying.

Operating days and practical tips

  • Regular closure: Monday and Tuesday. Check holiday notices before planning around a public holiday.
  • Bus type: Red and Green may use a one- or two-storey vehicle depending on the day. Orange operates with a single-storey vehicle.
  • Stopovers: Check the route map and timetable to make sure the bus you plan to board serves the stop and time you need.
  • Weather: Traffic and weather can affect operating times. Keep a buffer before your last planned connection.
  • Accessibility: Vehicle assignment and boarding conditions can vary. Contact the operator before travel if you need a specific accessible boarding arrangement.
