BTS in Busan: Fan Stops and a Local Travel Guide

Follow the Busan connections associated with BTS while discovering the city beyond the fan photo stops: neighborhood markets, coastal walks, local food and practical ways to move around.

The short version: Busan is especially meaningful to ARMY because Jimin and Jung Kook are from the city. Official tourism guides highlight places connected with the members alongside major Busan attractions. The best plan combines a few verified stops with nearby neighborhoods, food and coastal scenery instead of trying to cross the city repeatedly.

Start with the right expectations

This is a mixed guide for BTS fans and travelers who simply want to understand the city's pop-culture connections. Not every place mentioned online is an official BTS attraction, and a member's past visit does not mean a business is affiliated with BTS today. We distinguish places highlighted by tourism sources from ordinary public attractions and private businesses.

Busan is associated with members Jimin and Jung Kook. The city also hosted BTS's Yet to Come concert in 2022. For current event announcements, use official event channels; do not assume a past concert venue has a permanent BTS exhibition.

Fan stops that fit a wider Busan trip

Gamcheon Culture Village

Gamcheon is a major hillside attraction known for colorful houses, narrow lanes and public art. Korea Tourism Organization's BTS-focused travel feature identifies murals of Jimin and Jung Kook as a fan stop. Treat it first as a living residential neighborhood: keep noise down, leave doorways clear, follow signs and do not enter private property.

Seodong Miro Market and egg dumplings

The Korea Tourism Organization's fan itinerary includes Seodong Miro Market and mentions Matna Bunsik's egg dumplings in connection with Jimin's memories. This is a useful way to add a local market experience to a fan-oriented day. Check the stall's current operation and menu before making a special trip; small market businesses can change.

Explore the market respectfully, bring some cash as a backup for small purchases, and ask about ingredients if you have allergies.

Mandeok-dong, Mandeok Lego Village and Seokbulsa Temple

Official tourism material points to Mandeok-dong as an area associated with Jung Kook's childhood and recommends nearby Mandeok Lego Village and Seokbulsa Temple as area attractions. These are not private fan sites. Plan the visit around the neighborhood's public attractions and be considerate of residents.

Dadaepo Beach and Oryukdo

VISITKOREA's current BTS travel feature highlights Dadaepo Beach in connection with a Jimin vlog and Oryukdo as a location featured on BANGTANTV. Both also stand on their own as coastal destinations. Dadaepo is known for its broad beach and sunset atmosphere; Oryukdo offers dramatic sea views. Check weather, access and sunset time on the day.

Busan Citizens Park and Busan Museum of Art

The same official tourism feature lists Busan Citizens Park as a place visited by V and Busan Museum of Art as a place visited by RM. Treat these as public cultural and leisure stops, and check each venue's current exhibitions, opening days and access before visiting.

ZM-ILLENNIAL in Daeyeon-dong

VISITKOREA identifies ZM-ILLENNIAL, formerly known as MAGNATE, as a bakery café associated with Jimin's father. Business names and ownership can change, so confirm the current name and operating details before travelling. Do not assume that a café is an official HYBE or BTS-operated venue.

Two practical ways to plan the day

Route A: Gamcheon, Seodong and the old downtown

  1. Start at Gamcheon Culture Village and allow time for its slopes and lanes.
  2. Continue to Seodong Miro Market for a market walk and, if currently available, egg dumplings at Matna Bunsik.
  3. Finish with dinner in the Nampo-dong area or another stop that fits your accommodation.

This route links two fan-relevant stops with a broader look at Busan's hillside and market culture. Confirm transit connections on the day; the stops are not next door to each other.

Open Route A in Google Maps ↗

Route B: Mandeok and the coast

  1. Explore public attractions around Mandeok, such as Mandeok Lego Village or Seokbulsa Temple, if the terrain and access suit your group.
  2. Choose either Oryukdo or Dadaepo for a coastal visit rather than attempting both in a rushed afternoon.
  3. Have a meal near your final stop or return to a neighborhood with more dining choices.

Oryukdo and Dadaepo are in different parts of the city. Check travel time before combining them.

Respectful fan travel

  • Do not visit schools, homes or other private places as fan destinations. Keep the trip to public spaces and businesses open to visitors.
  • Do not block entrances, photograph residents without consent or leave stickers and other materials behind.
  • Do not treat a business as BTS-affiliated unless it clearly states an official relationship.
  • Check current opening hours, business names, transit routes and weather before departure.
