Seoul to Busan: KTX, Express Bus or Flight?

The best way to reach Busan depends on where you start in Seoul, where you need to arrive and how much time you can spend on transfers. Compare the full journey—not just the time in the vehicle.

2026 rail update: Korail announced an integrated high-speed rail service arrangement that applies KTX fare levels to services from both Seoul and Suseo. Do not treat KTX and SRT as two completely separate booking ecosystems without checking the current Korail notice and ticket inventory.

Quick answer

  • Choose KTX for a fast, city-centre-to-city-centre trip and a predictable arrival at Busan Station.
  • Choose an express bus if you prefer a lower-cost seat or a departure time that fits better, and can allow for road traffic.
  • Choose a flight if Gimpo and Gimhae airports fit naturally into the rest of your journey, or you are connecting to another flight. Include airport transfers and check-in time in the comparison.

KTX: the easiest default for many visitors

KTX usually works well for visitors staying near central Seoul or central Busan. Trains depart from Seoul Station and also serve the Suseo area under the updated high-speed rail arrangement. Most services arrive at Busan Station, which has metro and taxi connections into the city.

Korail announced a Seoul–Busan standard fare of KRW 54,400 under its 2026 high-speed rail changes. Actual ticket prices can vary by train, seat class, discount eligibility and travel date. Check the fare and timetable shown for your exact departure before booking.

Book through Korail’s official website or its official app. Popular departure times and holiday periods can sell out; reserve once your dates are fixed.

Express bus: compare the terminal and traffic

Express buses connect Seoul with Busan’s Central Bus Terminal at Nopo. This terminal is in northern Busan, away from the central Busan Station area and the main beach districts. A bus can suit travelers who are staying near the departure terminal or prioritizing the fare, but road conditions can make arrival times less predictable.

Check the exact departure terminal, service class, scheduled journey and arrival terminal on KOBUS. Busan has several terminals; do not confuse the Central Bus Terminal at Nopo with Seobu Terminal at Sasang.

Flight: count the airport time

Flights operate between the Seoul Gimpo and Busan Gimhae airport areas. The time in the air is only one part of the trip: add travel to Gimpo, airport arrival and security time, baggage collection and the onward journey from Gimhae into Busan. A flight can make sense when you are already near an airport or connecting from another route, but it is not automatically the fastest door-to-door choice.

Compare the full itinerary and baggage conditions directly with the airline. Flight times, fares and operating schedules are date-specific and are not fixed in this guide.

Compare the journey, not only the ticket

  • Starting near Seoul Station: KTX is usually the most direct option.
  • Starting in southeast Seoul: check services from Suseo as well as Seoul Station before crossing the city.
  • Staying near Nopo or traveling onward by bus: the express bus terminal may be convenient.
  • Connecting through Gimpo or Gimhae: compare flight schedules and the airport transfers on both ends.

After you arrive in Busan

Busan Station and Nopo are not interchangeable arrival points. Busan Station is central and connects with Metro Line 1; Nopo is at the northern end of Metro Line 1. Gimhae Airport is west of the city and connects to the Busan–Gimhae Light Rail. Choose your accommodation and first activity with the arrival point in mind.
