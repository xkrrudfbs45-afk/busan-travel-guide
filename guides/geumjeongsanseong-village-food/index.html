MOUNTAIN FOOD · GEUMJEONG-GU

What to Eat in Geumjeongsanseong Village: Duck, Mountain Greens and Makgeolli

Trade the beachfront for a mountain village meal. Geumjeongsanseong is known for duck dishes, earthy mountain vegetables and a traditional makgeolli with a distinctive tang.

Duck dishes are a local favourite

Duck bulgogi and other duck dishes are among the foods associated with Geumjeongsanseong Village. They are often ordered for a shared meal, making them a good choice for families or groups. Restaurant menus and serving sizes differ, so check whether a portion is intended for two or more people.

Mountain vegetable bibimbap

Sanchae bibimbap combines rice with seasonal mountain greens and vegetable side dishes. It offers a lighter alternative to a meat-centred meal and gives visitors a taste of ingredients associated with Korea’s mountain regions. The exact greens vary by season and restaurant.

Acorn jelly and other sides

Dotorimuk, a jelly made from acorn starch, is another traditional dish you may find on menus in the area. It is usually served with a savoury dressing and vegetables. Jeon, stews and other Korean country-style dishes may also be available.

Pair the meal with local makgeolli

Geumjeongsanseong Makgeolli is the area’s best-known traditional drink. Its tangy, grain-forward profile makes a useful contrast to rich duck dishes and savoury pancakes. If you drink, arrange transport in advance and never drive after drinking. Non-alcoholic drinks are the best choice for drivers.

Getting the most from a visit

Geumjeongsanseong Village is a mountain destination rather than a quick stop between downtown attractions. Check transit, return options, restaurant hours and group reservations before you go. For a deeper cultural visit, consider the official makgeolli-making experience, but confirm booking rules and current availability directly with the producer.
