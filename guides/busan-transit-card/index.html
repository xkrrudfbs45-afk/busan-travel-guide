Getting around

How to Use Public Transportation in Busan

A practical introduction to transit cards, metro rides, buses, transfers and common mistakes for visitors.

BUSAN, SOUTH KOREA6 MIN READCHECK DETAILS BEFORE TRAVEL

Quick answer: For many visitors, a rechargeable transport card is the easiest way to pay for ordinary metro and bus trips. Check which card or pass fits your itinerary, and do not assume every tourist pass covers every service.

Metro and buses work best together

Busan’s metro is useful for many major districts, while buses can take you closer to destinations that are farther from a station or located on hills. Some trips are easiest with a transfer, so check the whole journey rather than only the first leg.

Using a rechargeable transit card

Transport cards can reduce friction when boarding and transferring. Buy or top up a card at an authorized sales point, then follow the instructions for tapping when you enter and exit. On buses, make sure you use the reader as instructed; the correct tap procedure matters for fare calculation and transfer benefits.

Card availability, top-up methods and accepted payment options can vary by location and card type. Check current guidance before relying on a particular purchase method.

Tourist passes: check the fine print

Busan and Korea offer different passes and transport products, but their coverage, validity periods and eligible services differ. Compare the pass price with the trips you realistically plan to take. A pass is not automatically cheaper than paying per ride, and some airport, intercity or special services may be excluded.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Assuming every pass includes airport transfers or intercity trains.
  • Forgetting to check whether a transfer requires another tap.
  • Planning a late-night route without checking the final service.
  • Confusing a metro station’s straight-line distance with the actual walking route.
  • Relying on old fare tables or social posts for current prices.

Before leaving your accommodation

  • Check the route and the last service for the return journey.
  • Keep some balance on your card and know where to top it up.
  • Save your destination name in Korean when possible.
  • Allow extra time when changing between metro and bus.

Official sources

  • Busan Transportation Corporation — metro routes and service notices.
  • Visit Busan transport guide — visitor-focused transport information.

Fares, card products, pass conditions and transfer rules can change. Verify current terms with official sources before travel.
