GETTING AROUND

Busan Public Transportation: Cards, Metro & Buses

A straightforward guide to paying for rides, making transfers, and choosing the right transport option for your trip.

BUSAN, SOUTH KOREA7 MIN READCHECK CURRENT RULES

Quick answer: For most visitors making several trips, a rechargeable transport card is a convenient default for ordinary metro and city-bus journeys. Compare tourist passes against your actual itinerary before buying one, and check whether airport or special services are included.

Metro or bus: which should you use?

Busan’s metro is often the simplest way to travel between major areas, while buses can bring you closer to places that are farther from a station or located on hills. For some destinations, combining the two is the most practical option. Check the full route and walking distance rather than choosing by straight-line distance alone.

For late arrivals, check the final service before you travel. A route that works in the afternoon may not be available after midnight. Our Gimhae Airport late-arrival guide covers the main options for getting to your accommodation.

T-money, Cashbee and other transport cards

A rechargeable transport card can make ordinary bus and metro trips easier because you tap it at the reader instead of buying a separate ticket for each ride. T-money and Cashbee are familiar examples, but availability, sales points and top-up options can vary. Follow the instructions at the point of sale and check the card’s current terms.

Remember that a transport card is not the same thing as a tourist attraction pass. A card’s ability to pay for rides, a pass’s included attractions, and any special transport benefits are separate conditions that should be checked individually.

What about WOWPASS?

WOWPASS combines a prepaid payment function with a separate T-money transport function. Do not assume the two balances are interchangeable: the transport balance is managed separately from the WOWPASS spending balance. The app may offer supported ways to top up T-money, but the available method and conditions can change, so check the current official instructions before relying on it.

Read the official WOWPASS guide and the Korea Tourism Organization’s WOWPASS information for current details.

Bus and metro transfers: the detail that matters

Busan offers transfer benefits on eligible journeys, but you need to follow the applicable rules. The city’s visitor guide describes a general 30-minute transfer window and up to two transfers for eligible journeys. Use the same eligible transport card, tap when boarding and tap off when leaving a bus. Eligible modes and exceptions depend on the current rules.

  • Tap your card on boarding and tap off when exiting a bus.
  • Check the transfer time limit and eligible modes before your journey; the city’s visitor guide describes a general 30-minute transfer window and up to two transfers.
  • Do not assume airport limousine services or every special service qualify for the same transfer discount.
  • Cash payments may not qualify for the same transfer benefits as an eligible transport card.

Because transfer terms can be revised, confirm the current conditions in the official Visit Busan transport guide and the Busan bus information system’s transfer guide.

Are tourist passes worth it?

A tourist pass may be useful if you plan to visit several included attractions in a short period. It is not automatically the cheapest option. Before buying, list the attractions you genuinely intend to visit, check their current inclusion and opening hours, and compare the total with individual admission costs and transport expenses.

Read the conditions carefully: attraction admission, metro or bus rides, airport transfers and intercity transport may be treated differently. Never assume one pass covers every service.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Assuming a tourist pass includes every bus, train or airport service.
  • Forgetting to tap off a bus when required.
  • Assuming WOWPASS spending balance and T-money balance are one shared balance.
  • Planning a late-night journey without checking the last service.
  • Relying on old fare screenshots or social posts instead of current official information.
  • Underestimating walking time between a station, bus stop and destination.

A quick checklist before leaving

  • Check your route, transfer points and last service for the return trip.
  • Make sure your transport card has enough balance and that you know how to top it up.
  • Save your destination in Korean as well as English when possible.
  • Allow extra time when changing between metro and bus.
  • Recheck current fares and rules close to your travel date.

Official sources

  • Busan Transportation Corporation — metro routes and service notices.
  • Visit Busan transport guide — visitor-focused transport information.
  • Busan bus information system — bus transfer rules.
  • WOWPASS official guide — card and balance details.

Fares, card products, top-up methods, pass coverage and transfer rules can change. Verify current terms with the official sources linked above before travel.
