Pusan National University Food Guide: 5 Local Picks

Japanese-style home cooking, a famous tonkatsu queue, spicy gochujang bulgogi and charcoal-grilled chicken: use this local shortlist to plan a meal around Busan's university neighborhood.

GEUMJEONG-GULOCAL EDITOR PICKSCHECK DETAILS BEFORE VISITING

How to use this guide: Choose one place based on your appetite and schedule rather than trying to visit every restaurant in one go. Restaurant hours, menus, prices and queue systems can change; check the current map listing before travelling. These are editorial recommendations, not paid placements.

Yamabento (야마벤또): Japanese-style home cooking

Best for: a casual meal with a Japanese home-style feel.

Yamabento is a neighborhood pick around Pusan National University for visitors who want a Japanese-style set meal rather than a Korean barbecue or street-food stop. It makes a good option when you want a straightforward, comforting lunch or dinner while exploring the university area. Check the current menu and service hours before setting out.

Tonshou (톤쇼우): popular Japanese tonkatsu

Best for: Japanese-style pork cutlet and a meal you are happy to plan around.

Tonshou is known for Japanese-style tonkatsu and attracts long queues. It can be a destination in its own right, but waiting may take a meaningful part of your day. Check the restaurant's current reservation or waiting-list process and opening information before you go; avoid building a tight sightseeing schedule around an unconfirmed wait time.

Find Tonshou on Google Maps ↗

Daegil Gochu Bulgogi (대길고추불고기): spicy gochujang bulgogi

Area: near the side gate of Pusan National University.

Daegil Gochu Bulgogi is a local recommendation for a bold, spicy-savoury bulgogi meal. The gochu-style seasoning makes it a different choice from Japanese set meals or tonkatsu, and its location near the university's side gate makes it a useful option if you are already exploring the campus neighborhood. If you are sensitive to spice, ask staff about the current seasoning and available options.

Find Daegil Gochu Bulgogi on Google Maps ↗

New Charcoal Tongdak Main Branch (뉴숯불통닭 본점): charcoal-grilled chicken

Best for: a chicken meal with a charcoal-grilled flavour.

New Charcoal Tongdak's main branch is another neighborhood pick for visitors who want chicken with a smoky charcoal-grilled character rather than a standard fried-chicken meal. It is worth comparing with the curry chicken at Hoho Chicken House, another local recommendation in the wider Pusan National University area. Confirm the current menu, branch details and hours before visiting.

Hoho Chicken House (호호닭집): curry chicken

Best for: a distinctive Korean chicken meal near Pusan National University.

Hoho Chicken House is known locally for curry-seasoned chicken. If you want to try something different from standard fried or sweet-spicy Korean chicken, the curry version is the reason to put it on your list. Check the current menu and portion options before ordering.

Choose by the kind of meal you want

These five places are not a tasting route to complete in one afternoon. They are alternatives for different appetites and schedules:

  • A quieter, familiar meal: start with Yamabento's Japanese-style home cooking.
  • The meal is the main event: choose Tonshou only if you can build flexibility around its queue and current waiting system.
  • You want a distinctly Korean flavour: Daegil Gochu Bulgogi is the spicy-savoury option; ask about the current heat level if you are unsure.
  • You are eating with a group: compare the chicken options and check current portion sizes before deciding. Chicken dishes may suit sharing, but confirm the serving size and price rather than assuming.

For a first visit, pick one restaurant and leave the rest of the time for the university streets and nearby cafés. Restaurant names, menus and map pins can be similar, so check the Korean name and branch before setting off.

Useful Korean phrases at the table

You do not need fluent Korean to ask a few practical questions. These short phrases can help clarify the order before you commit:

  • 메뉴판 볼 수 있을까요? (menyupan bol su isseulkkayo?) — Could I see the menu?
  • 이 메뉴 맵나요? (i menyu maepnayo?) — Is this dish spicy?
  • 몇 인분부터 주문해야 하나요? (myeot inbunbuteo jumunhaeya hanayo?) — What is the minimum number of servings?
  • 웨이팅은 어떻게 하면 되나요? (waiting-eun eotteoke hamyeon doenayo?) — How does the waiting list work?
  • 카드 결제 되나요? (kadeu gyeolje doenayo?) — Can I pay by card?

If you have a food allergy, show the ingredient name in Korean as well as explaining it verbally; do not rely on a dish name alone. For severe allergies, confirm preparation and cross-contact directly with staff.

Make it a relaxed university-area outing

Pusan National University Station on Busan Metro Line 1 is a practical starting point for exploring the area. Pick a restaurant first, then leave time to walk around the university streets and browse nearby cafés and shops. If Tonshou is your priority, plan flexibly around its queue rather than assuming you can be seated immediately.

  • For Japanese-style food: compare Yamabento and Tonshou.
  • For a spicy Korean meal: try Daegil Gochu Bulgogi.
  • For chicken: compare New Charcoal Tongdak with Hoho Chicken House's curry chicken.
