## 1. Reddit draft for r/KoreaTravel

**Suggested title:** Planning Seoul to Busan by KTX? A few details worth checking before you book

**Post:**

If you're adding Busan to a first Korea trip, a few small checks can make the Seoul–Busan train journey less stressful:

- **Check the actual departure station on your ticket.** Don't assume every train described as “Seoul to Busan” leaves from the same station. Plan your journey to the station printed on the confirmed ticket.
- **Check the booking window on Korail itself.** Current regular ticket guidance says sales open at 07:00 Korea time two months before departure, but special holiday schedules and notices can differ.
- **Keep the actual ticket accessible.** A payment receipt or third-party voucher may not be the boarding ticket. Follow the instructions from the service that issued it.
- **Allow time for luggage and finding the platform.** At a large station, arriving with a little buffer is less stressful than trying to find the right car at the last minute.
- **Plan what happens after you arrive.** If your hotel check-in is later, ask the hotel about bag storage or compare a station-area storage option. Storage and luggage delivery are different services, so check the exact terms.

The official place to verify the train, station and current ticket rules is Korail's English site: https://www.korail.com/global
