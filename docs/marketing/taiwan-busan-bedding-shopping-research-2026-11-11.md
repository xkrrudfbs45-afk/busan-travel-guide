## Editorial opportunity

K-bedding is a credible Busan-specific shopping topic, not merely a Seoul trend transplanted to Busan. A Financial News report published 2026-08-27 described Taiwan visitors buying bedding at Busan's Gukje Market. It reported Taiwan-language signage, Taiwanese customer service staff, vacuum-packed bedding, and consolidated postal shipments to Taiwan. The article said merchants had noticed Taiwan customers since 2023 and described a sharp increase more recently. These are reported observations and merchant comments, not independently audited market statistics.

The same report cited Busan visitor figures for the first half of 2026: 2.42 million foreign visitors, including 477,606 Taiwanese visitors (19.7%). Treat these as figures quoted by the article; verify against the original official tourism-statistics release before reusing in public-facing copy.

## Required on-site verification before publishing a store recommendation

The owner has visited Gukje Market but does not know the bedding shops. Treat 3공구 as a starting area, not a confirmed single alley entrance or a verified list of shops.

During a future visit, record:
1. Exact building/공구, floor, aisle and map pin for each store.
2. Store name as displayed, business hours, weekly closing day and contact details.
3. Bedding categories and materials actually stocked (e.g. cotton, modal, cooling fabric, pillowcases, summer blankets).
4. Product dimensions, prices and whether prices are per item/set; note date and photographed price tags.
5. Country of manufacture on labels; never equate “sold in Korea” with “Made in Korea”.
6. Whether vacuum compression is offered, whether it is included in the price, and whether packaging fits airline baggage limits.
7. Taiwan shipping availability, carrier, fee calculation, delivery estimate, tracking, payment methods and responsibility for customs/import charges.
8. Languages supported and whether staff can explain materials and sizes clearly.
9. Whether card payments, tax refunds or foreign-currency payments are actually accepted; do not assume.
10. Permission to photograph store signs, products, labels, prices and staff.

Do not publish individual store names, exact prices, shipping claims, opening hours or “best shop” rankings until directly checked and dated. Mark owner-visited versus desk-researched information clearly.

## Second market to investigate: Busanjin Market (부산진시장)

The owner's field note suggests Busanjin Market is also a substantial bedding-shopping destination. This is supported by official tourism and municipal sources:
- Korea Tourism Organization accessible-tourism listing (updated 2026-11-10) describes Busanjin Market as a wedding-goods and fabric-specialist market with about 1,350 shops, including bedding, hanbok, fabrics and household goods. It lists 24 Jinsijang-ro, Dong-gu, phone 051-646-7041~3, 07:00–19:00 and Sunday closure. Treat market-level hours as a general guide; individual shop schedules can differ: https://access.visitkorea.or.kr/ms/detail.do?cotId=c6937954-c7b4-4605-b258-2b3ea11e0cc8
- Dong-gu official tourism page describes the market's long textile and bedding tradition and its wedding-goods focus: https://www.bsdonggu.go.kr/board/view.donggu?boardId=BBS_0000094&dataSid=5000192&menuCd=DOM_000000313003000000&startPage=1
- Official market directory lists individual shops and floors, including bedding retailers. Current directory: https://bjmhome.cafe24.com/ and floor/category listings: https://bjmhome.cafe24.com/mobile/sub.php?key=cate&keyword=1514012535&m=all

### Positioning versus Gukje Market

- Gukje Market / Nampo-dong: position as the visitor-friendly market-shopping experience to combine with BIFF Square, street food, cafes and nearby sightseeing. Taiwan visitor bedding demand, vacuum packing and shipping have been reported there, but verify each store's current service.
- Busanjin Market / Beomil-dong: position as a larger, specialist textile and wedding-goods market where visitors can compare bedding and fabrics across multiple shops. It may be better for shoppers who prioritize product range and material comparison over a compact sightseeing route.
- Do not claim Busanjin Market is cheaper, better, or more Taiwan-tourist-ready than Gukje Market without a documented comparison.
