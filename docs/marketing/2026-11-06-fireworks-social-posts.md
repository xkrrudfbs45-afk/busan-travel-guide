## Threads: traffic reminder

**Busan Fireworks Festival is tomorrow (Sat, Nov 7). Save this before heading to Gwangalli.**

Road closures start early:
- 10:00 — Gwanganhaebyeon-ro
- 14:00 — Hwangnyeongsan-ro (no detour)
- 15:30 — Gwangan Bridge upper deck, both directions
- 16:00 — more beachfront / Marine City / Hwangnyeongsan roads
- 18:00 — 11 bus routes on Gwangnam-ro are suspended
- 18:30 — Gwangan Bridge lower deck, both directions
- 19:30 — parts of Gwangan-ro and Namcheonbadaro

Busan City says subway services will be increased and the last-train time extended by 25 minutes for the festival. Check the station and direction you need before travelling; do not assume every route has the same final departure.

Also: the Gwangalli M Drone Light Show is cancelled on Nov 7 because of the fireworks festival. The next show is scheduled for Nov 14.

Official traffic notice: https://www.busan.go.kr/nbnews/1761578
Full local guide: https://go2busan.com/guides/busan-fireworks-festival/
