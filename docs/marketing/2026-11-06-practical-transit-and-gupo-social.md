## Instagram carousel: Cashless buses in Busan

1. **Cover:** Taking a bus in Busan? Some routes are testing cashless boarding.
2. **The pilot:** Since October 1, 2026, 35 routes and 542 buses are included in a three-month pilot.
3. **Routes to notice:** Seated buses 1001 and 1003 are included, along with 24 regular routes.
4. **Cash-only?** Busan's policy allows passengers to board first and pay later by bank transfer.
5. **The catch for visitors:** If you do not have a Korean bank account, do not rely on the transfer workaround.
6. **The safer plan:** Get a T-money or Cashbee card before your first bus ride. Tap on boarding and when you get off.
7. **CTA:** Save this before your Busan trip. Read Go2Busan's transport-card guide.

Caption: Busan is testing cashless buses on 35 routes from October 1, 2026. The pilot includes seated routes 1001 and 1003. Cash-only passengers may board first and pay later by bank transfer under the city's policy, but that may not be practical for visitors without a Korean bank account. The easiest plan is to prepare a transport card before boarding. Check the current route list and service notices before you travel.

## Threads: Late KTX arrival at Busan Station

Arriving at Busan Station after 9 PM? Don't rely on a random “last bus” time from an old post.

For Haeundae, the usual metro route is Line 1 to Seomyeon, then Line 2 toward Jangsan — but check the last train on both legs for your actual travel date. Bus 1003 also serves Busan Station and the Haeundae area, but check the live stop and direction. It is included in Busan's cashless-bus pilot, so have a transport card ready. If public transport has ended or you have bulky luggage, use the official taxi rank or a recognised taxi app.

Bus information: https://bus.busan.go.kr/busanbims/

## Instagram carousel: Gupo Market day

1. **Cover:** Want to see a traditional market in Busan? Time your Gupo Market visit.
2. **Where:** Gupo Market, 7 Guposijang 2-gil, Buk-gu, Busan.
3. **Market days:** The current general Korea Tourism Organization listing shows the 3rd, 8th, 13th, 18th and 23rd of each month.
4. **A date to double-check:** Another KTO destination listing also includes the 28th. The two pages do not match, so confirm before making a special trip on the 28th.
5. **Opening hours:** KTO pages show different general hours and note that individual shop hours vary. Do not expect every stall to be open at the same time.
6. **What to explore:** Fresh produce, seafood, everyday goods and the Gupo guksu story.
7. **CTA:** Save this and pair the market with our Gupo guksu local food-history guide.

Caption: Gupo Market is a traditional market where permanent shops and periodic market days come together. The current general Korea Tourism Organization listing shows market days on the 3rd, 8th, 13th, 18th and 23rd. Another KTO listing includes the 28th, so double-check that date before planning your visit. Opening hours vary by source and by shop; go earlier in the day if you want to see more stalls.

KTO market page: https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=66092
