# Go2Busan Product and Growth Strategy

_Last updated: 2026-11-06_

## North-star goal

Build the travel-planning platform international visitors consult before visiting Busan and continue using while in the city.

Go2Busan is not intended to be only a travel blog. It should help visitors discover neighborhoods, choose accommodation, find local food, plan realistic routes, understand transport, and book relevant stays and activities.

## Business outcomes

1. Grow qualified international organic traffic.
2. Become a trusted, practical Busan planning resource.
3. Convert travel intent into accommodation and activity affiliate clicks and completed bookings.
4. Build repeat use through saved plans, useful tools, timely information, and email.
5. Develop direct advertising and sponsored-content revenue after audience and trust are established.

## Positioning

**Busan, Beyond the Tourist Map.**

Go2Busan combines:
- local perspective and practical on-the-ground guidance;
- well-known attractions with neighborhood discoveries;
- firsthand recommendations clearly distinguished from hearsay;
- useful transport, accommodation, food, and itinerary information;
- multilingual access for international visitors.

Potential supporting message: "Local recommendations, practical guides, and real Busan travel tips."

## Priority audiences

### 1. Taiwan — Traditional Chinese
Treat Taiwan as the first focused growth market based on the visitor-market research shared by the project owner. Build Taiwan-specific content rather than simply translating English pages. Prioritize food, shopping, markets, accommodation areas, transport, and 2–4 day itineraries. Use natural Taiwan Traditional Chinese and arrange native-language review for important landing pages.

### 2. Japan — Japanese
Prioritize short trips, weekend itineraries, food, shopping, transport, airport arrival, and accommodation-area decisions.

### 3. English-speaking visitors
Prioritize first-time Busan planning, accommodation choices, 3-day itineraries, transport, airport arrival, practical travel preparation, and neighborhood guides.

### 4. Simplified Chinese and other markets
Expand after validating demand, translation quality, and operational capacity.

Market rankings and visitor statistics are strategic inputs, not permanent facts. Recheck current official data before publishing numerical claims.

## Product pillars

### Discover
- neighborhood hubs;
- local and famous food;
- attractions, beaches, markets, shopping, cafes, and seasonal events;
- nearby day trips.

### Plan
- realistic multi-day itineraries;
- interactive map;
- saved places and shareable trip lists;
- budget and Visit Busan Pass calculators;
- transport and arrival guidance.

### Book
- neighborhood-based accommodation discovery;
- Agoda or other approved accommodation partners;
- Klook and other relevant activity partners;
- useful travel services where relevant.

### Return
- current-month Busan information;
- practical updates and travel alerts;
- visitor Q&A and public FAQ;
- email trip-preparation service;
- social video and photo content.

## Differentiation: local knowledge

Local food and place content should include, where available:
- neighborhood and category;
- what to order or experience;
- why it is recommended;
- firsthand visit status;
- practical access and nearby itinerary;
- current price/hours only when verified;
- last-checked date and source;
- original photography where available.

Never imply that the project owner or editor visited a venue unless that is confirmed. Clearly label recommendations, tips, and unverified leads.

## Monetization sequence

1. Accommodation affiliate revenue.
2. Tours and activity affiliate revenue.
3. Relevant travel-service partnerships.
4. Direct local business advertising and clearly disclosed sponsored content.
5. Own digital products such as practical Busan guides.

Do not treat outbound affiliate clicks as completed bookings. Measure clicks in analytics and use partner dashboards for bookings, cancellations, and commissions. Verify actual partner approval, link attribution, terms, and commission rates before making claims.

## Growth principles

- Fix technical discoverability and indexation before scaling content production.
- Build fewer, stronger, intent-matched pages rather than mass-producing thin pages.
- Create localized content for the actual needs of each market.
- Connect every useful guide to relevant neighborhood, itinerary, and booking pages.
- Put booking prompts where users are making a decision, not indiscriminately everywhere.
- Use original photography and credible local detail as a durable advantage.
- Build direct traffic and repeat-use mechanisms so growth does not depend only on Google.

## North-star and diagnostic metrics

- International organic users and sessions by country/language.
- Search impressions, clicks, and index coverage for priority URLs.
- Engagement with neighborhood, food, accommodation, and itinerary pages.
- Affiliate CTA views and clicks by page, language, partner, and placement.
- Completed bookings and revenue from partner reporting.
- Saved trips, shares, newsletter subscribers, and returning users.

Targets such as 100,000 monthly visitors are ambitions, not forecasts. Set quarterly milestones from observed Search Console and analytics baselines; revise them as evidence accumulates.
