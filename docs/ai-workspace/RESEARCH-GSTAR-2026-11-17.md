# G-STAR 2026 visitor guide research — checked 2026-11-17

## Content decision
The homepage already featured G-STAR 2026 but its CTA led to a generic Haeundae neighborhood guide. There was no dedicated G-STAR visitor page in EN/JA/zh-TW. Create one localized page set and link the homepage event cards directly to it.

## Officially verified facts
- Public BTC exhibition: 19–22 November 2026 at BEXCO.
- BTC general adult ticket: KRW 18,000.
- BTC youth ticket: KRW 8,000 for people born on or after 1 January 2008.
- BTC tickets are online advance sales only; no onsite BTC sales.
- Purchase deadline: 24:00 KST on the day before the booked visit.
- Each ticket is valid only on its booked date; purchase limit 2 tickets per person per day.
- Wristband must be collected at the onsite exchange desk.
- Ticket verification closes 17:00; last admission 17:30.
- November 19 opening time 11:00. The event operating regulations list 10:00–18:00 for other exhibition days.
- Youth ticket holders may be asked for proof of age/student status; the official English BTC page includes passport among example documents.
- BTC is public exhibition; BTB is separate business access, Nov 19–21.
- BEXCO Hall 1: Centum City Station Line 2 Exit 1, approx. 50 m; BEXCO Station (Busan Museum of Art) Exit 7, approx. 100 m.
- BEXCO Hall 2: Centum City Station Exit 1, approx. 380 m; BEXCO Station (Busan Museum of Art) Exit 5, approx. 453 m.
- From Busan Station, take Line 1 to Seomyeon and transfer to Line 2 for Centum City. No journey time or fare is quoted. Hall distances are checked against BEXCO's own official subway directions: https://www.bexco.co.kr/eng/CMS/Contents/Contents.do?mCode=MN064.
- Ticketlink receives name, email, user ID and phone number as part of ticket transaction. The public official page does not establish which overseas phone numbers or cards will pass, so the guide does not promise international checkout compatibility.
- Child free-admission birth-year threshold differs between the English and Korean ticket pages; deliberately omitted rather than publishing a contradictory eligibility rule.
