## Official route checks

- [2026 KSIEC Fall Meeting: BEXCO directions (PDF)](https://www.ksiec.or.kr/ver2022/_subpage/competition/docs/transportation.pdf), event dates 2026-10-28 to 2026-10-30: lists express Bus 1001 and regular Bus 40 from Busan Station to BEXCO, plus the Line 1 → Seomyeon → Line 2 route. The source includes historical estimates and fares; none are repeated in the guide.
- [World Congress of Education: BEXCO directions](https://www.wce.or.kr/location?lang=en), checked 2026-11-20: lists Bus 1001 from Busan Station to the Centum City/BEXCO stop and the metro transfer at Seomyeon. The 2026 WCE page notes that route/stop locations can change with on-site conditions.
- [Busan City: Public Transportation](https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/public-transportation), checked 2026-11-20: identifies Metro Lines 1–4, Donghae Line and its BEXCO → Songjeong sequence, and links to the official Busan Bus Information System's English interface.
- [Busan Geopark: Jangsan / Haeundae and Songjeong access](https://www.busan.go.kr/geopark_en/jangsan), checked 2026-11-20: lists 1003 for Haeundae Beach and 1001/1003 among buses serving Songjeong Beach. It is a destination access reference, not a live timetable.
- [Busan Bus Information System (English)](https://bus.busan.go.kr/busanBIMS/mobile/EN/webApp/page/main/main.asp) is the live arrival / vehicle-location reference. It does not expose seat availability.
- No fixed fares, travel times, first/last departures or guaranteed seating claims were added.

## Competitor / structure review

- [Busan.net G-STAR 2026 guide](https://busan.net/en/guide/gstar-2026/) (published 2026-10-25, updated 2026-11-17) leads with venue access, nearest stations, map links and FAQ. It omits Bus 40 as a Busan Station alternative, so it is used only for its scannable structure; no text or image was copied.
- The Go2Busan page answers by destination, separates Bus 40's Centum City/BEXCO role from the Haeundae and Songjeong rail routes, and makes clear that Bus 1003 is not guaranteed to be less crowded.
