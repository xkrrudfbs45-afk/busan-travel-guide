# Restaurant queue guidance — 2026-11-18

## Scope
Add practical restaurant-waiting guidance to the existing EN, JA and zh-TW neighborhood food guides. This is an enhancement to existing pages, not a new URL. No restaurant is promised a specific wait time or guaranteed booking method.

## Editorial decisions
- Explain that Tableling, Catchtable, in-store tablets, paper lists and staff-managed queues may all be used; the method varies by venue and can change.
- For a visitor who cannot complete a Korean-number / SMS verification flow, recommend showing the screen to staff and asking whether on-site registration is possible. Do not promise that staff can bypass verification.
- Recommend checking branch, opening time, break time, last order and final queue registration before travelling.
- Recommend arriving before opening for a must-visit restaurant, choosing a less busy meal time when possible, and keeping a nearby alternative.
- Include Korean phrases for joining a queue, explaining the lack of a Korean phone number, asking how many parties remain, and checking break / final registration times.
- Do not state that every restaurant supports overseas phone numbers, overseas cards, app reservations or walk-in registration.
