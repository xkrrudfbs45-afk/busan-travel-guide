# Taejongdae Danubi train — source conflict addendum (2026-11-22)

This addendum supersedes the conclusion in the SNS audit's Taejongdae section that the Danubi train is currently operating. The available official sources conflict; the restart status is not established by this check.

## Evidence

1. **Latest dated suspension notice located — Busan Tourism Association, 2026-07-10**
   - Notice: https://bta.or.kr/noticeDetail?no=1637&page=1
   - States that Danubi train service has been suspended from 2026-07-03 until the urgent safety inspection is completed.
   - States that a free circular shuttle began on 2026-07-04 and runs Tuesday–Sunday, 09:30–17:30, with free tickets issued per departure at the Danubi ticket office, first come first served.
   - This is a dated notice, not proof that the suspension is still active on 2026-11-22; no later restart notice was found in this check.

2. **Visit Busan attraction page — generic operating information**
   - Page: https://www.visitbusan.net/index.do?lang_cd=ko&menuCd=DOM_000000201013001000&uc_seq=542
   - Lists the Danubi train's regular schedule, Monday closure, weather-related stoppage, and off-season operation 09:20–17:30.
   - The page does not mention the 2026-07-03 safety suspension or provide a visible recent update date in the retrieved content. It may be generic or stale and does not, by itself, establish that the train restarted.

3. **Operator site**
   - https://www.bisco.or.kr/taejongdae/sub/sub02/sub02_4/
   - Timed out during this check, so no current operator status could be retrieved.

## Safe editorial treatment

- Do not change the guide to say the train is operating solely on the basis of the generic Visit Busan timetable.
- Do not state an unqualified present-tense suspension without a current operator update.
- Preferred wording: “The latest dated notice we could verify (10 July 2026) said the Danubi train was suspended for a safety inspection and a free circular shuttle was operating Tuesday–Sunday, 09:30–17:30. Check the operator's latest notice before travelling.”
- Keep the 11/26 Instagram wording as a prompt to check current service; do not promise that the train is running.
- If the shuttle timetable is shown, label it as the 10 July notice's timetable and retain the same-day verification warning.
