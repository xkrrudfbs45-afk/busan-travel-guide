# Airport limousine direction clarification — 2026-11-22

## Decision

Correct a misleading English direction label in the existing Gimhae Airport → Haeundae guide. The times 07:10–16:50 (Jangsan branch) and 10:10–21:40 (Gijang branch) are departures **from Gimhae Airport**, not buses travelling airport-bound. Japanese and Traditional Chinese already described them as airport departures; their official check dates and the Route 307 pilot-expiry check were refreshed to 2026-11-22.

No new URL was created. No fares, stops, route times, or luggage rules were changed.

## Official verification — 2026-11-22

- [Busan Metropolitan City, airport limousine revision effective 2026-01-05](https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/bsnews01/1714674): the route table explicitly labels the times as “from Airport”: Jangsan 07:10 / 16:50 and Gijang 10:10 / 21:40. The same notice lists the Seomyeon/Bujeon service from Airport at 07:20 / 21:30.
- [Gimhae International Airport, current public transportation page](https://www.airport.co.kr/gimhaeeng/cms/frCon/index.do?CONTENTS_NO=1&MENU_ID=110): headings state “Gimhae International Airport → To Haeundae/Gijang” and “Departure Time (From Gimhae International Airport International Terminal)”; the Jangsan and Gijang time lists match the Busan City notice.
- [Busan City, large-suitcase city-bus pilot notice dated 2026-07-07](https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/bsnews01/1739074): Route 85 pilot through 2026-09-30 and Route 307 pilot 2026-07-01 to 2026-09-30. No later official extension notice surfaced in the public official sources reviewed on 2026-11-22.
- Visit Busan's [English](https://www.visitbusan.net/en/index.do?menuCd=DOM_000000303012002000), [Japanese](https://www.visitbusan.net/ja/index.do?menuCd=DOM_000000404012003000), and [Traditional Chinese](https://www.visitbusan.net/zht/index.do?menuCd=DOM_000000603012002000) transport pages retain the ordinary city-bus hand-luggage guideline of under 20 kg and 50 × 40 × 20 cm. Larger luggage should use the subway, airport limousine or taxi.
