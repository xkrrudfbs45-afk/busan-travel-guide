## Decision

## Busan–Gimhae Light Rail tagless status — 2026-11-22

- [T-money official announcement, 2026-11-17](https://www.tmoney.co.kr/aeb/cmnctn/news/readNewsView.dev?bdSeqNo=4450): test gates at Gaya University and Daejeo; installation across all 21 stations planned by February 2027; formal launch targeted for late March 2027. Setup is through Mobile T-money on Android. The announcement does not specify eligibility for overseas app-store accounts, overseas devices or foreign-issued payment cards.
- The existing transit-card pages already distinguished this project from Busan City's separate Haeundae–Gijang Tagless Pay pilot. The Haeundae–Gijang pilot notice (2026-04-01) describes 360 buses and four metro gates, requires the Android-only Tagless Pay app, and says the normal tap method remains where tagless is not installed.
- No new product page was created. The copy now tells visitors to keep using the ordinary transit card/ticket method until the operator confirms the service at their station and publishes overseas-user eligibility.
