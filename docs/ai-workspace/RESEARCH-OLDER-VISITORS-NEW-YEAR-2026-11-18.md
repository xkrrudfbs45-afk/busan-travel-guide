# Older international visitors and New Year planning — 2026-11-18

## Editorial decision
Treat older visitors as a meaningful audience segment, but do not assume that age automatically means disability or reduced mobility. Give practical choice-based advice: walking distance, steps / slopes, elevator access, seating, toilets, weather exposure, transfer count, luggage and the return journey. Avoid patronising language and avoid describing a destination as fully accessible unless the exact route and facilities are verified.

## Official accessibility source
Visit Busan's barrier-free tourism guide:
https://www.visitbusan.net/en/index.do?lang_cd=en&menuCd=DOM_000000303011001000&uc_seq=310

The page describes:
- Haeundae Station → Busan Aquarium → Dongbaek Park Coastal Trail / Nurimaru APEC House → The Bay 101. It mentions elevators at Haeundae Station and a wheelchair-friendly Dongbaek Park coastal trail.
- Songdo Beach / Skywalk / Cable Car → Amnam Park Observatory. It describes an accessible beach entrance, accessible facilities and an accessible trail at Amnam Park; electric wheelchairs are not permitted on the marine cable car for safety, and the page recommends changing to a regular wheelchair available on site.
- Songjeong / Fisheries Science Museum / Orangdae / Daebyeon / Jukseong route.
- Yeongdo route with Jeoryeong Coastal Trail and Taejongdae; Danubi Train and wheelchair rental information is noted.
Use the page as route-planning reference, not as a blanket guarantee that every segment, entrance or facility is step-free or currently available. Link to the official page and tell readers to confirm details.

## Narae Bus — important eligibility caveat
Busan City notice (2026-03-13):
https://www.busan.go.kr/nbnews/1722699
The current regular-operation notice says Tuesdays and Wednesdays, two routes, and explicitly lists eligible users as **Busan residents with disabilities**, with up to two accompanying people including a guardian. Applications via https://naraebus.com/ and contact 051-791-2023.
Do NOT promote Narae Bus as a transport option for overseas tourists or all older travellers. Recheck eligibility and service conditions if mentioned in future.

## K.Ride
Official landing page: https://kride.kakaomobility.com/
- Lists Standard, Comfort (extra space) and Minivan vehicle categories, alongside Deluxe and Luxury.
- Lists app UI in English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese; destination search and driver chat translation support 133 languages.
- Promotes card / debit / mobile payment and automatic payment.
Google Play listing: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_US&id=com.kakaomobility.kride
- Current listing describes service in 35 countries and confirms four UI languages.
The official landing page and store listing differ in country count (34 vs 35); omit the number. Existing research indicates signup may require a mobile number and the official privacy policy does not confirm all foreign country codes. Do not promise that Korean-number verification is unnecessary. Advise visitors to test registration and payment before departure. Vehicle availability varies by location/time; do not guarantee Comfort or Minivan availability.
