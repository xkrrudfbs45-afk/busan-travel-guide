## Editorial wording
The existing route already describes steep slopes, stairs and uneven coastal paths. The new note makes the practical consequence explicit: large suitcases and most strollers are difficult to manage in narrow lanes and on repeated stair sections. Visitors should leave bulky luggage at a hotel or a storage location whose hours and conditions they have confirmed.

## Accuracy guardrails
- This is a practical pedestrian-access warning based on local visitor experience, not a municipal prohibition.
- Do not claim the village is closed to visitors on weekends.
- Do not repeat the unverified rumour that the coastal tunnel is fully closed on weekends.
- Temporary access restrictions may occur for weather or on-site safety; only describe them when a current official notice confirms them.
