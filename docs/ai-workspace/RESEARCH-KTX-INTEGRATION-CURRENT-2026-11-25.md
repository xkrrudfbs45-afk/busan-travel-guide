## Editorial findings

1. **The former SRT app wording was too easy to read as a current booking option.** The live EN / JA / zh-TW pages said that the old SRT app or website might still offer limited non-member general-ticket access during the transition. SR's 2026-08-07 integration Q&A says SRT tickets were sold only for trains running through 2026-08-31 and that journeys from 2026-09-01 are purchased as KTX through Korail+ or the Korail website. SR's 2026-08-21 notice describes the limited non-member access as a temporary transition exception. The revised copy now makes the date boundary explicit and avoids suggesting a separate SRT channel for current journeys.
- SR Q&A, 2026-08-07: https://www.srail.or.kr/cms/article/view.do?pageId=TK0502000000&pageIndex=12&postNo=913
- SR app / website transition notice, 2026-08-21: https://www.srail.or.kr/cms/article/view.do?pageId=TK0502000000&pageIndex=16&postNo=922

2. **Taiwan Traditional Chinese needed a clearer quick answer.** The zh-TW page already contained the official two-month rule under section 2, but the opening callout only said to check the official site and the section repeated the same two-month explanation twice. The revised page now puts the two-month rule and holiday exception in the opening callout and removes the duplicate paragraph.
- Korail press release, 2026-10-08: from 2026-10-01, foreign visitors' ordinary ticket advance-sale window expanded from one month to two months: https://info.korail.com/info/selectBbsNttView.do?bbsNo=199&integrDeptCode=&key=911&nttNo=27403
- MOLIT press release, 2026-09-20: https://www.molit.go.kr/USR/NEWS/dtl.jsp?id=95092440
- The Korail 2026 notice confirms the two-month window. The usual 07:00 KST opening time is retained as a normal-pattern caveat, not a guarantee; special holiday sales can use separate dates / times. The live booking form and dated notice remain authoritative for a specific trip.
