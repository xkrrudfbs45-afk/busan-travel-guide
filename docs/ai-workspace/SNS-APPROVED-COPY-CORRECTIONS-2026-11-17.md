## 2. Nov 24 — Korea plug and voltage

Issue: the approved cards claim C/F or C/SE plug types. The VisitKorea official page used for this audit confirms 220V, 60Hz and a socket with two round holes; the cited page does not substantiate the exact C/F/SE labels.

Proposed replacement card 3 body:

### zh-TW

```text
韓國標準電壓為220V、60Hz，插座是兩個圓孔。請確認電器支援的電壓，必要時準備轉接頭。
```

### JA

```text
韓国の標準電圧は220V・60Hz。コンセントは丸い穴が2つの形です。機器の対応電圧を確認し、必要なら変換プラグを用意してください。
```

### EN

```text
Korea uses 220V, 60Hz, with two round-pin holes. Check your device's voltage range and bring an adapter if needed.
```

Official source: https://japanese1.visitkorea.or.kr/jpn/AKR/AK_JPN_3_2.jsp
