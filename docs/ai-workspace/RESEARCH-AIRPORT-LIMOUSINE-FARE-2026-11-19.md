# Gimhae Airport limousine fare and route correction — 2026-11-19

## Why this batch was selected

Nine existing EN / JA / zh-TW airport pages repeated the same outdated claim: Airport Limousine 2 (Seomyeon–Bujeon) costs KRW 7,500, and the airport's KRW 6,000 listing should not be trusted. The official Busan City update and the current Gimhae Airport bus page now agree that the adult Line 2 fare is KRW 6,000. The pages also lacked a clear explanation that Line 1 splits into Jangsan- and Gijang-bound services, and that not every departure continues to Gijang / Osiria.

This is an accuracy correction to existing high-intent pages, not a new content page.

## Official verification — checked 2026-11-19

### Busan City update, effective 2026-01-05

[Busan Airport Limousine Buses Reorganized with Reference to Usage Patterns](https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/bsnews01/1714674)

- Line 1 (Haeundae–Gijang): adult fare KRW 9,500.
- Line 2 (Seomyeon–Bujeon): adult fare reduced from KRW 7,500 to **KRW 6,000** effective 5 January 2026.
- Line 2 headway: 60–80 minutes; route and stops unchanged.
- Line 1 dispatch is split between Jangsan and Gijang branches. The Gijang branch includes Dongam Back Gate (Ananti Cove) and Osiria Theme Park (Matie Osiria). Not every service continues to Gijang.
- Airport Limousine 1 and 2 are separate services; no free transfer applies.
- The notice lists Bustago QR booking, a ticket machine near Gate 4 on the first floor, and boarding with a transit or credit card if seats are available. Cash is not accepted.
- The notice lists up to two pieces of baggage per passenger, with combined weight below 30 kg. This is still an operator rule to reconfirm before travel.

### Gimhae Airport current bus page

[Gimhae International Airport — Public Transportation / Bus](https://www.airport.co.kr/gimhaeeng/cms/frCon/index.do?CONTENTS_NO=1&MENU_ID=110)

- Both limousine routes board at Platform 3, first floor, at the international and domestic terminals.
- Line 2: KRW 6,000 adult fare; airport departures listed 07:20–21:30; about 70 minutes between services.
- Line 1: KRW 9,500 adult fare; airport departures listed 07:10–21:40. The page separates Jangsan-bound and Gijang-bound departures and warns that some time slots do not operate to Gijang.
- The airport page currently labels payment as credit-card only and warns that timetables and fares may change. Do not promise that every overseas-issued card or every Bustago checkout will work.
