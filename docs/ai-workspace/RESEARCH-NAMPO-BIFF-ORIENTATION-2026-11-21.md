## What travelers are trying to understand

Across Taiwan, Japan and English-language travel discussions, visitors commonly group Nampo, BIFF Square, Jagalchi and Gukje Market into one half-day or evening walk. The word “Square” can mislead a first-time visitor into looking for one large plaza or treating each named stop as a separate destination.

## Official fact check (2026-11-21)

- **Visit Busan — Nampo/Jagalchi walking route:** describes BIFF Square as one of Busan’s cultural streets; lists the address around 58-1 Gudeok-ro, Jung-gu; says the street is open at all times while stalls and shops generally operate around 10:00–22:00; and describes the stops from Gukje Market through BIFF Square, Gwangbok-ro and Yongdusan Park as within walking distance. https://www.visitbusan.net/index.do?lang_cd=en&menuCd=DOM_000000202012001000&uc_seq=2809
- **Korea Tourism Organization — BIFF Square:** describes a 428-metre-long street and identifies film-related handprints/sculptures on the pavement. The page also notes that shop hours vary. The exact length was deliberately not added to the visitor copy because it is unnecessary for the orientation fix. https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/whereToGo/locIntrdn/rgnContentsView.do?menuSn=351&vcontsId=95411
- The official evidence supports calling BIFF Square a street and a walkable link between nearby markets. It does **not** establish a blanket vehicle-exclusion rule, so the copy does not call it a “pedestrian-only street.”

## Content change

Added a concise, localized orientation callout immediately after the BIFF snack item in the existing EN, JA and zh-TW Nampo guides:
- Explains that “Square” is a named street, not one large open plaza.
- Connects the stop to a walk between Jagalchi and Gukje Market.
- Mentions the film-festival handprints on the pavement.
- Separates all-hours street access from variable stall/shop business hours.
- Links the official Visit Busan route and the KTO BIFF Square guide.
