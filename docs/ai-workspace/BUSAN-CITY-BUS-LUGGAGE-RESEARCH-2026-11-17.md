## Official verification

- Visit Busan's English transport guide: https://www.visitbusan.net/en/index.do?menuCd=DOM_000000303012002000
- Visit Busan's Japanese transport guide: https://www.visitbusan.net/ja/index.do?menuCd=DOM_000000404012003000
- Visit Busan's Traditional Chinese transport guide: https://www.visitbusan.net/zht/index.do?menuCd=DOM_000000603012002000
- The three official-language guides state the ordinary city-bus hand-luggage guideline as under 20 kg per person and under 50 × 40 × 20 cm (approximately a 20-inch carry-on). Larger / bulky luggage should use the subway, airport limousine or taxi.
- Busan City notice (2026-07-07), English: https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/bsnews01/1739074
- Busan City notice (2026-07-07), Japanese: https://www.busan.go.kr/jpn/bsnews01/1739149
- Busan City notice (2026-07-07), Traditional Chinese: https://www.busan.go.kr/zht/bsnews01/1739076
- The published trial allowed one suitcase up to 30 inches per person on Route 85 through 2026-09-30 (off-peak only, excluding 07:00–09:00 and 17:00–19:00), and on Route 307 from 2026-07-01 through 2026-09-30 (all day). The notice does not publish an extension beyond 2026-09-30. As of 2026-11-17, no later official extension notice was found.
- Route 85 ran through Huinnyeoul Culture Village, Busan Station, Seomyeon and Jeonpo. Route 307 linked Gimhae Airport, Centum City, Haeundae and Songjeong. City bus 307 is not Airport Limousine 1; the limousine has its own luggage allowance and timetable.

- Dcard's October 2026 trip report (40K+ views when checked) says the writer reached Haeundae Beach Train but found it sold out despite holding a Visit Busan Pass. The report also describes confusion at the ticket / queue area. This is one traveller's experience, not proof that every pass holder will be unable to board.
- Official Visit Busan Pass page lists Haeundae Beach Train as a Group B free attraction; Sky Capsule is not on the free-attraction list. The official Blue Line Park booking page says the weekly ticket release is Tuesday 07:00 Korea time for the week four weeks later; only a small number of same-day tickets are sold on-site and may sell out early.
