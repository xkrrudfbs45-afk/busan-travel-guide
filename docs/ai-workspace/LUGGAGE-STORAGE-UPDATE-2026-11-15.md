# Busan luggage-storage update — 2026-11-15

## Decision

Update the existing EN, JA and zh-TW luggage-storage guides rather than create another page. Public travel questions repeatedly concern large suitcases, the location of staffed counters, late collection and how to avoid walking back across the city. The existing guide already covered Zimcarry, ParkZim and metro lockers, but did not explain the newly added KTX-station lockers or clearly separate the KTX station from the Line 1 subway station.

## Official and local verification

- [Busan Metropolitan City — luggage-storage directory](https://www.busan.go.kr/intobusan-BTS-BOF-en/checkroom), checked 15 November 2026:
  - Zimcarry is listed inside KTX Busan Station, first floor, inside Exit 5.
  - The city lists subway lockers on Lines 1–4 and publishes line-specific dimensions and base prices.
  - Line 1 extra-large locker: 43 × 90 × 60 cm (width × height × depth), base price KRW 6,000. Time-based charges vary by line; the kiosk remains the final price source.
- [Busan CBS, 26 July 2026](https://bs.nocutnews.co.kr/news/6553408): reports that blue extra-large lockers were added to the 2F waiting hall (바다맞이방) at KTX Busan Station and began operating on 24 July 2026.
- [Busan Ilbo, 26 July 2026](https://v.daum.net/v/20260726181904782): independently reports the 2F installation. Other local reporting on 31 July attributes a separate 144-unit installation to Korail Distribution, with a different total. The operator/scope and totals are not sufficiently reconciled; this update deliberately does not quote an aggregate locker count or an unverified price for the new KTX bank.
- The 2F KTX locker bank, the 1F Zimcarry staffed counter and the Line 1 subway locker bank are treated as separate options. K-Rambles' station-level guide places the Line 1 lockers on B1 near exits 3, 4 and 6; Busan City's page confirms dimensions and base prices but does not state that exact floor/exit location. The KTX bank's live price and availability must be checked at its kiosk.

## Changes in this PR

- EN, JA and zh-TW luggage-storage guides now distinguish:
  1. KTX Busan Station 2F blue extra-large self-service lockers;
  2. KTX Busan Station 1F Zimcarry staffed counter, inside Exit 5;
  3. Line 1 subway lockers as a separate facility, with official dimensions and base price.
- Added a direct warning that the new KTX locker bank's price and live availability are not confirmed by the cited report.
- Corrected the Line 1 small-locker width from 42 cm to the official 43 cm.
- Added the existing verified reference photograph to JA and zh-TW with localized captions. The photograph is from Busan Central Bus Terminal, not KTX Busan Station, and is explicitly labelled as an illustrative example.
- Technical metadata (title/description/OG/JSON-LD) and cover-image delivery (LCP/CLS, eager loading, dimensions, responsive formats) are intentionally delegated to Claude in issue #455; this content PR does not change those implementation details.
- No new page was created because the existing three-language luggage-storage guides already cover the search intent.

## SEO and user-value hypothesis

A visitor searching for Busan Station luggage storage needs to know which building/floor to use before dragging a suitcase through the station. Distinguishing KTX 2F, staffed Zimcarry 1F and subway B1 should reduce wrong-floor searches and unnecessary backtracking. The localized headings and descriptions make the distinction discoverable in English, Japanese and Traditional Chinese. The correction to the official Line 1 locker width reduces the risk of a suitcase not fitting.
