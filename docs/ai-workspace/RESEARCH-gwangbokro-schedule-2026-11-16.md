## Official fact and status
- Busan Jung-gu's 2026-09-08 re-notice for the lighting-installation / event-operation contract specifies a **planned lighting period of 2026-12-04 to 2027-01-31, 17:30–22:30**.
- The listed project area is Gwangbok-ro, Gwangbokjungang-ro and hillside-road sections in Bosu-dong, Daecheong-dong and Yeongju-dong.
- This is a procurement / operations specification, not the final public programme. It does not establish the lighting-ceremony time, performances, admission, parking or all visitor arrangements. The notice allows changes. Pages therefore label the period as planned and instruct visitors to check the latest district announcement.
- Official e-minwon notice URL: https://eminwon.bsjunggu.go.kr/emwp/gov/mogaha/ntis/web/ofr/action/OfrAction.do?context=NTIS&homepage_pbs_yn=Y&jndinm=OfrNotAncmtEJB&method=selectOfrNotAncmt&methodnm=selectOfrNotAncmtRegst&not_ancmt_mgt_no=30073&subCheck=Y
- Cross-check: Busan Jung-gu notice No. 2026-724 dated 2026-08-25 reproduces the same planned lighting dates and hours in the public tender text. Tender text copy: https://ggmadang.com/bid/R26BK01697974
- The district's public culture-tourism calendar still gives only the broad December 2026–January 2027 window; do not treat it as a detailed visitor programme.

## Competitive insight
Third-party event aggregators are already showing the tender-specified dates and hours as event facts. Go2Busan differentiates by labelling them as a **planned lighting schedule from procurement documents**, not a confirmed final programme. This avoids copying aggregator certainty and prevents readers from assuming performances, admission or parking are confirmed.
