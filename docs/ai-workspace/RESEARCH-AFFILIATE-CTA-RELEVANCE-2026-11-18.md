## Findings

### BTS in Busan — Klook Gamcheon hanbok rental

The existing EN / JA / zh-TW CTA points to Klook activity 44779, Gamcheon Culture Village hanbok rental. The current Klook EN-US page (checked 2026-11-18) identifies Gamcheon Culture Village as the destination, describes hanbok rental with basic hairstyling and accessories, and showed 4.9/5 from 42 reviews and 900+ bookings at the time of checking. A visible recent review is dated 2026-10-26.

- Official destination: https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/44779-busan-gamcheon-cultural-village-hanbok-rental/
- The BTS route guide features Gamcheon Culture Village, so this is a relevant optional experience rather than a generic city-wide product.
- Preserve the existing owner-supplied aid=141680 and aff_adid=1539686 and the localized Klook destination paths.
- The current CTA is disclosed and uses rel="sponsored nofollow noopener noreferrer" through the build-time injector.
- Do not copy dynamic rating / booking counts into Go2Busan copy; they can change.

### Haeundae food — Agoda hotel search

The existing EN / JA / zh-TW Haeundae food CTA uses the owner-supplied Agoda CID 1979955, Busan city ID 17172, and locale mapping in content/affiliate/agoda-placements.json. The build-time URL generated by scripts/inject-agoda-cta.mjs contains the city parameter but no Haeundae-area filter or property-specific deep link.

- The Agoda partner-search URL was not accessible to the public web inspection tool in this pass, so the final landing-page UI and search results were not independently confirmed.
- Do not claim that the link opens a Haeundae-only result set.
- This PR changes only the localized CTA copy: it now explicitly says the link opens a Busan-wide search and tells visitors to filter to Haeundae before comparing the walk to the station, beach and food stops.
- Keep CID, city ID and locale parameters unchanged. Do not invent a property ID or area filter.

### Pusan National University food — no forced CTA

The zh-TW Pusan National University food guide has 18 GSC impressions at average position 4.8 in the owner-provided 28-day snapshot in #530, but the audit did not identify a bookable product whose actual destination is the PNU food neighborhood. Search results surfaced food experiences in Haeundae and Nampo, not PNU; those are not a good match for this page.

- Leave the page without an affiliate CTA for now.
- Reconsider only if a directly relevant, bookable PNU-area food experience is verified.
