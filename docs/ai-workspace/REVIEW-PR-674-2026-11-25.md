## Official fact checks

- Busan standard medium-taxi fare: [Busan City taxi fare table](https://www.busan.go.kr/depart/ahtaxi03). The current table lists ₩4,800 for the first 2 km, ₩100 per 132 m, and ₩100 per 33 seconds at 15 km/h or below. Late-night surcharge applies 23:00–04:00: 20% outside 00:00–02:00 and 30% from 00:00–02:00. Deluxe / large taxis have a different fare table.
- Tourist complaints: [Korea Tourism Organization Tourist Complaint Center](https://www.touristcomplaint.or.kr/en/intro). It accepts complaints about wrongful conduct in tourist facilities and transport. The 1330 helpline is listed as 1330 in Korea and +82-2-1330 from overseas, with Korean, English, Japanese and Chinese language support. A complaint is not an instant refund channel.
- Transfer rules and transit cards: [Visit Busan transport guide](https://www.visitbusan.net/en/index.do?menuCd=DOM_000000304011003000). The general guide describes a 30-minute transfer window and up to two transfers for eligible journeys, with exit taps required for transfer discounts.
- Foreign-card metro tickets: [Busan City payment update](https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/bsnews01/1764153). The city distinguishes single-journey ticket-machine card payments from rechargeable T-money / Cashbee top-ups and from direct contactless gate entry. The December extension has no exact activation date in the cited notice.
- City-bus QR fares: [Busan City ZeroPay notice](https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/bsnews01/1758410). QR bus payment is separate from transit-card transfers; group passengers pay individually and QR fares do not receive transit-card transfer discounts.
- Winter events: [13th Haeundae Light Festival](https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/bsevents/1762645) is listed for 2026-11-28 to 2027-01-10, 18:00–23:00, free. [Busan City Daily Busan update](https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/index/bsnews01/1763769) lists the Gwangbok-ro Street Festa on 2026-12-05 from 16:00–22:00, while the separate English event calendar still says 16:00–20:00. Keep the discrepancy explicit until the organizer reconciles it.
