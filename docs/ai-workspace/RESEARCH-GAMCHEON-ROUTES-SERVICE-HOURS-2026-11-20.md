## Official verification — 2026-11-20

- [Visit Busan Tourist Information Center (English)](https://www.visitbusan.net/index.do?lang_cd=en&menuCd=DOM_000000203016001000&uio_seq=6): lists 09:00–17:00 in November–February for the tourist-guide and baggage-storage service; 09:00–18:00 in March–October.
- [Gamcheon Culture Village official directions](https://www.gamcheon.or.kr/gamcheon/page/user/intro4): lists the centre itself as 09:00–18:00 year-round, phone 051-204-1444, village buses Seo-gu 2 / 2-2 and Saha 1 / 1-1, Toseong Station Exit 6 and Jagalchi Station Exit 1 (Chungmu Market). It also says large buses require advance reservation and parking is limited.
- The two official pages describe different service layers and should not be collapsed into one blanket village closing time. The page now recommends 17:00 as the safer winter cutoff only when a traveller needs the guide or baggage-storage service.
- The village remains a residential neighborhood; this change does not imply that every lane or shop has a common opening time.
