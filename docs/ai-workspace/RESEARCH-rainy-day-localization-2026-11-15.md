# Rainy-day guide localization research — 2026-11-15

## Decision

Improve the existing EN, JA and zh-TW rainy-day pages in place. Do not create another page: the three localized URLs already exist, are linked by reciprocal hreflang in `sitemap.xml`, and are present in `assets/data/search-index.json`.

The existing copy already advises visitors to choose one indoor anchor and avoid cross-city travel in heavy rain. The gap was practical localization: it did not explain which indoor anchors fit which district, or the family-specific entry, stroller, luggage and reservation restrictions that can make an apparently good option unusable.

## Official fact checks — checked 2026-11-15

- SEA LIFE Busan: [opening hours and last entry](https://www.visitsealife.com/busan/en/plan-your-visit/before-you-visit/opening-hours/) lists different weekday/weekend hours and last entry one hour before closing. [Accessibility FAQ](https://www.visitsealife.com/busan/en/plan-your-visit/information/help-center/) says visitors may bring their own stroller, no stroller rental is offered, and wheelchairs are first-come at the ticket counter.
- Spa Land Centum City: [Shinsegae official visitor information](https://s.shinsegae.com/store/entertainment/centum-spaland.do?storeCd=SC00008) says elementary-school age and older may enter, elementary-school children require a same-sex guardian, and large suitcases must be stored in separate suitcase lockers before entering the locker room.
- Busan Modern & Contemporary History Museum: [official visitor information](https://www.busan.go.kr/mmch_eng/exhibition-information) lists free general admission except special exhibitions and Monday closure (with the next-day holiday exception). Its [Interactive Kids' Zone](https://www.busan.go.kr/mmch_eng/interactive-kids-zone) is for ages 5–8 and requires an appointment.
- National Maritime Museum: [official website notice](https://www.mmk.or.kr/eng/) says online service is temporarily suspended from 2026-10-26 through 2026-12-28, but this is a website interruption, not a stated museum closure. The notice lists Tue–Fri 09:00–18:00 (last entry 17:30), weekends / public holidays 09:00–19:00 (last entry 18:30), and Monday closure (next weekday if Monday is a public holiday). The Children's Museum is Tue–Sun 10:00–17:00; individual visits are on-site first-come at the second-floor desk, groups require a phone reservation two days ahead on weekdays, and the notice lists +82-51-309-1902 for enquiries. Busan City's [English visitor listing](https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/bsfacility01/795637) describes general admission as free. The pages explicitly date the website interruption as checked 2026-11-15.
- Jagalchi Market: [Busan City official listing](https://www.busan.go.kr/geopark_en/dudo) confirms the modern market building and Line 1 access. The copy distinguishes the building from surrounding open-air stalls and alleys.
- ARTE Museum Busan: [official page](https://kr.artemuseum.com/busan) lists daily 10:00–20:00 operation and last entry at 19:00 at the time checked. No price was copied.
- Korea Meteorological Administration: [English short-range forecast](https://www.weather.go.kr/neng/forecast/short-term.do) supports local-area selection; linked from the coastal-weather advice in all three locales.

## Changes made

- Updated the existing English `/guides/busan-rainy-day-guide/`, Japanese `/ja/guides/rainy-day/` and Traditional Chinese `/zh-tw/guides/rainy-day/` body content.
- Added district-based options: Haeundae / Centum City, Nampo / old downtown, Yeongdo and Seomyeon.
- Added family-use constraints for Spa Land, SEA LIFE Busan, the Busan Modern & Contemporary History Museum Kids' Zone and the National Maritime Museum website interruption.
- Kept all schedules, prices and booking links out of the core route recommendation except where an official, dated caveat materially helps the visitor.
- Updated the visible review date on JA and zh-TW pages to 2026-11-15.
- No new images were added. Existing Haeundae rainy-street image remains in use; its existing registry entry records Anton Strogonoff / CC BY 2.0, commercial use permitted with attribution, checked 2026-11-15.

## SEO / user-value hypothesis

- Better match localized intent such as `Busan rainy day with kids`, `釜山 雨の日 子連れ`, `釜山雨天景點` and `釜山室內景點` without creating duplicate URLs.
- Reduce itinerary failure caused by crossing the city in heavy rain or choosing a venue that does not fit a child's age, stroller or luggage.
- Improve internal discovery through the existing family, Spa Land and Jagalchi guides.
- No traffic or conversion lift is claimed. GSC/GA4 were intentionally not queried in this recurring run.
