# Social content pack — Busan transit payments (2026-11-25)

## Editorial guardrails
- The foreign-card rollout applies to **single-journey tickets bought at Busan Metro ticket machines**. It does not mean a foreign bank card can be tapped directly at metro gates or used to top up T-money/Cashbee.
- As of 25 November 2026, Line 4 and Line 1 from Dongmae to Dadaepo Beach are still excluded. Busan City says these sections are planned for December after payment modules are installed; no exact start date is published.
- Ticket-machine payment methods listed by Busan City: overseas-issued Visa, Mastercard and JCB; Kakao Pay, Naver Pay, Samsung Pay, Apple Pay, WeChat Pay and Alipay. Wallet acceptance can still depend on the user's account and issuer.
- Busan city buses also accept overseas QR payments through ZeroPay with supported apps including Alipay and WeChat Pay. Each passenger pays individually and QR fare payment does **not** receive a transit-card transfer discount.
- For normal transit-card transfers, tap off when leaving a bus. The general published transfer window is 30 minutes and up to two transfers. Use one card per person as the simple default.
