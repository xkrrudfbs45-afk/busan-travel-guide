## Publishing cadence
- Week 1: Pins 1, 2, and 3.
- Week 2: Pins 4 and 5, plus one fresh visual variation for the best-performing topic.
- Weeks 3–4: Publish 2–3 pins per week, each with a distinct useful angle; avoid near-duplicate mass posting.
- Review outbound clicks and saves after 30 days. Do not judge success by impressions alone.

## Design rules
- Use a vertical 2:3 image where possible, readable headline overlay, and one clear promise.
- Keep the brand mark small and consistent.
- The visual must match the destination article. Do not imply that an illustration is a documentary photograph.
- Use the canonical page with UTM parameters as the pin destination; keep the UTM naming consistent.
- Publishing these drafts still requires a user or authorized integration to post them to Pinterest.
