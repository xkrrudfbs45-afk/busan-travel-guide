### Slide 2 — Gwangalli
**Want a different view of the drone show?**
Gwangalli Beach is the official viewing area, but your exact view depends on buildings, distance and crowd position. Consider the wider waterfront and book a café or hotel view only after confirming that it faces the show and has access for non-guests.
**Before you go:** Check the official schedule and same-day weather notice. Shows can be delayed or canceled.

### Slide 3 — Blue Line Park
**Don’t assume the “reverse direction” is always faster**
Sky Capsule and Beach Train have different ticket and boarding rules. Check your exact product, departure station and time before travelling. Intermediate Beach Train stations may use first-come, first-served boarding, and re-boarding waits can grow when busy.
**Before you go:** Use the official timetable and arrive early for your booked time.

### Slide 4 — Seafood
**Ask for the total before you order**
At Jagalchi and nearby seafood restaurants, pricing and service arrangements vary by venue. Ask whether the quote includes preparation, table-setting charges and side dishes.
Korean phrase: **조리비와 상차림 비용까지 포함한 총액이 얼마인가요?**
“What is the total, including cooking and table-setting charges?”

### Slide 5 — Pork soup
**Taste the broth first**
Dwaeji gukbap often arrives with seasonings on the side. Taste first, then add salted shrimp, seasoning paste or chives gradually. Every restaurant’s broth and condiments are a little different.
Korean phrase: **새우젓은 따로 주세요.** — “Please serve the salted shrimp separately.”

### Slide 6 — Spa Land
**Make a rainy afternoon your spa plan**
Centum City’s Spa Land can be a useful indoor option. Allow enough time to enjoy the facilities, and check current admission, operating hours and entry rules before setting the rest of the day around it. Weekday crowds vary; no time is guaranteed to be quiet.

### Slide 7 — Luggage
**Travel lighter between neighborhoods**
Zimcarry lists a staffed Busan Station location and offers luggage delivery/storage services. Check your exact hotel or destination, booking cut-off, service coverage, baggage conditions and opening hours before relying on same-day delivery.
**Before you go:** Keep passports, medication, valuables and essentials with you.

### Slide 8 — Night views
**Choose the view—and plan the way back**
Hwangnyeongsan and Yeongdo viewpoints offer different city and harbor perspectives. Check weather, access conditions, lighting and return transport. Use marked public routes and respect residential areas.

### Slide 9 — Yonggungsa & Taejongdae
**Earlier can help, but check the rules**
For Haedong Yonggungsa, verify current opening/access information and the sunrise time before planning an early visit. At Taejongdae, check the Danubi train’s operating status, route and queues before deciding whether to walk or use another permitted transport option.

### Slide 10 — CTA
**Plan Busan by neighborhood**
Seomyeon for central connections, Haeundae for the east coast, Nampo-dong/Jagalchi for markets and old downtown, and Gwangalli for a waterfront evening.
