## Verified facts
- 13th Haeundae Light Festival, theme: Deep Dive.
- Dates: 2026-11-28 to 2027-01-10.
- Lighting hours: 18:00–23:00.
- Area: Haeundae Beach and Haeundae Square / Gunam-ro.
- Opening ceremony: 2026-11-28 at 18:00, on Haeundae Beach.
- Free admission.
- Organizer/contact: Haeundae-gu Office, +82-51-749-4062.
- Source: Busan City event listing https://www.busan.go.kr/eng/bsevents/1762645
- Access: Haeundae Station, Metro Line 2, exits 3 or 5; follow Gunam-ro towards the beach. Official access info: https://www.haeundae.go.kr/tour/index.do?menuCd=DOM_000000309006004000
- Important: 23:00 is the listed lighting end time, not the last train time. Readers must check the last train for their own destination.
