# Go2Busan Threads — 30-Day Auto-Posting Calendar (Japan + USA)

**Purpose:** Ready-to-review copy for the two Go2Busan Threads accounts. Each account has 2 posts per day for 30 days: 60 Japanese posts and 60 English posts (120 total).

**Status:** Content calendar only. This file does not connect to Threads, schedule, or publish posts.

## Operating rules for Claude

1. Treat each post as a standalone Threads post. Keep the line breaks and punctuation as written.
2. Publish two posts per account per day, using the account's local audience time:
   - Japan: start with 08:00 and 20:00 Japan Standard Time.
   - USA: start with 08:00 and 18:00 Eastern Time as a test schedule. This is a broad starting point for a multi-time-zone audience, not a proven optimum. Review analytics after 14 days and adjust.
3. Use Day 1–30 as a rolling calendar from the agreed launch date. Do not assume the launch date.
4. Do not post both language versions to the same account. Japanese copy goes only to Japan; English copy goes only to USA.
5. Keep the voice helpful, curious, local-minded, and conversational. Avoid making every post an advertisement. Use a question or invite a reply where it feels natural.
6. Do not invent current prices, opening hours, event dates, transit fares, restaurant availability, or claims such as “the best” and “secret.” If a post depends on a time-sensitive fact, verify it before publishing or replace it with an evergreen post.
7. Website links are intentionally not attached to every post. Use the profile link as the default CTA. Add a direct localized guide URL only after checking that the page exists and matches the post.
8. Track per post: publish time, post ID/URL, views/reach if available, replies, likes, reposts, profile visits, and site sessions/clicks. Never report unobserved metrics as fact.
9. Midway updates are expected. At Day 14, review results and replace underperforming or time-sensitive posts. Keep a changelog of replacements and never silently overwrite published copy.
10. Do not publish more than the planned two posts per account per day unless the owner explicitly changes the cadence.

## Day 13 — Jagalchi market know-how

### Japan · Post 1 (08:00 JST)

### Japan · Post 2 (18:00 JST)
「いくらですか？」だけで終わらせず、何を何グラム買うのか、調理や席料が別かも確認。魚介を量ったり調理したりする前に、追加料金込みの総額を見せてもらいましょう。

### USA · Post 1 (08:00 ET)

### USA · Post 2 (18:00 ET)
A simple question can prevent confusion at a seafood market: “What’s the total including preparation and table fees?” Compare the final meal cost—not just the seafood price—and don’t assume every stall uses the same pricing model.
