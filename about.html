Go2Busan is a Busan-based travel guide created for visitors who want more than a checklist of famous sights. We share practical travel information, local food guides and neighborhood ideas to help you explore the city with more confidence.

A local perspective on Busan

Because Go2Busan is operated from Busan, our editorial focus starts with the city itself: how its neighborhoods differ, what to know before visiting markets and restaurants, how to move between areas, and how to plan a realistic day without unnecessary backtracking.

We cover Busan food and everyday local specialties alongside essential travel logistics such as airport transfers, accommodation areas, public transportation, luggage storage and itineraries. Our aim is to make local context useful to international visitors, not simply repeat a list of attractions.

What you can expect

  • Local food and markets: explanations of dishes, neighborhood food areas, ordering tips and practical checks before you visit.
  • Neighborhood guidance: the trade-offs between areas such as Seomyeon, Nampo and Haeundae, based on the kind of trip you want.
  • Useful travel details: route comparisons, planning advice and links to official information when available.
  • Clear uncertainty: opening hours, fares, schedules and policies can change, so we flag details that should be checked before travel.

Our editorial approach

Local perspective does not mean every recommendation is a personal endorsement or a first-hand visit. We aim to distinguish official information, published reporting and practical editorial guidance, and to avoid presenting unverified claims as personal experience. If you spot an outdated detail or have a useful local suggestion, please contact us.
