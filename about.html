Our approach

Busan travel advice, made clearer.

Go2Busan is an independent travel guide for visitors who want to spend less time solving logistics and more time enjoying South Korea’s coastal city.

What we focus on

We create practical guides about getting from the airport to your accommodation, choosing a neighborhood, using public transportation and planning days that make geographic sense.

How we work

We aim to explain trade-offs, flag details that may change and link to official sources where possible. Transport schedules, fares, passes and local policies can change, so check linked official information before you travel.

Editorial independence

Some pages may eventually include affiliate links. If a reader makes a qualifying purchase through those links, the site may earn a commission at no additional cost to the reader. Commercial relationships do not determine our editorial recommendations.
