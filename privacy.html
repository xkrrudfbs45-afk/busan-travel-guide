Privacy policy

Go2Busan is a travel information website. This policy explains the types of technical information that may be processed when you visit the site and what to expect when you follow links to third-party services. Site features and data practices may change, so this page is reviewed when relevant tools are added or updated.

Information and server logs

The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages and timestamps as part of delivering and securing the website.

Analytics

Go2Busan offers optional Google Analytics 4 (GA4) measurement to understand which guides are useful and improve the site. The GA4 tag is not loaded unless you choose “Allow analytics” in the privacy prompt. If you reject analytics, the tag is not loaded and the site remains usable. If you opt in, Google may process usage information such as page views, device and browser details, approximate location, referral source and interactions. Search text entered into Go2Busan’s on-site guide search is processed in your browser; the site does not send the raw query text to GA4. If you opt in, GA4 may receive search interaction events, result counts and the title or URL of a result you click. Your choice is stored in your browser. You can revisit your choice using the “Change analytics choices” control in the footer. If you withdraw consent after opting in, the site stops sending analytics events and attempts to remove GA-related cookies from this browser. Google’s own terms and privacy practices apply to data it processes. The hosting provider may separately process technical server logs for service delivery and security.

Affiliate links and third-party services

Some pages contain affiliate links to third-party booking or travel services. If you follow an affiliate link, the destination provider may receive technical information about your visit and may use cookies or similar technologies to attribute a referral or process a booking. Go2Busan may earn a commission from qualifying purchases at no extra cost to you. The provider’s own privacy policy and terms apply after you leave Go2Busan. We do not receive your full payment details through these links.

Go2Busan does not currently configure on-site advertising or a contact form that collects personal information. If these features are introduced, this policy will be reviewed and updated.

Embedded maps

The Busan map page shows an embedded Google Map only after you choose “Show the interactive map”. Until then, no Google Maps content loads on that page. Once it loads, Google may process technical information about your visit and use cookies under its own terms and privacy policy. Links that say “Open in Google Maps” or “Directions” take you to Google Maps.

External links

Our guides may link to external websites, including official transport and tourism sources. We do not control the privacy practices of external sites.

Privacy questions

For privacy-related questions, email go2busan.travel@gmail.com. Do not send payment details or other sensitive information by email.

Policy updates

This policy was last reviewed on November 6, 2026. It should be reviewed whenever the site’s tools or data practices change.
