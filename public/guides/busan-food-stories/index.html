Kkomjangeo: a charcoal-grilled food with a Busan story

Busan Metropolitan City’s local-history account connects grilled hagfish (kkomjangeo) with the post-Korean War years around Jagalchi. Refugees and people rebuilding their lives in the port district are described as grilling hagfish over charcoal, helping turn an inexpensive ingredient into a familiar meal. This is the city’s documented account of the dish’s local history, not a claim that one moment explains every version of the tradition.

Gijang has a distinct style: Korea Tourism Organization describes jipbul gomjangeo, hagfish roasted over straw, as a specialty associated with Gongsu Village. The cooking method differs from the charcoal-grilled market style. Check current restaurant hours and menus before travelling. For market ordering and seafood billing, see the Jagalchi Market practical guide.
