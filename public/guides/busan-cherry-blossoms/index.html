Busan Cherry Blossoms 2027: When to Go and Where to See Them

Busan’s cherry blossoms usually arrive in late March and are gone within a couple of weeks, so timing matters more than the spot list. This guide groups Busan City’s recommended blossom spots by area and explains which 2027 details are not announced yet.

When do cherry blossoms bloom in Busan?

Busan is one of the earliest major Korean cities to bloom. Busan City’s 2023 spring flower map put the start of cherry blossoms around 24 March, with canola flowers following from late March into early April. Exact timing moves every year with the weather, so treat late March to the first week of April as the usual window rather than a fixed date.

Blossoms do not last long. Rain and strong coastal wind can strip trees within days, so if you can, keep two or three possible days for blossom walks instead of planning everything around a single date.

For 2027, watch the Korea Meteorological Administration (opens in a new tab) spring forecast and Busan City’s event calendar. We will update this guide when official dates are published.

Where to see cherry blossoms, by area

The spots below come from Busan City’s official spring blossom map. Group them by area so you are not crossing the city for one photo.

Haeundae side: Dalmaji-gil and seaside streets

  • Dalmaji-gil (Dalmaji Street) — a hillside road above Haeundae with sea views between the trees. Easy to pair with Haeundae Beach or the Blueline Park area.
  • Haeundae Marina apartment area and Haeundae Arboretum — quieter options listed on the city map.

Namcheon-dong and the Gwangalli side

  • Samik Beach Apartment cherry blossom street, Namcheon-dong — one of Busan’s best-known blossom tunnels. It is a residential complex: keep voices down, do not block car entrances and avoid standing in the road for photos.
  • Pukyong National University Daeyeon Campus — a campus option on the same side of the city.

Dongnae and Yeonje: Oncheoncheon

  • Oncheoncheon Park and Oncheoncheon Cafe Street — a long streamside walk with cafes nearby, good for an easy half day. Pair it with a Dongnae visit.

Viewpoints: Hwangnyeongsan

  • Hwangnyeongsan Mountain — cherry blossoms and azaleas on the road up, with a city night view at the top. The road is not a short walk from the subway; most visitors use a taxi.

Nakdong River side: wide riverside trails

  • Daejeo Ecological Park (Gangseo-gu) — about 12 km of cherry-lined riverside road plus large canola-flower fields. Busan City lists Line 3 Gangseo-gu Office Station, Exit 1, for the festival venue.
  • Samnak Ecological Park (Sasang-gu) and Maekdo Ecological Park — riverside parks with long blossom paths.

Busan City’s map also lists Gupo embankment road, Gaegeum’s cherry blossom deck road, Busan Democracy Park, Hwamyeong Arboretum and the road to Jangansa Temple. Check each spot on a map app before you go, since the city map does not give transit directions for most of them.

Festival dates to watch

District festivals are announced each year, usually a few weeks before the blossoms. For reference, the 9th Nakdonggang Riverside Cherry Blossom Festival (opens in a new tab) ran on 3–5 April 2026 at Daejeo Ecological Park, with free admission. Busan City’s 2023 map also listed festivals at Samnak Ecological Park and Yunsan in Geumjeong-gu. Do not assume the 2027 dates will match; check the organizer’s notice in March.

The famous Jinhae Gunhangje cherry blossom festival is in Changwon, outside Busan. It is a popular day trip but draws very heavy crowds, so check Changwon’s official schedule and allow extra travel time if you add it.

Tips for a smoother blossom day

  • Go on a weekday morning if you can. Namcheon-dong, Oncheoncheon and Daejeo get busy on sunny weekends.
  • Use the subway and walk the last stretch. Parking near popular spots fills up quickly in peak week.
  • Pack a light layer. Evenings by the sea and the river can still feel cold in early April.
  • Be considerate in residential streets and do not pick or shake branches for photos.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to see cherry blossoms in Busan?

Usually late March to the first week of April. Busan City’s 2023 map put the first blooms around 24 March, but the timing changes every year, so check the 2027 forecast in February–March.

Are the 2027 festival dates confirmed?

Not yet. For reference, the Nakdonggang Riverside Cherry Blossom Festival ran on 3–5 April 2026 at Daejeo Ecological Park. Check the organizer’s notice before travelling.

Which spot is easiest for a short visit?

Namcheon-dong’s Samik Beach Apartment street and Oncheoncheon are compact walks within the city. Daejeo Ecological Park is larger and best if you have half a day.
