WINTER IN BUSAN · DECEMBER 2026

Busan December Winter Festivals & Events 2026

Five seasonal events for a winter evening in Busan—from beachside lights to a Christmas village and performances along Gwangbok-ro.

Official information checked 27 November 2026. Programmes may change.

Page last updated: Nov 27, 2026

Before you go: Outdoor programmes can change with weather or site conditions. Recheck the organiser’s latest notice and transit service before travelling.

1. 13th Haeundae Light Festival

Dates: 28 Nov 2026–10 Jan 2027 · Hours: 18:00–23:00 · Admission: Free

Where: Gunam-ro / Haeundae Square and Haeundae Beach, Haeundae-gu. Explore the illuminated street and beach installations; allow time to walk between areas.

Transit: Busan Metro Line 2, Haeundae Station for Gunam-ro; beach installations are a walk from the station. Check station exits and walking directions on the day.

Official Busan event listing

2. Christmas Village Busan 2026

Dates: 26 Nov–25 Dec · Venue: Busan Cinema Center outdoor plaza, Haeundae-gu. Weekday hours listed as 16:00–22:00; weekends and 24–25 Dec 11:00–22:00. The operating calendar is date-specific—confirm your ticket day and time before going.

Admission: Tickets listed at ₩5,900–₩14,900; children under 36 months free with eligibility proof. Advance timed tickets are required.

Transit: Metro Line 2, Centum City Station; use the venue’s current directions to reach the Cinema Center.

Busan City event listing · Ticket notice

3. December Street Festa & Balcony Music Show

Street Festa: 5 Dec, 16:00–22:00. Balcony Music Shows: 12 and 19 Dec, 18:00–21:00. Admission: Free public street event.

Where: Gwangbok-ro pedestrian street, Jung-gu (Nampo area). Street performances, parade, pop-up performances and activities on 5 Dec; balcony concerts on 12 and 19 Dec.

Transit: Busan Metro Line 1, Nampo Station; walk toward Gwangbok-ro.

Visit Busan official programme

4. Gwangbok-ro Winter Light Tree Festival

Dates: 4 Dec 2026–31 Jan 2027 · Hours: 17:30–22:30 · Admission: Free.

Where: Gwangbok-ro and Gwangbokjungang-ro, Jung-gu. Large tree, street lighting and illuminated sculptures.

Transit: Metro Line 1, Nampo Station, then walk along the shopping streets.

Visit Busan official event page

5. Millak Luce Festa: Camellia Flower Festival

Dates: 14 Nov 2026–10 Jan 2027. The city’s English event listing reports daily 18:00–23:30 and free admission; confirm details for the specific evening.

Where: Millak Waterside Park, Suyeong-gu. Transit: Bus routes and walking access vary by entry point; use a map for Millak Waterside Park and check the route from your nearest station.

Busan City official listing

Make an evening plan

Group events by area: Haeundae and Centum City are convenient to combine on a separate night; Nampo’s Gwangbok-ro lights and street performances can be paired with nearby markets. Leave a buffer for walking, queues and the last train.
