Yeongdo, Busan: A One-Day Coastal Walk from Huinnyeoul to Taejongdae

Yeongdo is more than a photo stop. Pair a hillside seaside village with a coastal path and the cliffs of Taejongdae for a day that shows a quieter, maritime side of Busan.

Busan, South KoreaIndependent travel guideUpdated November 2026

Page last updated: Nov 23, 2026

Before you go: Yeongdo's sights are spread across the island. This route is designed as a sequence of separate stops connected by local transport—not one continuous short walk.

Start with the island's maritime story

If you have time before heading west, Kangkangee Arts Village near the old ship-repair area offers a different introduction to Yeongdo's working waterfront. Treat it as an optional first stop rather than part of the coastal walking trail; check current access and exhibition information before going.

Huinnyeoul Culture Village and the coastal path

Huinnyeoul Culture Village sits along a steep coastal slope and is known for narrow lanes, sea-facing viewpoints and small cultural spaces. The Korea Tourism Organization lists a coastal route of approximately 5.37 km linking the Jeoryeonghaean Coastal Walking Trail, Huinnyeoul Culture Village, Haneul Observatory and Taejongdae. Treat that as a route description, not confirmation that every lower-path section is open today. Allow time for stairs, slopes and photo stops. This is a residential neighbourhood: keep noise low, do not block doorways, and follow signs around private homes.

Check the lower coastal path before planning a continuous walk. Huinnyeoul’s village lanes and the lower Jeoryeong Coastal Trail are separate routes. Yeongdo-gu’s 14 January 2026 notice described a temporary partial opening of selected lower-path sections from 09:00 to 16:00, with the section behind the tunnel blocked until construction. The district’s 4 September 2026 update discussed slope-safety works and staged reopening, but did not confirm that the full trail is open today. Check the latest district notice and on-site barriers on the day. If a section is closed, stay in the village lanes and use local transport to reach Haneul Observatory or Taejongdae.

Coming from Busan Station? Yeongdo-gu’s tourism page lists buses 508, 85 and 82 for the Huinnyeoul area, but the page was last updated on 19 January 2024. Treat the list as a starting point, not a live timetable; check the current stop, direction and arrival in the official Busan Bus Information System (opens in a new tab) before boarding. If driving, use a designated public parking area and walk in; do not try to enter the narrow residential lanes. Yeongdo-gu visitor information and parking list (opens in a new tab).

Haneul Observatory: a sea-facing pause

Haneul Observatory is included in the official coastal route and offers an open view toward the sea. If the lower path is open, it can be a rest stop along the coastal section; otherwise, reach the observatory separately by local transport or skip it. Check access notices and weather, especially on windy or rainy days.

Finish at Taejongdae

Taejongdae is a coastal park and national geopark known for cliffs and ocean views. The Danubi Train can help visitors reach major viewpoints within the park; operating status, waiting times and ticket information should be checked on the official site before arrival. Busan Geopark lists local bus routes 66, 88 and 101 from Busan Station to Taejongdae, but route details can change—confirm on the day.

A practical one-day schedule

Morning: Kangkangee Arts Village (optional) or go directly to Huinnyeoul. Late morning: walk through the village lanes; use the lower coastal path toward Haneul Observatory only if the current notice and on-site signs confirm it is open. Otherwise, transfer by bus or taxi and treat the observatory as a separate stop. Lunch: choose a currently open restaurant or café in the area; do not assume every small shop opens daily. Afternoon: travel to Taejongdae and use the Danubi Train or walk according to your energy and the park's operating conditions. Leave time to return before the evening transport becomes less frequent.

If you have extra time

The National Maritime Museum is another Yeongdo stop, especially for families or visitors interested in Korea's maritime history. It is not on the coastal walking route, so add it only if opening hours and travel time fit your day.
