The 2026 Busan Fireworks Festival: 34 Fair-Price Stays Selected by the City

Page last updated: Nov 23, 2026

For the 2026 Busan Fireworks Festival, Busan City published a list of 34 accommodation businesses near Gwangalli that met its fair-pricing criteria for festival week. This page keeps that list as a reference for how festival-week pricing works; it is not a current booking recommendation.

2026 festival update · This list covered the 2026 festival period, which has passed. Use it as a reference for fair-price checks, and confirm current rates and availability on the booking page.

Festival date: Saturday, November 7, 2026

Area: Gwangalli Beach, with event viewing areas also around Igidae and Dongbaek Island

City list available: November 4–8, 2026

Why this list matters

Busan City reviewed 157 accommodation businesses within 500 metres of the festival venue. It compared advertised prices on five booking platforms from October 26 to November 3. The average listed price for festival night was 94.5% higher than the comparison Saturdays. The city selected 34 businesses, mainly those whose prices had risen by 20% or less, while also considering whether previously announced rates were honoured and whether hygiene-related complaints or legal violations had been recorded.

Important: “Fair-price” is the city’s screening label, not a promise that rooms are cheap, available, unchanged in price, high quality or facing the fireworks. The list does not confirm a sea view or event view.

Checks worth keeping for a future festival-week booking

  • Confirm the total price for the festival night, including taxes, fees and any minimum-stay condition.
  • Ask whether the room category shown is the one actually available; photos may show a different room type.
  • Check cancellation deadlines, check-in hours and late-arrival procedures before paying.
  • If fireworks views matter, ask the property to confirm the view from the specific room. A Gwangalli address does not guarantee a view.
  • Check the route back after the event. Crowds and road controls can make the final walk or taxi journey slower than usual.

If Gwangalli rooms are scarce or expensive

On busy event nights, compare other bases before booking a distant room at a festival premium. Our Busan accommodation-area guide compares major bases such as Seomyeon and Haeundae. For a coastal stay outside the busiest central areas, see Songjeong and Gijang accommodation.

Official information

Busan City said the 2026 34-business list was published on Visit Busan from November 4 through November 8, under Busan to Visit → Accommodation → Busan Fireworks Festival Fair-Price Accommodations. That listing was time-limited and may no longer be available; check Visit Busan for any list issued for a later festival.
