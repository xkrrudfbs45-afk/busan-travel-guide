Nampo-dong Neighborhood Guide: Markets, Street Food and the Old Downtown

Nampo-dong is Busan’s old downtown, with markets, shopping streets and the harbor close together. Use this page to plan the area, then open the detailed guide for each stop.

Quick summary

  • Best for markets, street snacks, shopping streets and Busan’s old downtown.
  • Nampo and Jagalchi stations are on Metro Line 1, a short ride from Busan Station.
  • The trade-off is distance from the east-coast beaches, so pair it with Yeongdo, Songdo or Gamcheon rather than Haeundae.

Is Nampo-dong the right area for you?

Choose Nampo-dong if markets, street food and older city streets matter more to you than the beach. It is also a practical first stop after arriving at Busan Station.

Many visitors stay in Seomyeon and come here on Line 1. Our where-to-stay guide covers Nampo and the Busan Station area as bases.

Getting there and around

  • From Busan Station (KTX): Metro Line 1 runs south to Nampo and Jagalchi stations. If you have time and light bags, try the Busan Station to Nampo walking route.
  • From Gimhae Airport: a common metro route is the light rail to Sasang, Line 2 to Seomyeon, then Line 1 south. With heavy bags or a late flight, compare a taxi in the late-arrival guide.
  • Getting around: the area is walkable, but side streets are hilly in places. A transit card covers the metro and buses.

What to eat

  • Jagalchi Market: confirm the type, weight, price and cooking fee before you order seafood.
  • Bupyeong Kkangtong Market: fried pork galbi, market snacks and the night market.
  • BIFF Square snacks such as ssiat hotteok and fish cakes.

What “BIFF Square” means: It is a named street, not one large open plaza. Walk along it between Jagalchi Market and Gukje Market, and look for the film-festival handprints set into the pavement. The street itself is open at all times, but food stalls and shops keep their own hours. See Visit Busan’s Nampo–Jagalchi walking route (opens in a new tab) and the Korea Tourism Organization’s BIFF Square guide (opens in a new tab).

  • Nampo Jjukkumi (남포쭈꾸미): a local tip for jjukkumi with a moderate kick; find it on Google Maps (opens in a new tab).
  • 18beon Wandangjip (18번완당집): try Busan-style wandang, delicate dumplings in a light soup, near BIFF Square; find the Nampo shop on Google Maps (opens in a new tab).
  • Kkangtong Golmok Halmae Yubu Jeongol (깡통골목할매유부전골 본점): a market-side tip for tofu-pouch hot pot in Bupyeong Kkangtong Market; check current service before visiting. find it on Google Maps (opens in a new tab).

Jagalchi in the morning, Kkangtong after dark

These two markets work best at different times. Visit Jagalchi Market in the morning if you want to see the seafood market at its liveliest; the official visitor listing gives general hours of 05:00–22:00, but individual stalls and restaurants do not all follow the same schedule. The market’s outdoor trading area is especially atmospheric early in the day.

Bupyeong Kkangtong Market has daytime shops listed around 08:00–20:00, with hours varying by store. Its night market is listed for 19:30–23:30 around the public parking lot and Arcade 2. Arriving before 19:30 does not mean every night-market food stall will already be open.

  1. Morning: explore Jagalchi and check seafood prices before ordering.
  2. Afternoon: walk through BIFF Square and the Nampo shopping streets.
  3. After 19:30: head to Kkangtong for the night-market atmosphere and street food.

Check market closing days: Visit Busan lists Sunday as Gukje Market’s closing day (shops generally 09:00–20:00). Jagalchi Market closes on the 1st, 3rd and 5th Tuesdays (05:00–22:00 on open days). Store hours vary; check the official Gukje Market listing (opens in a new tab) and Jagalchi Market listing (opens in a new tab) before planning around either market.

Before ordering seafood at Jagalchi
  • Ask the seller to confirm the fish or shellfish, weight, unit price and total seafood cost before it is prepared.
  • Ask whether the restaurant charges separately for preparation, table seating, side dishes or a per-person fee. These can vary by shop and order.
  • Confirm the final total with the restaurant before sitting down or placing the order. A displayed fish price alone may not be the full meal cost.

Useful Korean: 총 얼마인가요? (How much is the total?) · 상차림비가 별도인가요? (Is the table setting fee separate?) · 손질비와 조리비가 포함됐나요? (Are preparation and cooking included?)

See and do nearby

  • Kkangtong Market bath and sauna shopping for local everyday goods.
  • Taejongdae on Yeongdo, with the Danubi train, and MOMOS Coffee’s Yeongdo roastery.
  • Songdo Beach and Gamcheon Culture Village to the west.

Gwangbok-ro in December 2026: three Saturday dates

Plan a Nampo-dong evening around Street Festa on December 5, 16:00–22:00 or the Balcony Music Shows on December 12 and 19, 18:00–21:00, around Gwangbok-ro pedestrian street. Busan City's November 24 Daily Busan article lists these times. A separate English event-calendar entry gives the Street Festa end time as 20:00, so check the organiser's latest notice before planning around the final hour.

The winter-light installation period is separate from the live programme. Pair the event with BIFF Square, Gukje Market or Jagalchi, and check individual shop hours before you go.

A simple Nampo half day

  1. Morning: walk from Busan Station through Choryang, or take Line 1 to Nampo.
  2. Lunch: Jagalchi Market, after checking prices.
  3. Afternoon: BIFF Square snacks and the shopping streets.
  4. Evening: Kkangtong night market. Check the day’s opening times locally.

Leave time for hills and crowds, and keep one indoor stop in reserve for rain.

Common questions

How do I get from Busan Station to Nampo-dong?

Take Metro Line 1 south to Nampo or Jagalchi Station. With time and light bags, you can also walk via Choryang; our walking route guide has the details.

Is Nampo-dong a good place to stay?

It suits travelers focused on markets and the old downtown. If your plans are spread across the city, compare Seomyeon; for the beach, compare Haeundae or Gwangalli.
