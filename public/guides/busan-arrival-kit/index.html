South Korea Arrival Checklist for Busan

A smooth arrival starts before you board. This checklist brings together the decisions that matter on arrival day: official entry information, the route from Gimhae Airport, mobile data, local transport, payment and luggage. Confirm details for your own passport, flight time and hotel address.

Arrival-day checklist

  • Check passport validity and the Korean entry rules that apply to your nationality and travel date using official information.
  • Save your hotel's Korean address, phone number and check-in instructions.
  • Use your landing time to check the route from Gimhae Airport and the last available service.
  • Confirm your phone supports eSIM, is carrier-unlocked and has a suitable data plan.
  • Prepare a payment card usable in South Korea and a small amount of Korean won.
  • Check where to buy and top up a transit card.
  • Save hotel, attraction and return-transport booking confirmations.
  • Check luggage-storage hours, size limits and collection deadlines.

Arriving before hotel check-in?

Do not assume your room will be ready. First ask your hotel whether it can hold your bags and when you can collect them. If that is not possible, use the Busan luggage-storage guide to compare a staffed counter or station locker near your next stop. Check the bag size, opening hours, fee and final collection time before leaving your luggage.

If you want a shower or a place to rest as well as luggage storage, Spa Land at Shinsegae Centum City is a paid jjimjilbang option, not a free public luggage room. Shinsegae lists hours of 09:00–22:00, with last admission at 21:00. Large suitcases cannot go into the internal locker room; use the separate suitcase lockers by the ticket counter before entering. Admission and luggage storage are charged separately. Spa Land opens at 09:00, so plan a separate first stop if you reach Centum City earlier. Recheck same-day hours, locker availability and size limits on the official Spa Land page (opens in a new tab).

Large suitcases on city buses: Busan's temporary large-luggage pilot on routes 85 and 307 ran only from 1 April to 30 September 2026. Do not assume that exception is still in force; check the city's official notice (opens in a new tab) and current operator rules before boarding.

  • Mobile data and eSIM

    Before departure, confirm eSIM compatibility, carrier-unlock status and the plan's activation instructions.

  • Transit cards and local transport

    Check where to buy and top up a transit card, and how metro and bus transfers work.

  • Cash, cards and payments

    Bring a card that works in South Korea and a modest amount of Korean won. Payment methods vary by market stall and small shop.

  • Where to stay

    Seomyeon is a flexible base, Haeundae suits coastal plans and Nampo works for markets and older downtown. Choose using the exact hotel address.

  • Luggage storage

    Before leaving bags before check-in or after checkout, confirm opening hours, size limits and collection deadlines.

  • Tourist help: 1330

    The Korea Tourism Organization provides a tourist information and complaint service. Check its official page for current contact options and supported languages.
